## Joint 1: polynomial quotient proof

**Verdict: source inspection passes, subject to the independent machine gate.** The named Lean theorem has hypotheses `[CommRing K] [ExpChar K p]`, arbitrary variable type `σ`, natural `p,e`, and `g : MvPolynomial σ K`. It concludes

```lean
g ^ p ^ e - map (iterateFrobenius K p e) g ∈ fieldEquations σ K (p ^ e)
```

where `fieldEquations` is exactly the ideal generated by `X i ^ q - X i` for all `i : σ`. The proof converts membership to equality in the ideal quotient and uses `MvPolynomial.induction_on`. Its cases are exhaustive for finite-support multivariate polynomials:

1. For `C a`, `C_pow`, `map_C`, and `iterateFrobenius_def` identify the two quotient expressions; no variable equation is used.
2. For `f+g`, `add_pow_expChar_pow` gives the characteristic-power additivity, `map_add` distributes the twist and quotient map, and the two induction hypotheses close the case.
3. For `f * X i`, `mul_pow` and `map_mul` reduce to the induction hypothesis and `X i ^ q = X i` in the quotient. The latter is supplied directly by `Ideal.subset_span` for that generator.

**Worked attacks.** Set `e=0`, so `q=1`: each generator is zero and the asserted identity reduces to `g-g=0`; this boundary does not require a nontrivial quotient. Set `g=C a`, even when `a` lies outside the prime field: the twist is coefficientwise `a ↦ a^(p^e)`, so the constant case holds without incorrectly fixing `a`. Set `g=(a*X i)+(b*X j)` with `i≠j`: additivity separates its terms; each variable factor is reduced by its own field equation. A characteristic-zero freshman-dream counterexample is excluded by `[ExpChar K p]` for the characteristic-power argument, and omitting the field equations would invalidate the variable case. I found no `sorry`, `admit`, locally declared `axiom`, or `unsafe` in the reviewed file. These inspections are not a substitute for building the pinned theorem and auditing its imported axioms.

The Lean theorem is more general than the source's finite-field setting: it asks for a commutative ring of exponential characteristic `p`, not a field `K=F_(q^n)`, and accepts any variable type rather than a fixed finite `M`. It does **not** assert that `p` is prime as a separate explicit parameter or construct a finite field; the source case requires instantiating the typeclass with the intended finite field.

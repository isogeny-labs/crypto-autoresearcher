claim: |
  Let D = Z^B semidirect Z/2, where the Z/2 factor acts by negating the Z^B
  factor, and write an element of D as a pair (a, e) with a in Z^B and e in
  {+1, -1}. Say the pair (e, a) is REPRESENTED BY a word w over the alphabet
  {t_1, ..., t_B, v} when a_i is the signed count of occurrences of t_i in w
  (each occurrence signed by (-1)^(number of v letters to its right)) and
  e = (-1)^(number of occurrences of v in w).

  Define m(e, a) by:
    - m(e, a) = 0 if e = +1 and every coordinate of a is non-negative;
    - m(e, a) = 1 if e = -1;
    - m(e, a) = 2 if e = +1 and some coordinate of a is negative.

  Then for every natural number d, the pair (e, a) is represented by some word
  of length at most d IF AND ONLY IF |a|_1 + m(e, a) <= d, where |a|_1 is the
  sum of the absolute values of the coordinates of a.

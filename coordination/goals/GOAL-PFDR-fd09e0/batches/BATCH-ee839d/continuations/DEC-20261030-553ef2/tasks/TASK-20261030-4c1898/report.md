## Worked attempts to break the interface

1. **Generic unit becomes a nonunit.** In `Q(c)[N,x]`, `(N,c*x)=(N,x)`, so x has a normalized witness with coefficient 1/c. 2. **No denominators, but a bad pivot/minor.** The matrix with rows `(1,1)` and `(1,1+c)` has determinant c. At c=p it has rational rank two and residue rank one. Similarly `c*x^2+x+1` loses degree modulo p. Denominator checks alone cannot enforce rank, degree or trace preservation. 3. **Global freeness does not certify clipping.** For `I=(x+c*y)` and `V=span{x}`, the global quotient is free and the leading x is monic.

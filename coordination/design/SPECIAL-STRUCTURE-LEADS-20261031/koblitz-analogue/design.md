# Design: prime-field Koblitz analogue (CM by Z[(1+sqrt(-7))/2]), orbit rho with canonicalisation charged

Draft protocol: `protocol.json` (unfrozen, `validate.txt` = OK, 0 findings). Toy and public parameters, planted logs only.

## Lead and question
LEAD-STR-small-cm-disc-nonunit-endo (closed_obstructed). Does the sqrt(h) iteration saving of rho on classes {+-alpha^i P}, alpha=(1+sqrt(-7))/2 of order h on the prime-order group G, survive as a total counted-cost saving against matched negation rho once every canonicalisation is charged?

## What the lead already says
The obstruction (IDEA-20260901-6b63a4, 506ba2): h >= log N/log n, and a class of size h costs about h alpha-evaluations to canonicalise, net factor <= sqrt(h)/kappa. `revisit_when` names exactly this object. KR-RHO-482ed3 prices the binary analogue (Frobenius, class gain sqrt(m), canonicalisation still charged); KR-RHO-0528e7/825bca give the automorphism control (0.790*sqrt6); KR-RHO-fb88e6 the cycle handling. LEAD-STR-glv-gls-isogeny-loop's "orbit = whole group" premise is escaped by design here; LEAD-RHO-non-automorphism-equivalence (H-EWALK-29cb90) is the test bed; LEAD-RHO-matched-rho-calibration says toy rho carries fixed set-up cost, so set-up is reported separately. The Koblitz ladder (research/koblitz_rho_ladder_20261013) explicitly did not price canonicalisation.

## Hypotheses
H0: pooled total-cost ratio (best alpha-class arm / negation rho) >= 1. H1: < 1 at some h. **Predicted: H0, a net loss.** Iterations fall by (K/2)^-1/2 (0.23 at h=19), but a step costs ~7 FME (M+S+20I, batch-inverted affine add) and canonicalisation adds (h-1) alpha images at ~9-13 FME each. Break-even needs c(alpha) < c(step)/(sqrt(h)+1), about 1.2 M at h=19, below any degree-2 rational map. Predicted ratio 4-10 at h=19-23, growing roughly as 1.2-2 sqrt(h). Falsifier of the closure: pooled 95% upper bound < 1, or any h<=24 curve with upper bound < 0.9 stable under all sensitivity settings.

## Instances (construction recipe, with corrections found by probe)
For h in 16..110, M_h = 2^h+1-L_h (L_0=2, L_1=1, L_{n+1}=L_n-2L_{n-1}). Take prime l | M_h, 2^18<=l<=2^34, (-7/l)=1, ord_l(lambda)=h exactly for one of the two roots of t^2-t+2 (norm-l element delta=x+y*omega via qfbsolve). Frobenius pi=1+-2*delta*mu, N(mu)=m<=40, p=N(pi) prime. Then #E=4*l*m (not l), E[2] rational, G=[4m]E(F_p). E: y^2=x^3-35x-98 or its twist, whichever has 4lm points. alpha is Velu's 2-isogeny on the rational 2-torsion point whose quotient keeps j=-3375, composed with x->u2*x (u2 a square, so defined over F_p). Of the two such kernels only the one with eigenvalue of order h is used. Class size K=|<lambda,-1>|=h or 2h (computed). One curve per (l, conjugate): 45 curves, 200 planted logs each. Seeds: SHA-256(protocol hash || domain || curve || target [|| arm]), domains setup/target/walk.

## Arms and controls
Reference rho-neg (matched, same code path, orbit [id]+negation). Baseline rho-alpha-affine (naive affine full-point enumeration). Candidates rho-alpha-batched (x-only projective chain, y tracked, one batch inversion per orbit) and rho-alpha-crossmul (min-x by projective cross-multiplication); the cheaper per curve decides. A/A: rho-neg-aa with an independent walk-seed stream. Positive: j=0 matched-N curves (5 sizes, 20-28 bits), classes <+-1,zeta> of size 6, expected ratio 0.50-0.85 against negation. Negative: 45 generic-discriminant curves of matched bit length, alpha code path with orbit [id], expected ratio 1; this also checks CM-7 curves are not cheaper for plain negation rho.

## Counted metrics
Primary: FME = M + S + 20 I from an instrumented field type, covering Q tables, walk starts, steps, look-ahead and escape work, every alpha image, normalisation or cross-multiplication, y updates, DP checks, collision resolution, verification. Add/sub and mod-l ops uncharged but recorded; sensitivity w_I in {8,40}, add 0.2 M, mod-l mul 1 FME. Secondary: walk steps (calibration P1), canonicalisation FME/step (kappa), walk-only cost, verified fraction. No clock is read.

## Decision rule
Preconditions: A/A interval holds 1, null interval holds 1, positive control in [0.50,0.85], P1 within 15% on >=90% of curves; else invalid_measurement. Then pooled geometric-mean ratio (two-level bootstrap, B=9999, bonferroni m=3, min p 2e-4 < 0.0167): lower bound > 1 confirms closure and records kappa and the slope against log h; upper bound < 1 reopens (next_action: advance, cost model wrong); otherwise inconclusive.

## Inadmissible moves
Changing curves, targets, seeds, weights, cap or canonicaliser after a look; leaving canonicalisation, normalisation, y tracking or cycle work uncharged; amortising alpha images across walks without giving the reference the same saving; quoting iteration counts or walk-only cost as speedup; extrapolating to deployed curves.

## Probe (feasibility only; not a result; scripts and outputs in `probe/`)
`gp -q < probe_construction.gp`: for h=16..48 and l>=2^18 with l | M_h exactly (the recipe as written), only h=19 (l=262543) and h=23 (l=4196903) give a prime p; none for the other h in 16..24. `gp -q < probe_cofactor_wide.gp` (h=16..110, l in [2^18,2^34], m<=40): 45 (l, conjugate) pairs, 12-48 prime p each, 1150 curves in all; equality-case h/log2 l = 1 only at h=19, 23, others have h/log2 l in 2..5, so the sweep tests growth with h. `probe_verify.gp`: #E = 4l exactly, group Z/2l x Z/2, three rational 2-torsion points. `probe_alpha.gp`: for h=19 and 23, x(alpha P) = x([root]P) on 10/10 random points for both j-preserving kernels; the matching roots have orders 19 and 43757 (h=19) and 23 and 2098451 (h=23). Recipe errors found: p must be N(1+-2*delta), prime order is impossible (cofactor >= 4 because pi-1 is divisible by alpha and its conjugate), and -1 lies in <lambda> for several even h (class size h, not 2h).

## Cost, implementation, checker
Effort medium: Rust crate `koblitz_analogue_rho` (u64/u128 field with counting wrapper, Montgomery batch inversion, r-adding walk with DP, orbit trait), gp used offline for curve generation. Reuse as reference: research/isogeny_volcano_m51_deep_rho_20261013/rho.c (RNG domains, startup certificate of the canonicaliser against slow enumeration, retry accounting), src/crypto_autoresearcher/index_calculus/{rho,curve,symmetry}.py and research/rho_negation_map_20261020 (negation guards), research/koblitz_rho_ladder_20261013/analyze.py (bootstrap analysis). No CM or Velu construction code exists in the repo beyond gp use; the gp scripts here are the generator. Compute: about 1.5 core-hours, 20-30 min on 4 cores (arithmetic estimate, unmeasured; replace by per-arm timing before freeze).
Independent checker (Python, no shared code): rebuilds each curve from (p,l,m), verifies #E, alpha identity and order, replays a nonce-sampled 5% of rows from recorded seeds and checks the planted log, canonical representatives and the field-operation counts against its own counting mirror; tamper test alters a recorded count, a seed, a class representative and one curve parameter, each must be refused.

## Open risks
Finite-size dilution: at l near 2^20 set-up and walk starts rival the walk (class walks take hundreds of steps), so walk-only cost is reported beside the total. Fruitless cycles are more frequent with larger classes; a shared policy is used but its cost may differ by arm. FME weights are conventions (sensitivity covers them). Only 2 curves have h<=24. Analysis script and binary hash are not yet written, so `frozen_before_data` is false and the preflight runtime is an estimate. The existence of a cheaper canonicaliser beyond the two named is not excluded; any found before freeze enters as an arm.

## LEADS

Ranking is by expected information per unit compute against the best-known exponent 1/2 (log_N units, Pollard rho, KR-RHO-13bf67). Leads 1–4 become the records below. Leads 6–13 are, in my judgment, worthless as attacks on a prime-order secp256k1-shaped curve; they are listed with the one-line reason so nobody re-files them, and each names the repository surface that would close it.

1. **Scheme-degree barrier for algebraic factor bases over F_p** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: any factor base cut out algebraically, F = E ∩ Z, satisfies |F| ≤ deg(E∩Z) ≤ deg(E)·deg(Z); for a Weierstrass predicate g(x)=0 of degree B the predicate block of the arity-m decomposition system has quotient algebra of dimension exactly B^m while the system has only ~2^m B^m/N rational solutions. Reduction: none, it is a lower bound — under H1 (F4 pays ≥ quotient dimension) relation collection costs ≥ N/2^m per relation, exponent 1 vs 1/2, for every β. Extends `IDEA-20260801-021` (planar bound 3d_p) and explains the "small parameters only" clause of KR-IC-5931ee; the Weil-descent escape of KR-IC-73db3f (field equations of degree q) does not exist over F_p. Strongest obstruction to the barrier: a structured quotient — cyclotomic g = x^B − c, the prime-field analogue of KR-IC-49c882's complete splitters — might let F4 run below the dimension. Falsification: F4 sweep at p ∈ [2^12, 2^14], B ∈ {4..32}, m ∈ {2,3} with fitted exponent e ≤ 0.75 for any family. Artifacts: `RESEARCH_PKM_CRITERION.md`, `RESEARCH_GROEBNER_F4.md`, `cryptanalysis/experiments/f5-algebra-control`. Zero-compute theorem plus a day of F4.

2. **Additive energy of x-interval factor bases** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: pull the interval base D_L = {P : x(P) < L} back through the (toy, enumerated) log map to a symmetric set A ⊂ Z/N and compute E(A) = #{a+b=c+d} exactly, the L^4 moment of the Fourier coefficients of 1_A; compare with random symmetric subsets and a progression. This complements the L^∞ statistic C(p) of `IDEA-20260805-c4f675` and the exact mean of `IDEA-20260805-a25f11` with a dispersion measurement of 4-term relation supply. Reduction: none; exponent unchanged. Obstruction: the only proof route, Kohel–Shparlinski-type character sums, gives E(A) − |A|^4/N = O(p|A|log²p), which certifies the random model only for β > 2/3, so measurement is the only route at IC parameters. Falsification: |z| > 3 in two cells with the same sign, reproduced. Artifacts: `crypto/docs/ECDLP_CALIBRATION_PRIMES.md`, `research/ecdlp_autolab`, `IDEA-20260805-a9a95d` (read for whether the moment identity is already stated).

3. **Bit-blasted SAT for prime-field decomposition: matched kill test** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: encode S_3/S_4 decomposition with interval predicates as CNF over bit-blasted F_p arithmetic (interval = forced zero high bits, the only thing SAT gets free) and race CaDiCaL/Kissat against univariate root-finding enumeration on identical targets at p ≈ 2^12, 2^16, 2^20. Reduction: none; exponent unchanged. Obstruction: F_p multiplication is an integer multiplier plus reduction, (log p)² gates, no XOR structure, so the Gaussian-elimination advantage behind KR-IC-5765d2 is absent. Falsification: T_SAT/T_enum ≤ 2 at p ≈ 2^20 for arity 3 with slope below enumeration. Artifacts: `RESEARCH_SAT_SEMAEV.md`, `research/sat_factor_base_review_20260912`, `cryptanalysis/experiments/g2-n31-four-add-sat-gate-20261010` (solver-pipeline control). Closes a lane the repo still spends on, with numbers.

4. **j = 0 Aut-quotient summation polynomials U_m in u = x³** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: on y² = x³ + b with p ≡ 1 (mod 3), U_m := ∏_{a∈(Z/3)^{m−1}} S_m(x_1, ω^{a_2}x_2, …, ω^{a_m}x_m) is a polynomial in the cubes u_i (weighted homogeneity of S_m), of degree 6^{m−2} per variable, vanishing iff some Aut-twists of the points sum to O. A u-interval base has B/3 entries for the same coverage, giving the known linear-algebra constant 9; the decomposition stage has per-relation cost ratio 3^{(m−1)ε} ≥ 1 since the univariates are 3^{m−1} times larger. Reduction: none; exponent unchanged, constant only (KR-IC-955fd6, KR-IC-b0fcda, `IDEA-20260727-001`). Falsification: deg_u U_3 ≠ 6, or a measured u-pipeline faster by ≥ 1.5× per relation. Artifacts: `RESEARCH_SYMMETRIZED_SEMAEV.md`, `RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md`, `IDEA-20260726-002`; register both pipelines as ecbench methods so `ecbench verify --replay-all` replays them (method API in `crypto/docs/ecbench`, not quoted in the brief, must be read).

5. **Riemann–Roch determinant formulation vs Semaev elimination** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: R + P_1 + … + P_m = O iff the m+1 points are coplanar in the embedding by L((m+1)O), i.e. a multilinear determinant in (1, x, y, x², xy, …) vanishes; y stays a variable with the curve equations instead of being eliminated into degree-2^{m−1} polynomials. Over F_p both routes give the same enumeration rate p/(2B) per relation (exponent 1), so this is worthless for the target; the only live question is extension-field solving degree, testable on the n = 31 binary infrastructure (`cryptanalysis/experiments/g2-n31-degree-filtration-20261010`) if `research/semaev_alternatives_audit_20261104` has not already compared a determinant/Diem-style system — read that directory first. Nearest KR-IC-8b9daa, KR-IC-5a43e5. Falsification: first-fall degree of the determinant system ≥ Semaev's at matched parameters.

6. **Elliptic-code / ISD reading of decomposition** `[known]` — Mechanism: decomposition over a base of size n is finding a weight-(m+1) word in the dual of the evaluation code of L((m+1)O) at the base (Cheng's AG-code hardness correspondence). Over F_q with q ≈ 2^256 every representation/birthday ISD gain needs coefficient enumeration over F_q and collapses to Prange — the same collapse as KR-RHO-5b1c0a; the amortized rate p/n per relation is representation-free (hash the hyperplane through R and m−1 base points). Exponent 1, needs < 1/2. Worthless beyond unifying language; the afternoon check is the ISD code behind `RESEARCH_TII_MCELIECE.md` / `research/tii_mceliece` confirming p/(2B) ± 10% at p ≈ 2^24.

7. **Digit-box factor base from the special prime** `[speculative]` — Mechanism: p = 2^256 − 2^32 − 977 invites F = {x = Σ c_i 2^{32i}, c_i < 2^16}, whose indicator is a product of eight Dirichlet kernels (Fourier-concentrated, cf. `IDEA-20260727-006`). Obstruction: the group law is a rational map blind to the Z-digit structure of F_p, unlike F_q-linearity in Weil descent. Prediction: yield equals an interval base of equal size within noise; exponent 1. Probably worthless; test via `cryptanalysis/experiments/factor-base-yield-v2` on special-form calibration primes; the `cado-*` NFS infrastructure does not transfer because E(F_p) has no sieve-able representation.

8. **Hecke/volcano position for j = 0** `[known]` — secp256k1 has End = Z[ω], h(−3) = 1: every ℓ-crater is a single vertex, descending isogenies reach conductor-ℓ^k curves of identical order, and KR-RHO-755e35 plus `IDEA-20260731-017` / `IDEA-20260805-38aebb` already cover decomposition statistics across the class. Reduction: none. Worthless for the target except as a control; `cryptanalysis/experiments/ecdlp-hecke-volcano-position-20261011`, `ecdlp-hecke-d39-holdout-20261026` and `RESEARCH_VOLCANO_FLOOR_RHO.md` must be read for whatever signal they report, and any residual must pass the null-object control of `IDEA-20260731-011`.

9. **Torsion-lift p-adic coordinates** `[known]` — Since p ∤ N, Ext¹(Z/N, Z_p) = 0 and Ẽ(Z_p) ≅ Z_p ⊕ Z/N for every lift Ẽ: each P has a unique torsion lift P̂ by Hensel iteration. Smart's attack needs [N] to act as p on E_1; here N is a p-adic unit, λ([N]P̃) is lift-dependent, and the coordinates of P̂ are algebraic numbers of degree ≈ N. Exponent unchanged; worthless (KR-RHO-53c89f, KR-IC-cd159a). Only value: a sanity regression of (x̂ − x)/p mod p on k at toy p alongside the trace-1 control of `IDEA-20260805-cc4c2c`, using whatever lifting code sits behind `RESEARCH_EDS_RESIDUE.md`.

10. **CM-module / class-group port** `[known]` — E(F_p) ≅ O_K/(π−1) as an O_K-module with class number 1: no ideal classes to serve as a factor base, no norm smoothness on points; the structure is the scalar λ ≡ ω, i.e. GLV, whose only known yield is the √6 constant of KR-RHO-0528e7 (a rho variant, out of scope here). Worthless; `RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md` and `secp256k1_cm_audit` are the closure; `RESEARCH_GLV_HNP.md` concerns ECDSA nonce leakage and is out of scope.

11. **Transfer: covers, extension embedding, MOV, twist** `[known]` — Covers C → E over F_p land in J(C)(F_p) of order p^g where index calculus costs p^{2−2/g} ≥ p^{4/3} (g ≥ 3) or p (g = 2), both above p^{1/2}; embedding E(F_p) into E(F_{p^n}) makes it a direct factor so the subfield base {x ∈ F_p} yields no cross-relations; MOV needs small embedding degree, anomalous needs t = 1. Worthless (KR-IC-5ea2f8, KR-IC-46d822, KR-IC-a4dd54, KR-RHO-53c89f); a one-page checklist in `docs/ECDLP_ATTACK_MATRIX.md` closes it; `research/ghs-c2pnb` and `RESEARCH_DIEM_DESCENT.md` are binary/extension artifacts that do not transfer.

12. **Learned predictor of k-bits from x(kP)** `[speculative]` — Train on (x(P), x(kP)) → bit of k. Exponential sums make every polynomial-size statistic of x(kP) over k-intervals flat to p^{−1/2}·polylog, so toy advantage is memorization. Worthless; read `elliptic-curve-complexity-ml-4b4228` and `research/aes_neural_distinguisher` for what was tried; kill test: held-out k-ranges, advantage > 3σ over 1/2 at p ≥ 2^24 with model size < p^{1/4}.

13. **Half-arity claw / k-SUM decomposition** `[known]` — Collide {x(P_1+P_2)} with {x(R+P_3+P_4)}: n² time and memory per target, total ≈ p^{3/4}; conditional k-SUM bounds forbid birthday rearrangements below n^{⌈k/2⌉}. Already `IDEA-20260731-007`; nearest KR-RHO-5b1c0a. Exponent 3/4 vs 1/2. Worthless; listed to prevent re-filing.

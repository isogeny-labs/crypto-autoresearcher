## LEADS

Conventions throughout: $E:\ -x^2+y^2 = 1 + d\,x^2y^2$ over $\mathbb F_p$, $p=2^{255}-19$, $a=-1$, $d=-121665/121666$, $k := 2d = -121665/60833 \bmod p$ (precomputed once). Extended point $(X{:}Y{:}Z{:}T)$, $Z\neq0$, $x=X/Z$, $y=Y/Z$, $T=XY/Z$, i.e. invariant $ZT=XY$; curve equation in these coordinates $-X^2+Y^2 = Z^2 + dT^2$. $\mathcal E$ = projective $(X{:}Y{:}Z)$, $\mathcal E^e$ = extended. $i := \sqrt{-1}$ (exists since $p\equiv1 \bmod 4$). Rational torsion: $E[8](\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathbb Z/8$; $E[2](\mathbb F_p)=\{(0,1),(0,-1)\}$, $E[4](\mathbb F_p)=E[2]\cup\{(\pm i,0)\}$. None of the ledger records quoted in the BRIEF files any explicit Edwards25519 addition/doubling law (they are model-transport / ECDLP records), so `[known]` below means "known in HCD 2008 / BBJLP 2008 / EFD", cited with `recalled` provenance.

**1. Unified extended addition $\mathcal E^e\times\mathcal E^e\to\mathcal E^e$, $a=-1$ (HCD §3.1, EFD add-2008-hwcd-3)** `[known]`
$A=(Y_1-X_1)(Y_2-X_2)$, $B=(Y_1+X_1)(Y_2+X_2)$, $C=T_1\cdot(kT_2)$, $D=2Z_1Z_2$, $E=B-A$, $F=D-C$, $G=D+C$, $H=B+A$; $X_3=EF$, $Y_3=GH$, $T_3=EH$, $Z_3=FG$.
Check: $E=2(X_1Y_2+Y_1X_2)$, $H=2(Y_1Y_2+X_1X_2)$, $F=2(Z_1Z_2-dT_1T_2)$, $G=2(Z_1Z_2+dT_1T_2)$, so $X_3/Z_3=E/G$, $Y_3/Z_3=H/F$ are the affine law and $Z_3T_3=EFGH=X_3Y_3$.
HCD count: 8M + 1D. Verified recount: **8M + 1c + 9a** (4 for $Y_i\pm X_i$, 1 for the doubling of $Z_1Z_2$, 4 for $E,F,G,H$); 0S. Complete (lead 2). Identity: $(0{:}1{:}1{:}0)+P_2 = (4X_2Z_2{:}4Y_2Z_2{:}4Z_2^2{:}4X_2Y_2)=P_2$ ✓; $(0{:}{-1}{:}1{:}0)+P_2=(-X_2{:}-Y_2{:}Z_2{:}T_2)$ ✓; $P+(-P)=(0{:}GH{:}FG{:}0)$ with $H=F=2(Y_1^2-X_1^2)\neq0$, i.e. the identity with nonzero $Z_3$ ✓. Symmetry: the change of basis $(X,Y)\mapsto(Y-X,Y+X)$ diagonalises the two bilinear forms $Y_1Y_2+X_1X_2$ and $X_1Y_2+Y_1X_2$ that $a=-1$ makes appear with equal weight — this is what buys 8M instead of 9M. Repo: `EdPoint::add` currently costs **11M + 1I per call** (`let d = ed25519_d();` runs `fe_inv` every call; `c` is three `fe_mul`, `dd` is two). Replace those three lines by `c = fe_mul(&self.t, &fe_mul(&K2D, &other.t))` with a cached `K2D`, and `dd = fe_add(&zz,&zz)` with `zz = fe_mul(&self.z,&other.z)`. Branch-free.

**2. Completeness theorem specialised to Edwards25519** `[known]` (Bernstein–Lange 2007 Thm 3.3 / BBJLP 2008 Thm 3.3)
Hypotheses: $a=\alpha^2$ with $\alpha=i\in\mathbb F_p$; $d$ non-square ($d^{(p-1)/2}=-1$, asserted by test); inputs affine-representable ($Z_i\neq0$) with $Z_iT_i=X_iY_i$. Claim: $F\neq0$ and $G\neq0$ in lead 1 for *all* rational pairs. Proof: $F=0$ or $G=0$ means $\epsilon:=dx_1x_2y_1y_2\in\{\pm1\}$, forcing $x_1x_2y_1y_2\neq0$. Multiply $P_2$'s equation by $dx_1^2y_1^2$: $dx_1^2y_1^2(ax_2^2+y_2^2)=dx_1^2y_1^2+\epsilon^2=ax_1^2+y_1^2$. Hence $(\alpha x_1+\epsilon y_1)^2=ax_1^2+y_1^2+2\epsilon\alpha x_1y_1=dx_1^2y_1^2(ax_2^2+y_2^2)+2dx_1^2y_1^2\alpha x_2y_2=dx_1^2y_1^2(\alpha x_2+y_2)^2$. If $\alpha x_2+y_2\neq0$ then $d=\big((\alpha x_1+\epsilon y_1)/(x_1y_1(\alpha x_2+y_2))\big)^2$ is a square — contradiction. Otherwise repeat with $-\alpha$: $-\alpha x_2+y_2=-2\alpha x_2\neq0$, same contradiction. ∎ Consequence: no exceptional pair exists over $\mathbb F_p$; the four points at infinity of the $\mathbb P^3$ model $\{XY=ZT,\,-X^2+Y^2=Z^2+dT^2\}$ are $(0{:}\pm\sqrt d{:}0{:}1)$ and $(\pm\sqrt{-d}{:}0{:}0{:}1)$, non-rational exactly because $d$ and $-d$ are non-squares. Repo surface: a test `ed25519_complete_on_torsion` over all 64 pairs of $E[8](\mathbb F_p)$ plus $\{B,2B,\dots\}$ asserting $Z_3\neq0$, $Z_3T_3=X_3Y_3$, on-curve.

**3. Dedicated doubling $\mathcal E\to\mathcal E^e$, sign-normalised (HCD §3.3 variant)** `[adaptation]`
$A=X_1^2$, $B=Y_1^2$, $C=Z_1^2+Z_1^2$, $E=(X_1+Y_1)^2-A-B$, $G=B-A$, $F'=C-G$, $H'=A+B$; $X_3=EF'$, $Y_3=GH'$, $T_3=EH'$, $Z_3=F'G$.
Derivation: with $P_1=P_2$ the unified law gives $E=4XY$, $H=2(X^2+Y^2)$, $F=2(Z^2-dT^2)$, $G=2(Z^2+dT^2)$; the curve equation $Y^2-X^2=Z^2+dT^2$ substitutes $G$ and $F$ without touching $T$, so $T_1$ is *not read*. HCD writes $F=G-C$, $H=-(A+B)$; here $F'=-F$, $H'=-H$ negate all four outputs simultaneously, the same projective point, and the negation disappears. **4M + 4S + 7a**, 0c. Complete: $G=Z^2+dT^2$ and $F'=Z^2-dT^2$ vanish only if $\mp d$ is a square, impossible (lead 2). Traces: $2(0{:}1{:}1{:}0)=(0{:}1{:}1{:}0)$; $2(0{:}{-1}{:}1{:}0)=(0{:}1{:}1{:}0)$; $2(i{:}0{:}1{:}0)=(0{:}{-1}{:}1{:}0)$ ✓. Repo `EdPoint::double` is **5M + 4S + 7a** (`fe_mul(&BigUint::from(2u32), &fe_sq(&self.z))` is a full multiply) — replace by `fe_add(&zz,&zz)`; replace `h = fe_sub(&0,&fe_add(a,b))` and `f = fe_sub(&g,&c)` by `h = fe_add(a,b)`, `f = fe_sub(&c,&g)`.

**4. Mixed addition, $Z_2=1$ (EFD madd-2008-hwcd-3)** `[known]`

**5. Projective-Niels readdition cache** `[adaptation]` — record 1.
Cache $N_2=(Y_2-X_2,\ Y_2+X_2,\ kT_2,\ 2Z_2)$ once (1c + 3a); then $A=(Y_1-X_1)n_1$, $B=(Y_1+X_1)n_2$, $C=T_1n_3$, $D=Z_1n_4$, then $E,F,G,H$ and outputs as in lead 1: **8M + 6a, 0c, 0I**. **6. Dual addition law, $a=-1$ (HCD §3.2, EFD add-2008-hwcd-4), with exact exception set** `[adaptation]` — record 2.
$A=(Y_1-X_1)(Y_2+X_2)$, $B=(Y_1+X_1)(Y_2-X_2)$, $C=2Z_1T_2$, $D=2T_1Z_2$, $E=D+C$, $F=B-A$, $G=B+A$, $H=D-C$; $X_3=EF$, $Y_3=GH$, $T_3=EH$, $Z_3=FG$. Affine content: $x_3=\dfrac{x_1y_1+x_2y_2}{y_1y_2-x_1x_2}$, $y_3=\dfrac{x_1y_1-x_2y_2}{x_1y_2-y_1x_2}$ (verified by cross-multiplication against the unified law using both curve equations). **8M + 0c + 10a** (8a with a cache $(Y_2+X_2,\,Y_2-X_2,\,2T_2,\,2Z_2)$). Not complete: $Z_3=0\iff X_1Y_2=Y_1X_2$ or $Y_1Y_2=X_1X_2$; the first is $x(P_1-P_2)=0$, the second $y(P_1-P_2)=0$, hence **$Z_3=0\iff P_1-P_2\in E[4](\mathbb F_p)$** (4 points). In a prime-subgroup scalar multiplication this is exactly $P_1=P_2$, which can occur in the last iterations for specific scalars, so it is not constant-time-safe as the only law. Use: second law of a complete system over $\bar{\mathbb F}_p$, and a cheap **test oracle** (must agree with lead 1 off $E[4]$). Saves 1c versus lead 1 but 0 versus lead 5.

**7. Rigidity of exceptional loci: the dual law cannot be completed by representation changes** `[adaptation]`
The conditions $X_1Y_2=Y_1X_2$, $Y_1Y_2=X_1X_2$ are bihomogeneous, so invariant under $(X_i{:}Y_i{:}Z_i{:}T_i)\mapsto(\lambda_iX_i{:}\lambda_iY_i{:}\lambda_iZ_i{:}\lambda_iT_i)$ and under any cached rewriting of the same polynomials; and the exceptional set contains $\mathbb F_p$-rational pairs ($P_1=P_2$). Therefore no choice of representatives, caches or constant folding yields a complete law of this shape on Edwards25519; completeness is a property of the polynomial map, decided by whether its exceptional divisor $\{P_1-P_2\in S\}$ has $S(\mathbb F_p)\neq\emptyset$. For lead 1, $S$ = the four points at infinity, $S(\mathbb F_p)=\emptyset$ (lead 2). Repo surface: documentation + the lead-13 control (same checker over a small prime with square $d$, where failures must appear).

**8. T-dropping mixed system $\mathcal E\leftrightarrow\mathcal E^e$ (HCD §4) as a uniform double-then-add loop** `[adaptation]` — record 3.
Per scalar bit: doubling (lead 3) $\mathcal E\to\mathcal E^e$ **4M+4S**, then affine-Niels addition with $T_3$ omitted $\mathcal E^e\times\text{Niels}\to\mathcal E$ **6M**, then branch-free `cselect(bit, sum, doubled)` on $(X,Y,Z)$ only. Doubling never reads $T_1$, addition always produces the operand of the next doubling, so omitting $T_3$ is unconditional, not bit-dependent. **10M + 4S + 7a per bit, 0c, 0I**, versus repo `scalar_mul` 16M+4S+1I per 1-bit and 5M+4S per 0-bit, variable-time. 254 iterations over bits $253..0$ of a clamped scalar (bit 254 is always set: initialise acc $=P$).

**9. The M↔S trade in doubling** `[known]`
$E=(X_1+Y_1)^2-A-B$ (1S + 2a, HCD) versus $E=2X_1Y_1$ (1M + 1a): **4M+4S+7a** versus **5M+3S+6a**. The squaring form wins iff $\mathrm{cost}(S)+\mathrm{cost}(a)<\mathrm{cost}(M)$: always on limb-array fields ($S\approx0.8M$); never if `fe_sq(x)` is `fe_mul(x,x)` (then it loses one addition). The BRIEF's `x25519.rs` uses `fe_mul(&a,&a)` for all four ladder squarings, suggesting the repo has no S/M distinction — record 4 measures the ratio and picks. Both forms branch-free and complete.

**10. Unified doubling by the complete law with squarings (one code shape for add and double)** `[adaptation]`
$A=(Y_1-X_1)^2$, $B=(Y_1+X_1)^2$, $C=k\,T_1^2$, $D=2Z_1^2$, $E=B-A$, $F=D-C$, $G=D+C$, $H=B+A$, outputs as lead 1: **4M+4S+1c+7a**, reads $T_1$, complete by lead 2. Costs 1c more than lead 3 but every group operation in a scalar multiplication then has the identical $(A,B,C,D,E,F,G,H)$ dataflow, useful for an op-sequence-uniform `scalar_mul` and as a second doubling oracle. Repo: optional `EdPoint::double_unified`.

**11. Free negation and signed-digit windows on the Niels cache** `[known]`
$-P=(-X{:}Y{:}Z{:}-T)$; on caches $N(-P)=(n_2,n_1,-n_3,n_4)$. A width-$w$ signed window needs $\{P,3P,\dots,(2^{w-1}-1)P\}$ only, half the table, with constant-time conditional negation = masked swap of $n_1,n_2$ + masked negation of $n_3$. Uses the involution $x\mapsto-x$. Repo: fixed-base precomputation for `scalar_mul(B, ·)`; variable-time today.

**12. Montgomery $u$ from extended coordinates as a cross-implementation oracle** `[adaptation]`
$u=(1+y)/(1-y)$ gives $(U{:}W)=(Z+Y{:}Z-Y)$: 2a, 0M, one $I$ for affine. Exceptional loci: $W=0\iff P=\mathcal O$ ($u=\infty$, ladder returns $Z_2=0$, encoded 0); $U=0\iff P=(0,-1)$ ($u=0$). Base point consistency: $y_B=4/5\Rightarrow u=(9/5)/(1/5)=9$. Test: for random seeds, `x25519_base(s) == fe_to_bytes(u(sB))` with $sB$ from the Edwards loop, and for small-order inputs both sides give the zero string. Side note on the same file: `ladder` does its four squarings as `fe_mul(&a,&a)`; with a real `fe_sq` the step is 5M+4S+1c, 1c by 121665 (shift-add), not 9M+1c.

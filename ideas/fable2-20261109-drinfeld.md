# Mythos ECDLP ideation — drinfeld (20261109)

## LEADS

1. **The theorem that fails, stated exactly.** Scanlon (2001, recalled): for a Drinfeld $A$-module $\varphi:A=\mathbb F_q[T]\to L\{\tau\}$ over a finite field $L=\mathbb F_{q^n}$, the map $a\mapsto\varphi_a(x)$ is $\mathbb F_q$-linear in $a$ and $\varphi_a$ is additive in $x$, so "given $x,\ y=\varphi_a(x)$, find $a$ mod $\mathrm{ann}(x)$" is an $n\times(\deg a+1)$ linear system over $\mathbb F_q$: polynomial time, for every rank $r$. Two hypotheses carry the proof: (H1) the scalar ring is an algebra over a finite constant field $\mathbb F_q$; (H2) the module scheme is $\mathbb G_a$, so $\mathrm{End}=L\{\tau\}$ consists of additive polynomials. For $E/\mathbb F_p$ with $\mathbb Z$ (or $\mathrm{End}(E)=\mathbb Z[\omega]$) acting, (H1) fails because a ring of characteristic 0 has no finite subring, and $\mathbb Z/N$, though a field, acts on a 1-dimensional $\mathbb F_N$-space whose only computable $\mathbb F_N$-coordinate is the log itself; (H2) fails because $\mathrm{Hom}(E,\mathbb G_a)=\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb G_a,E)=0$ over $\mathbb F_p$. The sharpest quantitative consequence: $(\tau-1)^p=\tau^p-1$ in $L\{\tau\}$, so $\ker(\tau-1)^k\subset\mathbb F_{p^{p^{\lceil\log_p k\rceil}}}$, whereas $(\pi-1)^N\ne\pi^N-1$ in $\mathrm{End}(E)$ and $\ker(\pi-1)^2=(\pi-1)^{-1}E(\mathbb F_p)$ is rational exactly over $\mathbb F_{p^N}$ (torsion-wave record "Lang torsor defined over $\mathbb F_{p^N}$"). Reduction: none. Exponent: n/a. Obstruction: it is a theorem. Test: none; cite the drinfeld-wave lead "Scanlon's $\mathbb F_q$-linearity, Spec $\mathbb Z$ has no constant field" and `RESEARCH_KOBLITZ_INDEX_CALCULUS.md` for the $\mathbb Z[\tau]$ case that also lacks a constant field. `[known]`

2. **Survivor 1 — Drinfeld torsion is $p$-primary, so the only elliptic twin is $N=p$.** In Drinfeld land $\varphi[a]\cong(A/a)^r$ is a $p$-group inside a characteristic-$p$ ambient; the elliptic configuration with that property is exactly the anomalous curve, where Semaev/Satoh–Araki/Smart give polynomial time (KR-RHO-53c89f), and over extensions the $p$-Sylow $E(\mathbb F_{p^{k_p}})[p]$ (drinfeld-wave "Drinfeld distance" record). Reduction: ECDLP in $\langle P\rangle$ of order $N\ne p$ to nothing: the $N$-part and $p$-part are CRT-independent (torsion-wave CRT record). Exponent: $1/2$ unchanged. Obstruction: $\gcd(N,p)=1$. Test: the p-adic-wave trace-one record already has anomalous and same-prime non-anomalous arms. `[known]`

3. **Survivor 2 — the complete list of $\mathbb F_p$-linear carriers of $E/\mathbb F_p$ and the vanishing theorem.** $\mathrm{Hom}_{\mathrm{grp}}(E(\mathbb F_q),(\mathbb F_q,+))\neq0$ iff $\mathrm{char}\,\mathbb F_q\mid\#E(\mathbb F_q)$; the trace equation of KR-IC-f5c584 (ordinary binary curves, $2\mid\#E$) is the positive instance. The computable additive carriers of a prime-order $E/\mathbb F_p$ are $\mathrm{Lie}(E)$ via $E(\mathbb F_p[\varepsilon])=E(\mathbb F_p)\times\mathbb F_p$ (split by the constant lift), $\hat E(\mathbb Z_p)$, $E[p]^{\mathrm{\acute et}}$ over $\mathbb F_{p^{k_p}}$, $H^1(E,\mathcal O)$ (the $E^\natural$ record) and $H^0(E,\Omega^1)$ (Semaev's $\omega_P=df_P/f_P$, the Cartier lead); all are killed by $p$, so $\langle P\rangle$ maps to zero. → Record 4. Test: a toy script enumerating the carriers, with the anomalous arm as positive control. `[adaptation]`

4. **Rank-2 Drinfeld module as an elliptic-shaped positive control.** $\varphi_T=\theta+g\tau+\Delta\tau^2$ over $\mathbb F_{q^n}$ has torsion $(A/a)^2$, a determinant (Weil-type) pairing, an ordinary/supersingular dichotomy and Gekeler's Frobenius polynomial, yet its DLP costs $O(n^\omega)$ by lead 1. The filed Carlitz control is rank 1; a rank-2 arm checks that the ladder-slope estimator (IDEA-20260805-4d31bc, IDEA-20260805-bb4488) and the descent gate (IDEA-20260801-003) return exponent 0 on an object whose *shape* is elliptic. Degeneration: over $L=\mathbb F_q$ itself, $\tau=\mathrm{id}$ and $\varphi_a(x)=a(\theta+g+\Delta)\,x$, one division — the mirror of $E(\mathbb F_p)=\ker(\pi-1)\cong\mathcal O_K/(\pi-1)$ with the division replaced by the ECDLP. → Record 1. `[adaptation]`

5. **Degenerate module over its own characteristic field.** Any Drinfeld module over $L=\mathbb F_q$ has $\varphi(L)\cong A/(T-\theta')$, $\theta'=\varphi_T(1)$, a scalar module. The port says: $E(\mathbb F_p)\cong\mathcal O_K/(\pi-1)\cong\mathbb F_N$ (Lenstra, port-wave record) is the same shape, and "divide in $\mathbb F_N$" *is* the discrete log. Exponent $1/2$. Obstruction: lead 1 (H1). Test: none. `[known]`

6. **Carlitz torsion = Artin–Schreier–Witt filtration = polynomial-basis factor base.** For $\varphi_T=\tau-1$ over $\mathbb F_p$ and $n=p^m$, $V_k=\ker(\tau-1)^k\subset\mathbb F_{p^{p^m}}$ has $\dim V_k=k$ and the Leibniz identity $\delta(xy)=x\delta y+y\delta x+\delta x\,\delta y$ ($\delta=\tau-1$) gives $V_jV_k\subset V_{j+k-1}$, hence $\dim\langle V_k^2\rangle\le 2k-1$ — exactly the FPPR bound of KR-IC-73db3f, attained by $\mathrm{span}\{1,z,\dots,z^{k-1}\}$ for an Artin–Schreier generator $z$, with strict drops at subfields ($p=2$: $V_2=\mathbb F_4$). So the function-field object re-derives the known best $V$ and nothing better. → Record 3 (constant-$\theta$ arm). `[adaptation]`

7. **Drinfeld-submodule factor bases on prime-degree binary fields (ecc2k130 lane).** For $\varphi_T=\theta+\tau$, $\theta\in\mathbb F_{2^n}\setminus\mathbb F_2$, $L$ is a cyclic $\mathbb F_2[T]$-module $A/(a_\theta)$ and every divisor $b\mid a_\theta$ gives a structured subspace $V_b=\ker\varphi_b$ of dimension $\deg b$ stable under $x\mapsto\theta x+x^2$. On $\mathbb F_{2^{131}}$ ($n$ prime, $\tau^{131}-1=(\tau-1)\Phi_{131}(\tau)$) the constant-$\theta$ filtration is trivial, so these are the only Drinfeld-shaped candidates. Prediction: $\dim\langle V_b^2\rangle=\min(n,d(d+1)/2)$ generically, no gain over KR-IC-73db3f. Exponent: constant factors at most. Test: exhaustive over $\theta$ at $n\in\{7,11,13\}$. Feeds `cryptanalysis/experiments/g2-n31-*` and `RESEARCH_KOBLITZ_INDEX_CALCULUS.md`. → Record 3. `[adaptation]`

8. **Lucas sparsity of Carlitz torsion polynomials as quasi-subfield candidates.** $(\tau-1)^k=\prod_i(\tau^{p^i}-1)^{k_i}$ has $\prod(k_i+1)$ terms and second exponent $p^{k-p^v}$ ($v$ = lowest nonzero digit of $k$), so $V_k$ is a linearised complete splitter $X^{p^{n'}}-\lambda(X)$ with $n'=k$, $n=p^m$, $\beta=1-p^v/k$; for $k=cp^{m-1}$: $(\beta,n'/n)=(1-1/c,\,c/p)$, $c=1$ the subfield. The pair $(1/2,\,2/p)$ for odd $p$ must be reconciled with KR-IC-49c882's "$\beta\ge3/4$" (hypothesis on $n'/n$, on $p=2$, or on $\lambda$). Exponent for prime-field ECDLP: void ($n=1$). → Record 2. `[adaptation]`

9. **Drinfeld isogenies are additive polynomials; the Hecke lane's mirror is trivial.** Joux–Narayanan (2019, recalled): the rank-2 Drinfeld isogeny problem is polynomial by $\tau$-linear algebra, and an isogeny maps a subspace factor base to a subspace, so isogeny-coupled index calculus "works" there for the same linearity reason the DLP is easy. On $E/\mathbb F_p$ an $\ell$-isogeny is a degree-$\ell$ rational map and the moduli-wave null (overlap $B^2/N$) applies; KR-RHO-755e35. Test: none beyond the filed `cryptanalysis/experiments/ecdlp-hecke-*` audits. `[known]`

10. **Carlitz–Hayes reciprocity is Cheon's torsor with hardness swapped.** Frobenius acts on $\Lambda_P$ as $C_{T-\theta}$, so an auxiliary $C_{Q^d}(x)$ reveals $Q^d\bmod P$ and recovering $Q$ is a DLP in $(A/P)^*=\mathbb F_{q^{\deg P}}^*$: module easy, torsor hard. For $E$: module hard, torsor $(\mathbb Z/N)^*$ at $L(1/3)$ (port-wave Pohlig–Hellman-on-the-torsor record). Exponent: unchanged. `[known]`

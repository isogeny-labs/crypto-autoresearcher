## LEADS

1. **Flattening-rank floor for the target-specialised Semaev tensor** `[adaptation]` — extends the wave-1 wildcards lead "Tensor rank of the x_R-specialised Semaev tensor; rank 2 ⟹ multiplicative k-sum". Mechanism: $T(x_R)\in\mathrm{Sym}^m(\mathbb F_p^{d+1})$, $d=2^{m-1}$, is the coefficient tensor of $S_{m+1}(x_1..x_m;x_R)$ on the basis $(1,x,\dots,x^d)$; tensor rank, symmetric rank and border rank are all $\ge$ the $(d+1)\times(d+1)^{m-1}$ flattening rank. If that rank is full, no identity $S_{m+1}=\sum_{t\le r}\prod_i a_{t,i}(x_i)$ with $r\le d$ exists over any extension, so the rank-2 multiplicative k-sum and the border-rank-2 (W-state) additive k-sum reductions are dead for all but a bounded exceptional target set (cut out by $(d+1)$-minors of degree $\le(d+1)d$ in $x_R$). Reduction closed: PDP → k-sum over images of rational functions. Exponent: delta 0 vs 1/2; the lane is closed, not improved. Obstruction: the floor leaves rank between $d+1$ and the generic rank undetermined. Falsification: flattening rank $<d+1$ for $\ge1\%$ of $x_R$ at a toy prime, or an explicit 4-term identity for $S_4$. Record A.

2. **The ternary cubic attached to $S_3$ has $j=0$ for every curve; Waring rank 3; its symmetry is Möbius only at $j(E)=0$** `[unverified]` — a symmetric $(2,2,2)$ form is an element of $\mathrm{Sym}^3(F^3)$, i.e. a plane cubic $F_E(u)=3u_0u_2^2-6u_1^2u_2-6au_0u_1^2-12bu_0^2u_1+a^2u_0^3$. Hand computation: as a quadratic in $u_2$ its discriminant quartic $t^4+2at^2+4bt-a^2/3$ has invariants $I=0$, $J=-16(4a^3+27b^2)$, so $F_E$ is smooth with $j(F_E)=0$ for *every* nonsingular $E$, hence Fermat-equivalent over $\bar{\mathbb F}_p$ and of Waring rank 3 (the 3×3 flattening minimum), giving $S_3=\sum_{t=0}^2 q_t(x_1)q_t(x_2)q_t(x_3)$ with quadratics $q_t$. Geometrically: $P_1+P_2+P_3=O$ iff the Veronese images $(1,x_i,x_i^2)$ are apolar to a $j=0$ cubic. This is a different symmetry from the torsion symmetrisation in `RESEARCH_SYMMETRIZED_SEMAEV.md` (KR-IC-955fd6); predicted to lie in $\mathrm{Sym}^2(\mathrm{PGL}_2)$ (a Möbius map of the x-line) only when $a=0$, where it is the GLV map $x\mapsto\zeta x$. Exponent: delta 0. Obstruction: rank 3 of $S_3$ is not inherited by $S_{m+1}$ (lead 1 tests exactly this). Falsification: Sage finds $I\ne0$ or a rank-4 $F_E$ for a nonsingular $E$. Record D.

3. **No variety-based presentation-invariant floor exists for the F4 wall; the wall is Nullstellensatz degree of the chosen generators** `[adaptation]` — extends IDEA-20260731-014 and the relations-wave record "Three presentations of one zero-dimensional membership system". Mechanism: for a non-decomposing target the membership ideal $\langle S_{m+1}(x;x_R),x_i^B-1\rangle$ is $(1)$, so its projective closure is empty; for a decomposing target it is the ideal of $\le$ a handful of $\mathbb F_p$-points with Castelnuovo–Mumford regularity $\lesssim\#$solutions $\ll D_{\rm wall}$. Hence regularity, Hilbert function of the variety and degree of the ideal (KR-IC-fbdb61 distinguishes these from solving degree) cannot bound the F4 wall from below; the only invariant floor is the minimum of the effective-Nullstellensatz degree over generating sets of bounded degree. Exponent: delta 0; this is a scoping statement. Obstruction: the min over generating sets is not computable in general. Test: at $m=3$, $B\in\{8,16\}$, compute $\mathrm{reg}$ of the point ideal on SAT targets and the wall logged by `RESEARCH_GROEBNER_F4.md`'s instrumentation (next agent reads that file for the wall definition); prediction $D_{\rm wall}-\mathrm{reg}\ge B/2$. Falsification: $D_{\rm wall}\le\mathrm{reg}+2$ at $B=16$.

4. **UNSAT-side polynomial-calculus refutation degree of the $\mu_B$ Semaev system** `[adaptation]` — extends `RESEARCH_FFD_PROOF_COMPLEXITY.md` (next agent checks whether it measured first-fall degree on SAT instances, as the a1-n53-xor-degree-proof-20261012 artifacts in `cryptanalysis/experiments` suggest for binary fields) and IDEA-20260801-002. Mechanism: a random target decomposes with probability $\approx B^m/(m!N)$, so the relation stage is $\approx N/B^m$ refutations per relation; by Clegg–Edmonds–Impagliazzo (recalled) Gröbner degree equals PC degree, so F4 on the common UNSAT instance costs $\ge$ the degree-$D_{\rm PC}$ Macaulay matrix, $\binom{D_{\rm PC}+m}{m}$ columns. If $D_{\rm PC}\approx c(B-1)$ with $c\ge m-1$, the Gröbner relation stage cannot beat $B^m$ enumeration: the joint-solve exponent $e_k$ of the relations wave is pinned at $m$. Exponent needed for a lever: $c<(m-1)/1.5$; best known 1/2 unaffected. Obstruction: with $m$ variables the PC degree→size translation is weak; the bound is only meaningful as a Macaulay-column count. Falsification: median $D_{\rm PC}$ over UNSAT targets $\le 1.5(B-1)$ at $m=3$, $B=16$, below the random-unit control. Record B.

5. **Algebraic-degree profile of the ECDLP map: $\mathbb F_p$ interpolation degree/weight and $\mathbb F_2$ algebraic immunity of DL bits** `[adaptation]` — Lange–Winterhof and Kiltz–Winterhof (recalled, no KR row) prove degree lower bounds for polynomials interpolating the EC discrete log on large sets; no analogue exists for Boolean annihilators. Mechanism: a degree-$d$ annihilator $g$ of the 1-set of $b_j(x)=\text{bit}_j(\mathrm{DL}(x))$ is a free degree-$d$ constraint on the bits of $x(Q)$ for the bit-blasted SAT lane (`RESEARCH_SAT_SEMAEV.md`, Stage-26 CaDiCaL work in `crypto` PRs #155–#157, wave-1 "Bit-blasted SAT" record). Prediction: both profiles sit at the random-function counting values (interpolation degree $|X|-1$; algebraic immunity equal to the smallest $d$ with $\binom{n}{\le d}>|X_1|$). Exponent: delta 0; a positive signal would be a new object, not yet an algorithm. Obstruction: counting-bound saturation is expected on Fourier grounds (barriers-wave hybrid-sum record). Falsification: immunity two below the counting bound on any bit at $n\le16$. Record C.

6. **Determinantal complexity of $S_{m+1}$ is $m+1$; the hardness is not in the polynomial** `[known]` — the relations-wave incidence-determinant record writes $R+\sum P_i=O$ as $\det[\varphi(P_i)]=0$ with $\varphi$ a basis of $L((m+1)O)$, linear in each point, and the wildcards ISD lead reads decomposition as a weight-$m$ dual codeword of an elliptic AG code (Cheng, recalled). What a determinantal lower bound would rule out: nothing, since the determinantal complexity is already minimal; the cost lives in the rationality constraint "the $m$ vectors come from $x(F)$", i.e. a syndrome-decoding instance at rate $\to1$, which is k-sum again. Exponent: delta 0 (KR-IC-5a43e5, KR-RHO-5b1c0a). Falsification: any ISD variant below $B^{\lceil m/2\rceil}$ on elliptic codes at toy length.

7. **Strassen degree bound on division polynomials / EDS** `[known]` — wave-1 wildcards lead; `RESEARCH_EDS_RESIDUE.md`. $\deg_x\psi_k\approx k^2/2$ gives a straight-line lower bound $\Omega(\log k)$ for $x(kP)$ from $x(P)$, met by double-and-add; the inverse direction has no degree tool because $k\notin\mathbb F_p$, which is why lead 5 recasts it as interpolation. Rules out: nothing about ECDLP; delta 0. Test: none; closure note.

8. **Bilinear complexity of the biquadratic addition law** `[known]` — Bosma–Lenstra (recalled): complete addition laws on a Weierstrass cubic in $\mathbb P^2$ are bidegree $(2,2)$ and form a 3-dimensional space; each is a tensor in $\mathrm{Sym}^2F^3\otimes\mathrm{Sym}^2F^3\otimes F^3$ (6×6×3). A rank lower bound only bounds multiplications per step (excluded by the hard constraints) and the AND-gate count per step in bit-level CNF (a constant in $n$). Delta 0 (KR-RHO-13bf67). Cheap test: flattening ranks of the 6×6×3 tensor for the Bosma–Lenstra laws, half a day; records the constant for the SAT lane and nothing more.

9. **Algebraic adversaries over $\mathbb F_p$ have no lower-bound tool** `[known]` — Shoup/Maurer bounds (KR-RHO-13bf67) hold only for group-operation-and-equality algorithms; the algebraic group model assumes DL hardness rather than proving it; the only algebraic lower bounds on circuits using the coordinates are counting (lead 5) and exponential-sum (barriers wave). Consequence for this lane: every record here is a measurement or a presentation certificate, never an unconditional "no algebraic ECDLP algorithm" theorem. Delta 0. No test.

10. **Alekhnovich–Razborov expansion precondition for the bit-level PDP CNF** `[adaptation]` — PC/resolution degree $\Omega(n)$ and size $\exp(\Omega(n))$ follow for refutations of bounded-width systems whose variable–constraint hypergraph expands. Mechanism: measure spectral and sampled boundary expansion of the CNFs in `cryptanalysis/experiments/g2-n31-four-add-sat-gate-20261010` and the Stage-26 CaDiCaL instances, against random k-CNF of equal density. If expanding, the bit-level SAT lane is exponential in $m\log p$ on UNSAT targets, i.e. $\ge$ enumeration; if not (dense field-multiplication gadgets), the theorem does not apply and the lane stays undetermined. Delta 0. Obstruction: multiplication gadgets have high arity and local density. Falsification: expansion matched to random k-CNF yet solver time sub-enumeration on certified-UNSAT targets.

11. **Degree-2 SoS / Sherali–Adams relaxation of interval PDP as a convex-relaxation kill test** `[speculative]` — encode "R decomposes at arity 3 over $\{x<L\}$" with 0/1 indicator variables and the Semaev equation; solve the degree-2 SoS SDP at $p\approx2^{10}$ on 100 decomposing and 100 non-decomposing targets. Known SoS lower bounds for planted sparse problems (recalled) predict no separation. Delta 0 if negative; a positive separation would be a new object. Obstruction: SoS lower bounds are for random instances; structured instances are not covered. Falsification: AUC $>0.6$ at degree 2.

12. **Exceptional-target determinant $\Delta_2(x_R)=\det M(x_R)$ and the IDEA-20260723-006 set** `[adaptation]` — for $m=2$ the specialised $S_3$ is a 3×3 matrix whose determinant is a polynomial of degree $\le6$ in $x_R$; its roots are the targets where the tensor degenerates. Prediction: roots are $\bar{\mathbb F}_p$-points related to $x(E[2])$, so no $\mathbb F_p$-rational exceptional target exists on a prime-order curve, and the rank-drop sets of lead 1 are contained in IDEA-20260723-006's $O(m)$ exceptional target set. Delta 0. Test: factor $\Delta_2$ symbolically; folded into Record A.

13. **Algebraic natural-proofs meta-barrier** `[speculative]` — Forbes–Shpilka–Volk / Grochow–Kumar–Saks–Saraf (recalled): if succinct hitting sets exist, no "algebraically natural" (low-degree, efficiently computable) property separates hard polynomials from easy ones. Consequence: the invariants in leads 1, 2, 5 (flattening rank, Waring rank, interpolation degree) cannot by themselves become unconditional circuit lower bounds for DL-related maps; what they can be is certificates about presentations. Delta 0. No test; filed so the lane does not promise theorems it cannot deliver.

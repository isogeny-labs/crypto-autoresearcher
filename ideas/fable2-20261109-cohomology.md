1. **Translation rigidity and the Kato–Saito collapse: pure cohomology of (E, P, Q) is scalar-blind** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: translation τ_P is algebraically equivalent to the identity (connected family), so τ_P^* = id on every Weil cohomology (ℓ-adic, crystalline, rigid); the class of the graph of τ_P equals the diagonal, and every correspondence built from P, Q by the group law has the class of its P = Q = O specialisation. On 0-cycles, CH_0(E^n)_0 ≅ E(F_p)^n (Kato–Saito class field theory, recalled), so the Chow-motivic content of the pair is the pair. Hence a point enters cohomology only as an extension class (the 1-motive [Z→E]) whose F_p-realisations the wave-2 cohomology record "every realization of the 1-motive … is split except at ℓ = N" already tabulates. Reduces ECDLP to: nothing (barrier). Exponent 1/2 → 1/2, delta 0. Obstruction: it is a theorem. Falsification: exhibit an F_p-rational cohomological invariant of (E, P, kP), functorial in the pair, that is not constant in k; the Kummer-cover isomorphism of lead 2 is the sharp test case. RECORD A.

2. **Kummer covers of the Miller quotient are E itself for every wrong guess** `[known]` — With h_j = f_Q·f_P^{−j} (Miller functions, divisor N[(Q) − j(P) + (j−1)(O)]), the μ_N-torsor X_j: z^N = h_j is étale; for j ≢ k it is a genus-1 curve with a rational point (gcd(N, p−1) = 1 makes every value an N-th power) whose Jacobian is E/L_1 ≅ E (Lang), so X_j ≅ E and the covering is the isogeny with kernel the Frobenius-eigenline L_p where π acts as p; for j ≡ k it is E × Spec F_p[z]/(z^N − c), with F_p[z]/(z^N − c) ≅ F_p × F_{p^{k_N}}^{(N−1)/k_N}. All X_j have #X_j(F_{p^r}) = #E(F_{p^r}) for r < k_N = ord_N(p); the first discriminating Euler factor is the MOV field. The wave-2 cohomology lead "Kummer sheaf of the Miller function" and KR-RHO-53c89f cover this. Delta 0. Falsification: a j ≠ k with #X_j(F_{p^r}) ≠ #E(F_{p^r}) for some r with gcd(N, p^r − 1) = 1.

3. **Canonical Frobenius splitting of H^1_rig(E ∖ {O, P, Q}); the index-N residue lattice is killed by Z_p; the Abel–Jacobi block is Miller-computable** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: the Gysin sequence 0 → H^1(E) → H^1_rig(U) → R(−1) → 0 has Frobenius eigenvalues {u, p/u} on H^1(E) and {p, p} on R (u the unit root, u ≠ 1 since N ≠ 0, u ≠ p since u is a unit), so Ext^1 of F-isocrystals over F_p vanishes and the eigenvalue-p eigenspace splits the sequence uniquely. The log-exact classes [d log g], g with divisor on S, are exact eigenvectors (F^*d log g = p·d log g + d(log of a principal unit)) and their residue lattice L_k = {(a,b,c): a+b+c = 0, b + ck ≡ 0 mod N} has index N in Z^3_{deg 0}; L_k ⊗ Z_p = Z_p^3_{deg 0}, so the integral F-crystal also splits and carries no trace of k. The algebraically normalised third-kind form ω_P^alg = (y + y_P)/(2(x − x_P))·dx/y satisfies ω_P^alg − (1/N)d log f_P̂ = e(P̂)·ω exactly, with e(P̂) ∈ Z_p computable by a Miller loop on the torsion-section lift (extends the wave-2 Coleman record, `RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md` for the CM Frobenius); so the full Frobenius matrix on H^1_rig(E ∖ S) for S ⊂ E(F_p) on secp256k1 costs Õ(|S| log N) against Tuitman's Õ(p|S|), and e(Q̂) − k·e(P̂) equals the coefficient of the algebraic form d log ĝ − ω_Q^alg + kω_P^alg, computable only from ĝ, i.e. from k.

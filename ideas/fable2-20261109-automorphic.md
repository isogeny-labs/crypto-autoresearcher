1. **The L-function of the 1-motive of a point is the L-function of its semisimplification** `[known]`. Mechanism: for any lift Ẽ/Q with P̃ ∈ Ẽ(Q), M_P̃ = [Z → Ẽ, 1 ↦ P̃] has V_ℓ(M) an extension of Q_ℓ by V_ℓẼ whose Kummer class is unramified at good primes q ≠ ℓ, so every good Euler factor is det on the graded pieces and L(M_P̃, s) = ζ(s)·L(Ẽ, s) identically in P̃. The point survives only in extension *periods* (elliptic log, Coleman, p-adic log) and in the Beilinson/Scholl regulator, i.e. heights, which obey ĥ(kP̃) = k²ĥ(P̃) only on a compatible lift. Reduction: compatible-lift problem (filed as `[known]` in the automorphic and descent waves; the cohomology-wave record "Every realization of the 1-motive of a point … is split except at ℓ = N" is the realization-level statement). Exponent: none moves. Obstruction: no automorphic L-function sees non-semisimple structure. Falsification: an Euler factor at a good prime of a two-point lift that differs between P̃ and Q̃; test on 20 lifts at ℓ ≤ 100 in PARI (`ellap`), expected identical. Next agent: confirm in `ledger/proposals` the id of the cohomology-wave 1-motive record before filing a cross-reference.

Reduction: none (barrier). Exponent delta 0. Obstruction: this is a theorem schema, not a theorem; "every construction" is not a closed class. Falsification test: at p ≤ 2^10 compute #V(F_{p^n}), n ≤ 3, for V = Bl_{(P,Q)}(E×E) and for V = Bl_{(P,P')}(E×E) with random P'; predicted equal. Extends the wave-2 RECORD A (diamond/trace-formula population) by stating the closure it instantiates.

4. **Power traces, the dynamical zeta of [k], and the spectral measure all reduce to ord_N(k), the Cheon residue** `[known]`. Reduction: evaluating either requires applying [k] to points other than P, i.e. k²P from kP (CDH); ord_N(k) is the Pohlig–Hellman class of ind k in Z/(N−1), the quantity the port-wave record "Pohlig–Hellman on the scalar torsor" and `cryptanalysis/include/ca_cheon.h` already price. Exponent: √N generic (KR-RHO-13bf67). Obstruction: generic-model hardness of CDH. Falsification: any algorithm deciding k^d P = ±P for a chosen d | N−1 in o(√N) without k.

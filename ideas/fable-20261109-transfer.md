## LEADS

1. **Pairing (MOV / Frey–Rück) transfer with an embedding-degree certificate.** Map: Tate pairing $E(\mathbb F_p)[N]\times E(\mathbb F_{p^k})[N]\to\mu_N\subset\mathbb F_{p^k}^*$, $k=\mathrm{ord}_N(p)$; image of $(P,Q)$ is $(e(P,T),e(Q,T))=(g,g^k)$. Target order $N\mid p^k-1$, target DLP exTNFS $L_{p^k}(1/3)$, which beats $2^{128}$ only when $p^k\lesssim2^{3200}$, i.e. $k\le12$. Needed exponent: $k\le12$; best known for secp256k1: $k\mid N-1$ and generically $k\approx N$ (Balasubramanian–Koblitz density $\le 12/N$ for a random prime-order curve). Obstruction: $k$ is an isogeny invariant and astronomically large. Falsification: factor $N-1$ in PARI and compute $\mathrm{ord}_N(p)$; a result $\le12$ kills the obstruction. `[known]` — KR-RHO-53c89f; Wave-1 transfer record "Transfer-admissibility certificate".

2. **Anomalous / $p$-adic transfer, including ascent to $\mathbb F_{p^{k_p}}$.** Map: Hensel lift then formal-group log $\tilde E_1(\mathbb Q_p)\to p\mathbb Z_p$; target $(\mathbb Z/p,+)$, DLP $O(1)$. Needs $N=p$ ($t=1$); secp256k1 has $t\approx1.27\cdot2^{128}$. Ascent: $p\mid\#E(\mathbb F_{p^k})$ iff $t^k\equiv1\pmod p$, but the acquired $p$-torsion is a different isotypic component and the formal log kills the prime-to-$p$ group. Exponent needed: 0; obstruction: $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb Z/N,\mathbb Z/p)=0$. Test: `CTRL-ANOMALOUS-TRACE1` (IDEA-20260805-cc4c2c) on a same-prime non-anomalous arm returns nothing. `[known]` — KR-RHO-53c89f; Wave-2 drinfeld record "Drinfeld distance $k_p$".

3. **Sextic genus-2 cover of $y^2=x^3+7$ — the required feasibility calculation.** Cover $C:y^2=x^6+7\to E$, $(x,y)\mapsto(x^2,y)$, degree 2, genus 2; the second quotient $(x,y)\mapsto(1/x^2,y/x^3)$ lands on $E_{49}$, so $\mathrm{Jac}(C)\sim E_7\times E_{49}$ and $\#\mathrm{Jac}(C)(\mathbb F_p)=N\cdot N'$ with $N'$ a sextic-twist order $\approx2^{256}$ (quadratic twist $p+1+t$ if 7 is a cube mod $p$): target size $\approx2^{512}$. Best DLP there: GTTD double-large-prime genus-2 index calculus, factor base $\approx p^{1/2}=2^{128}$ points, cost $\tilde O(p)=2^{256}$, linear algebra $2^{128}\times2^{128}$. Even "touch the factor base once" costs $2^{128}$ = all of rho. Needed exponent $<1/2$ in $\log N$; delivered $1$. Obstruction: the size law — every abelian target over $\mathbb F_p$ of dimension $g$ has $p^g$ points and IC costs $p^{2-2/g}\ge p$. Test: none needed (theorem); toy genus-2 IC slope is already pre-registered. `[known]` — Wave-1 transfer record "Degree-2 cover $y^2=x^6+b$ ... costs $p^{1+o(1)}$"; KR-IC-a4dd54.

4. **Higher-genus and plane-quartic covers (Diem's $\tilde O(q)$ for non-hyperelliptic $g=3$).** Any $f:C\to E$ over $\mathbb F_p$ gives $f^*:E\hookrightarrow\mathrm{Jac}(C)$, $\#\mathrm{Jac}\approx p^g$; Diem's genus-3 algorithm costs $\tilde O(p)$, GTTD costs $p^{2-2/g}\uparrow p^2$. No cover can be defined over a smaller field because $p$ is prime (no subfield), and no correspondence lands in a simple abelian variety (Poincaré: $\mathrm{Hom}(E,A)\neq0$ forces $E$ to be an isogeny factor). Needed: a target of size $<p^{1}$ receiving a nonzero map — impossible by Tate for anything not isogenous. Test: none; closed. `[known]` — Wave-1 "Transfer-admissibility certificate" (size law), KR-IC-a4dd54, KR-RHO-755e35.

5. **Kani $(\ell,\ell)$-gluing $E\times E'\to\mathrm{Jac}(H)$ with $E'$ free, and the Poincaré escape.** Order $N\cdot N'$ with $N'$ chosen smooth or special; the $N$-part still sits in a genus-2 Jacobian ($\tilde O(p)$). Composite route $E\to\mathrm{Jac}(H)\to E''\times E'''$ (Kani's product case) gives a homomorphism $E\to E''$, hence an isogeny, hence KR-RHO-755e35. Real multiplication on $\mathrm{Jac}(H)$ buys only an automorphism constant. Falsification: exhibit a genus-2 $H/\mathbb F_p$ with $N\mid\#\mathrm{Jac}(H)$ and $\mathrm{Jac}(H)$ simple — impossible by point count of $\mathrm{Jac}(H)\otimes\mathbb Q$ ... closed. `[known]` — Wave-2 moduli lead "Genus-2 Humbert/Kani covers"; Wave-1 transfer lead "Kani gluing".

6. **Unit-sibling curves: six primes $q_u=\mathrm{Nm}(1+(\pi-1)u)$, $u\in\mu_6$, each carrying a $j=0$ curve of order exactly $N$.** Lenstra's isomorphism $E(\mathbb F_p)\cong O_K/(\pi-1)$ depends on the ideal, not on $\pi$; changing $\pi-1$ by a unit keeps the group $O_K/\mathfrak n$ but moves the prime ($u=-1$: $q=p-2t+4$). This is the only transfer whose target order equals $N$ without being an isogeny — it survives the order computation trivially. ECDLP reduces to ECDLP on $E_u/\mathbb F_{q_u}$, another 256-bit prime-order curve: exponent $1/2$ even if the map were free. Obstruction (theorem): compatible maps form a torsor under $(O_K/\mathfrak n)^*=(\mathbb Z/N)^*$, realised geometrically by choosing primes of the ray class field $K_{\mathfrak n}$ above $p$ and $q_u$, with Galois group $(O_K/\mathfrak n)^*/\mu_6$; the choice *is* the log. Test: toy point counts of all six siblings; secp256k1 primality and $\mathrm{ord}_N(q_u)$. `[adaptation]` — extends the Wave-2 torsion lead "Cross-field CM transfer" (speculative); closes it. → Record 1.

7. **Anomalous sibling over a 512-bit prime.** Take $\alpha=a+(2a+1)\omega$ (trace $-1$, $\mathrm{Nm}\,\alpha=3a^2+3a+1$) with $\alpha\equiv0\pmod{\mathfrak n}$, i.e. $a\equiv-\lambda(1+2\lambda)^{-1}\pmod N$ ($\lambda$ the GLV eigenvalue, $(1+2\lambda)^2\equiv-3$). Then $q=\mathrm{Nm}(1+\alpha)=\mathrm{Nm}\,\alpha\approx3N^2\approx2^{514}$, the curve $E_\gamma/\mathbb F_q$ with Frobenius $1+\alpha$ has $\#E_\gamma=q$ (trace 1, Smart/Satoh–Araki/Semaev in polynomial time) and contains $O_K/\mathfrak n\cong\mathbb Z/N$ as its $N$-torsion. Target survives every order computation and has a polynomial DLP; needed exponent 0. Obstruction: identical torsor theorem — the compatible map is a scalar choice. Value: the exact positive control for the cross-characteristic lane (target solver verified on a brute-forced compatible map at toy size) and the sharpest statement of what cannot leak. `[adaptation]` → Record 3.

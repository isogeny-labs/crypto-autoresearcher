## LEADS

Wave-1 p-adic items that forced each choice are named in brackets. All five keep the exponent at $1/2$; the delta against Pollard rho (KR-RHO-13bf67) is $0$ in every case and is stated as such.

1. **Local-exit barrier over every finite extension $K/\mathbb{Q}_p$, ramified or not.** `[adaptation]` *(forced by wave-1 lead 12, which treated only $\mathbb{Q}_p$-pairings, and record 1, which fixed one lift rule).* Mechanism: $0\to E_1(K)\to E(K)\to E(k_K)\to 0$ with $E_1(K)\cong\hat E(\mathfrak m_K)$ pro-$p$; since $p\nmid N$ the $N$-torsion lifts uniquely (torsion section $s$), so every homomorphism $h:E(K)\to M$ into a torsion-free module satisfies $h(\tilde P)=h(\tilde P-s(P))$, and every quadratic map $q$ likewise ($q|_{\mathbb{Z}/N}=0$ because $N^2q(T)=q(NT)=0$, and the bilinear cross term with a torsion argument vanishes). This closes in one statement the formal log, Coleman integrals of first and second kind, Buium characters, Mazur–Tate/Bernardi/Schneider heights, over $\mathbb{Q}_p(p^{1/e})$ for all $e$ (at $e\ge p-1$ the formal group acquires $p$-torsion, never $N$-torsion). Reduction: none; the value depends only on the lifter's choice. Obstruction: it is a barrier, the analytic-local analogue of the exit-map theorem in `IDEA-20260727-005` and the port-wave exit-dimension record. Falsification: at $p\le2^{10}$, over $K=\mathbb{Q}_p(p^{1/e})$, $e\in\{1,2,p-1,p\}$, enumerate $\hat E(\mathfrak m_K)[N]$ to precision $\pi^{20}$; any nonzero element kills the barrier. Next agent reads `crypto/sage` for the lift helpers the wave-1 records used.

2. **Buium's order-2 $\delta$-character $\psi_2$ on non-canonical lifts.** `[adaptation]` *(forced by wave-1 lead 6, order 1 on the canonical lift only).* Mechanism (recalled, Buium 1995/1997): for ordinary $E/\mathbb{Z}_p$ without a Frobenius lift the $\mathbb{Z}_p$-module of $\delta$-characters of order $\le2$ has rank 1, generated by $\psi_2:E(\mathbb{Z}_p)\to\mathbb{Z}_p$ built from $\phi(x)=x^p+p\delta x$ and $X^2-a_pX+p$, and $\ker\psi_2=E(\mathbb{Z}_p)_{\mathrm{tors}}$. By lead 1, $\psi_2(s(P))=0$ and $\psi_2(L(P))=u\cdot\log_{\hat E}(L(P)-s(P))$ for a unit $u$ depending on $E$ only. Reduction: none (zero information about $k$). Obstruction: Buium's explicit formula must be transcribed; the next agent must read the 1995 paper, not recall it. Falsification: implement $\psi_2$ to precision $p^3$ at $p\le2^8$ on a non-CM prime-order toy curve; $\psi_2(s(P))\ne0$ on any $P$ would refute the recalled kernel theorem and would be the only interesting outcome.

3. **Local Tate duality is Frey–Rück in disguise; the $H^1$ half wave-1 lead 12 omitted.** `[known]` (KR-RHO-53c89f). Mechanism: Kummer gives $E(\mathbb{Q}_p)/N\cong E(\mathbb{F}_p)\hookrightarrow H^1(\mathbb{Q}_p,E[N])$ with image $H^1_{ur}\cong E[N]/(\mathrm{Frob}-1)E[N]\cong\mathbb{Z}/N$ (needs $p\not\equiv1\bmod N$); $H^1_{ur}$ annihilates itself, which is wave-1 lead 12's triviality, while the perfect pairing pairs it against $H^1/H^1_{ur}\cong(\text{$p$-eigenline of Frob on }E[N])(-1)$, generated by $R$ with $\mathrm{Frob}\,R=pR$, rational only over $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}$, $k=\mathrm{ord}_N(p)$, and the value is the Tate pairing $\langle P,R\rangle\in\mu_N\subset\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^*$. Reduction: ECDLP $\to$ DLP in $\mathbb{F}_{p^k}^*$ at $L_{p^k}(1/3)$ with $k\approx N$ for secp256k1, i.e. exponentially worse than $p^{1/2}$. Obstruction: embedding degree, already the certificate quantity of the transfer-wave admissibility record. Falsification: compute $k=\mathrm{ord}_N(p)$ for secp256k1 and for every curve in `crypto/docs/ECDLP_CALIBRATION_PRIMES.md`; any $k<2^{20}$ on a curve not built for it would be news. Next agent checks `crypto/docs/ECDLP_ATTACK_MATRIX.md` for an existing embedding-degree column.

4. **The Hensel-digit coboundary $f_H$ and its hybrid spectrum (Record A).** `[speculative]` *(forced by wave-1 lead 11, which asked only for the algebraic degree of the second Witt digit and ignored the cocycle structure and the spectral test).* Mechanism: fix the Hensel lift $L$ to $\mathbb{Z}/p^2$; the extension $0\to\mathbb{F}_p\to E(\mathbb{Z}/p^2)\to E(\mathbb{F}_p)\to0$ splits by $s$, so $c_H(R,S)=L(R)+L(S)-L(R+S)$ is a 2-coboundary, $c_H=\delta f_H$ with $f_H(R)=N^{-1}\,t([N]L(R))/p\bmod p$, computable in $O(\log N)$ additions mod $p^2$. $f_H$ is the only non-algebraic $\mathbb{F}_p$-valued function the $p$-adic lane produces for free. Reduction attempted: a digit-box factor base $\{x(R)<p^\beta,\ f_H(R)<p^\gamma\}$ with excess internal relations. Required: the hybrid sum $\sum_k e_p(af_H(kP))e(bk/N)$ far above $\sqrt N$ at low frequencies; best known exponent $1/2$ unchanged unless it is. Obstruction: $f_H$ is a carry of integer cubes, expected Weyl-equidistributed. Falsification: the exhaustive matrix at $p\le2^{14}$ from one log table, the same instrument as the barriers-wave hybrid-sum record, with a random-function control; `research/ecdlp_autolab` is where the enumeration must run.

5. **The quasi-quadratic $p$-adic local height on torsion sections is $-\log_p(b_R^2)/(4N)$, $b_R$ Ward's periodicity constant (Record B).** `[adaptation]` *(forced by wave-1 lead 12, which closed only additive and bilinear invariants, and lead 9, which names $b$ but not the height).* Mechanism: homogeneous solutions of $\lambda(R{+}S)+\lambda(R{-}S)-2\lambda(R)-2\lambda(S)=\varepsilon\log_p(x(s(R))-x(s(S)))$ on $\mathbb{Z}/N$ are quadratic forms into $\mathbb{Q}_p$, hence zero, so the law determines $\lambda$ on torsion sections uniquely; the $\sigma$-identity $\sigma(kR)=\sigma(R)^{k^2}\psi_k(R)$ and Ward periodicity force $\lambda(s(R))=-\log_p(b_R^2)/(4N)$, with $b_{kR}=b_R^{k^2}\psi_k(R)^{-2N}$ exactly. On $j=0$ the automorphism $\zeta$ makes Frobenius diagonal on $H^1_{dR}$ in the basis $(\omega,\eta)$ (distinct $\zeta^*$-eigenvalues $\zeta,\zeta^2$; relevant to `RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md`), so Coleman double integrals between torsion sections are not obstructed by the Frobenius matrix; the identification above says they contain nothing beyond the EDS data of `RESEARCH_EDS_RESIDUE.md`. Reduction: ECDLP $\to$ the EDS residue/association problem, Lauter–Stange's equivalence, exponent $1/2$ unchanged. Falsification: the law fails on one pair at toy $p$.

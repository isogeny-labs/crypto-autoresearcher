# Angle: point decomposition and relation generation over a prime field

Framing fixed before the leads. Over F_p nothing compresses the factor base: the small-x interval base used by the Semaev lane (RESEARCH_SEMAEV_DECOMPOSITION.md, per the brief's titles) is not algebraic, so every recorded solve enumerates m−1 variables and root-finds the last, giving cost ≈ N/B per relation — exactly what a trivial rejection-sampling collector (random point lands in the factor base) also costs. That is the content of KR-IC-5931ee and of IDEA-20260727-003's floor. Every lead below is honest about this: the only exponent-moving quantity in this family is the *joint-solve exponent* e_k (Lead 2); everything else is a delta-0 discriminating measurement or a known constant. The three degree/elimination leads (3, 4, 5) share one novelty: a factor base whose membership predicate is simultaneously algebraic and cheap (x^B = 1, B | p−1), which for the first time lets F4/SAT act on a zero-dimensional prime-field membership system.

## LEADS

1. **μ_B factor base with resultant-tower decomposition.** Mechanism: for B | p−1 take F_B = {P : x(P)^B = 1} (≈B points, B/2 x-values). Membership is a degree-B binomial costing log₂B squarings. Decompose R by enumerating x_1..x_{m−2} over rational x in μ_B and solving (x_{m−1}, x_m) ∈ μ_B² jointly: write s(X,Y) = S_{m+1}(…,X,Y;x_R), DFT its 2^{m−1}+1 coefficient polynomials on μ_B (the length-B DFT exists because B | p−1), build ∏_ζ s(X,ζ) (degree 2^{m−1}B) by a product tree, compute X^B mod it, gcd with X^B−1. Reduces ECDLP to B relations plus sparse LA. Cost: relation stage ≈ 2·m!·N·log²B field mults (exponent 1, constant 2·m!), LA (m+1)B² = (m+1)(m!N)^{2/m}; best known 1/2 (rho). Obstruction: solving k variables jointly costs B^{k−1}, so enumeration is never beaten; for secp256k1 a divisor of p−1 near p^{1/m} is needed (2·3·7 | p−1 by hand; the rest is not in the brief). Falsification: at p≈2^30, m=3, B=2^10..2^14 the (x_2,x_3) solve time scales as B^{1.0±0.1} — the floor stands. Extends RESEARCH_SEMAEV_DECOMPOSITION.md (adds an algebraic base); contradicts the degree-only reading of IDEA-20260801-021 (it bounds |F| ≤ 3d but does not price sparse predicates); would make the p_exist/p_solve split of GOAL-RELN-001 (IDEA-20260805-96cb3d) unnecessary for this base. `[adaptation]` of IDEA-20260727-006's "Fourier-accessible" base and KR-IC-5931ee.

2. **The joint-solve exponent e_k is the only exponent lever.** Define C(k) = cost of finding all zeros of S_{m+1}(fixed; X_1..X_k) on μ_B^k, C(k) ∝ B^{e_k}. With B^m = m!N the relation stage is B·B^{m−k}·C(k), so beating rho needs e_m < m/2 − 1. Resultant tower: e_k = k−1. Sorted-list claw (IDEA-20260731-007): e_k = ⌈k/2⌉ with B^{k/2} memory — misses the rho threshold by exactly one factor of B for every m. Reduces ECDLP to "find torsion points of G_m^k on a hypersurface over F_p", for which no sub-enumerative algorithm is known (Bourgain-type bounds on |V ∩ H^k| are non-constructive). Required: e_4 < 1 at m=4; best known 2 (claw) / 3 (tower). Obstruction: the F_p-rational structure of S (it splits over F̄_p in the last variable) gives 2^{m−k}× more zeros than a random polynomial but no faster way to find them. Falsification: measured e_2 = 1, e_3 = 2 on both S and a null polynomial (Record B). `[speculative]`

3. **F4 on the algebraic-membership system (degree lead).** System Σ_B = {S_{m+1}(x_1..x_m;x_R), x_i^B − 1, i=1..m}: m+1 equations in m unknowns, degrees 2^{m−1} per variable (total ≤ m·2^{m−1}) and B. d_reg: first fall at B (first use of x_i^B → 1); Macaulay bound on the regular m-subsequence m(B−1)+1; estimate D_max ∈ [B+2^{m−1}, mB] (not a semi-regularity claim — KR-IC-fbdb61). Macaulay columns at degree ≈B: C(B+m,m) ≈ B^m/m!, so predicted F4 exponent in B lies in [m−1, mω]. Yield per solve B^m/(m!N) rational relations (≈1) plus (2^m−1)× spurious F̄_p zeros filtered. LA: (m+1)B². Total ≥ B·B^{m−1}·polylog = m!N·polylog if F4 matches the tower, ≥ (m!N)^{1+1/m} if e_F4 = m; exponent ≥ 1 vs 1/2. Experiment: p ∈ {2^20,2^24,2^28} with 128 | p−1, m=3, B=16..128, 20 targets, record D_max and time, fit e_F4, compare tower (e=2) and a random-polynomial null. Distinguishes from the recorded baseline because RESEARCH_GROEBNER_F4.md's systems (the next agent must read it to confirm) carry no membership equation and are positive-dimensional; here F4 returns a 0-dim basis and the observable is D_max ≈ B. Would make obsolete the p_exist/p_solve separation for algebraic bases. `[adaptation]` — Record A.

4. **Chained S_3 tower vs flat S_{m+1} over F_p (degree lead).** System Σ_C = {S_3(x_1,x_2,u_1), S_3(u_j,x_{j+2},u_{j+1}) j=1..m−3, S_3(u_{m−2},x_m,x_R), x_i^B−1}: 2m−1 equations in 2m−2 unknowns; S_3 has degree 2 per variable, total 4. d_reg: Macaulay on a (2m−2)-subsequence m(B−1)+3(m−2)+1 ≈ mB — the same wall; first falls at degree 8 as F4 re-derives S_4 = Res_u(S_3,S_3). Columns C(B+2m−2, 2m−2) ≈ B^{2m−2}/(2m−2)! ≫ B^m/m!, so prediction T_C/T_F ≥ 1 growing like B^{m−2}. Yield and LA identical to Lead 3; exponent ≥ 1. Experiment: same grid as Lead 3, both forms, metric T_C/T_F and D_max. Baseline: the boolean chained form of src/semaev_tree.py (IDEA-20260801-002, IDEA-20260805-90a841) is favoured over F_2^n because link variables inherit vector-space membership; over F_p the links carry no membership, so the predicted reversal is the discriminating outcome. Extends RESEARCH_HIGHER_SEMAEV.md; would make an F_p port of the chained tree obsolete if the ratio is ≥ 1. `[adaptation]` — Record C.

5. **Incidence-determinant presentation over L((m+1)O) (degree lead).** Variables (x_i,y_i), i=1..m; basis φ = (1, x, y, x², xy, x³, …) of L((m+1)O), dimension m+1 (monomials x^a y^b, b ≤ 1, 2a+3b ≤ m+1); equations det[φ(P_1);…;φ(P_m);φ(−R)] = 0 (multilinear across points; per-point degree ≤ ⌊(m+1)/2⌋ in x and 1 in y; total degree ≤ m⌊(m+1)/2⌋ — polynomial in m against S_{m+1}'s m·2^{m−1}), y_i² − f(x_i), x_i^B − 1: 2m+1 equations in 2m unknowns. Eliminating the y_i returns S_{m+1} times its sign conjugates, so the projected variety equals Lead 3's. d_reg ≈ mB + O(m²) by Macaulay; first falls at ≤ m²/2+3 (y-elimination). Yield, LA identical; exponent ≥ 1. Experiment: same grid, record T_D/T_F and the degree at which the first y-free polynomial appears (prediction 2^{m−1} per x-variable: F4 rebuilds S_{m+1}). KR-IC-8b9daa (Karabina's symmetric formulation) is the binary cousin; IDEA-20260731-014 asks exactly whether presentation moves Gröbner cost. The next agent must read research/semaev_alternatives_audit_20261104 for whether this determinant is already audited. `[adaptation]` — Record C.

6. **Symmetrised S_{m+1} in elementary-symmetric coordinates over F_p.** RESEARCH_SYMMETRIZED_SEMAEV.md presumably follows FGHR/FHJRV (KR-IC-955fd6), whose 2-torsion translation needs 2 | #E and is unavailable on prime-order curves; only Aut(E) survives (Lead 7). Writing S̃(e_1..e_m; x_R) shrinks the polynomial by m!, and μ_B membership becomes "X^m − e_1X^{m−1} + … divides X^B − 1", checkable in log B polynomial multiplications — a polylog membership *test* in e-space. But S̃ = 0 is one equation in m unknowns ranging over F_p, so solving still enumerates; exponent 1. Cheap test: confirm divisibility check cost ∝ log B and that enumerating e-space is no cheaper than enumerating x-space at p ≈ 2^24. `[known]` via KR-IC-955fd6; extends the symmetrised file only by the divisibility test.

7. **Weighted homogeneity on j = 0 (secp256k1 model).** With a = 0, S_3 is weighted-homogeneous of weight 4 (wt x = 1, wt b = 3), so S_{m+1}(ζx_1..ζx_m; ζx_R) = ζ^w S_{m+1} for ζ³ = 1; if 3 | B the zero set on μ_B^{m+1} is μ_3-stable and, with negation, relations come in orbits of 6. Constant factor 6 on relation collection and on B — the index-calculus shadow of KR-RHO-0528e7's √6, already recorded as IDEA-20260726-002 and IDEA-20260727-001 (O(1) displacement rank, no LA gain). Falsification: measured tower-cost drop ≠ 3 on orbit representatives at p ≡ 1 mod 3, 3 | B. `[known]`

8. **Rejection-sampling collector as the mandatory charged null control.** Mechanism: walk R_i = R_{i−1} + P_step, accept when x(R_i)^B = 1 (log B squarings); each acceptance is a relation R_i = ±F at N/|F_B| group operations — the same per-relation cost as enumeration-plus-root-finding Semaev. Reduces nothing; it is the baseline against which every F_p decomposition claim must show ratio < 1. Obstruction: none; it is a control. Falsification of a *lead*: ρ_m = M_tower/M_rejection ≥ 1 (predicted ≈ 2·m!·log²B). Extends cryptanalysis/experiments/factor-base-yield and factor-base-yield-v2 (the next agent must read their receipts to see whether the N/|F| rate is already measured) and the `ic` command's "bounded factor-base comparisons"; would make obsolete any Semaev-vs-rho comparison in RESEARCH_SEMAEV_DECOMPOSITION.md that lacks this arm. `[adaptation]` of IDEA-20260731-011 and IDEA-20260805-061f97 — Record D.

9. **Unlikely-intersection yield audit.** Count Z_S = |{x ∈ μ_B^k : S_{m+1}(fixed; x) = 0}| aggregated over 10^4 random fixings against Z_null for a random polynomial of the same multidegree. Prediction: Z_S/Z_null ≥ 2^{m−k} (S splits over F̄_p into 2^{m−1} linear factors in the last variable; the rational part alone gives 2^{m−k}B^k/N), independent of B. A ratio growing with B would be the first structural excess of summation-polynomial zeros on a subgroup product — the only place an "unlikely intersection" signal could show. Exponent effect: none unless the excess is super-constant. Falsification: ratio flat in B within Poisson error. Extends IDEA-20260805-a25f11's exact mean μ(B,m) and RESEARCH_PKM_CRITERION.md (the next agent must read it for the recorded admissibility criterion of algebraic bases and check μ_B against it). `[speculative]`

10. **Large-prime variation with μ_B ⊂ μ_{B'}.** B | B' | p−1; the tower's final gcd is taken with X^{B'} − 1 at polylog extra cost, producing one-large-prime relations; LP graph elimination as in KR-IC-a4dd54's double-large-prime tradition. Cost per relation ≥ N/B' still; exponent 1. Already recorded as IDEA-20260731-012. Cheap test: LP-relation rate equals (B'/B)·(full rate) at p ≈ 2^28. `[known]`

13. **Union-of-cosets predicate for deployability.** If p−1 has no divisor near p^{1/m}, use F = ∪_{j≤r} {x^d = c_j} with d | p−1 small: predicate ∏_j(x^d − c_j) has degree rd but costs log d + r multiplications mod the degree-2^{m−1} polynomial in the gcd step. Loss factor r = B/d against Lead 1; for secp256k1 with d ≤ 13441 (if that is the largest small divisor — not in the brief, must be checked) r ≈ 2^{50} at m = 4, i.e. back to enumeration. Falsification: gcd-step time ∝ r at fixed d, p ≈ 2^28. `[adaptation]`

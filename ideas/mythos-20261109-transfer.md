3. **Sextic genus-2 cover of $y^2=x^3+7$ — the feasibility calculation.** Cover $C:y^2=x^6+b\to E_b$, $(x,y)\mapsto(x^2,y)$, degree 2, genus 2; the second involution $(x,y)\mapsto(-x,-y)$ has quotient $Y^2=X^3+b^2$, so $\mathrm{Jac}(C)\sim E_b\times E_{b^2}$ and $\#\mathrm{Jac}(C)(\mathbb{F}_p)=N\cdot N_2$: the order condition is automatic for every $(p,b)$, and $\varphi^*$ is injective on $E[N]$ ($N$ odd). Size: $\mathrm{Jac}(C)(\mathbb{F}_p)\approx2^{512}$ for secp256k1. Degree-1 factor base $\approx p/6$ orbits under the order-12 automorphism group $\approx2^{253}$ elements — enumerating it alone exceeds rho's $2^{127}$ (with $\sqrt6$). Relation probability $\approx1/2$; Gaudry–Thomé–Thériault–Diem double large primes give $p^{1+o(1)}\approx2^{256}$; a reduced base $B=p^\alpha$ costs $2p^2/B+B^2\ge p^{4/3}$. Exponent 1 vs 1/2, delta $+0.5$. Obstruction: the base field cannot shrink; GHS gains come from $q^n\to q$. Test: toy ladder of charged genus-2 IC vs `ca_rho.h`, pipeline modeled on `research/ghs-c2pnb`; `RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md` must be read for the twist orders. [adaptation] → Record 1.

4. **Superelliptic towers and the size law.** $C_k:y^2=x^{3k}+7\to E$ via $x\mapsto x^k$, genus $g=\lfloor(3k-1)/2\rfloor$, $\mathrm{Jac}(C_k)\sim E\times\mathrm{Prym}$, cost $p^{2-2/g}\uparrow p^2$. General statement: any cover or correspondence $C\to E$ over $\mathbb{F}_p$ puts $E$ in $\mathrm{Jac}(C)$ up to isogeny, pullback injective on the $N$-part when $\gcd(N,\deg)=1$, and Jacobian IC over $\mathbb{F}_p$ costs $\ge p^{2-2/g}\ge p$ for all $g\ge2$; the "hidden correspondences" family of IDEA-20260805-51996a is inside this law. Exponent needed $<1/2$, delivered $\ge1$. Test: none beyond Riemann–Hurwitz; falsified only by a genus-$g$ Jacobian IC over $\mathbb{F}_q$ costing $<q^{1/2}$ on a subgroup of order $\approx q$. [known] KR-IC-a4dd54, KR-IC-5ea2f8.

7. **Ascent to $\mathbb{F}_{p^n}$ plus Gaudry/Diem decomposition.** Weil restriction $W$ of a base-changed curve splits: $W\sim E\times T$ ($T$ trace-zero, dim $n-1$); the $N$-part stays in the $E$ factor. Diem's $n\approx\sqrt{\log q}$ needs $q=p$ to be the small field; $p$ cannot shrink. Exponent $\ge1$ vs 1/2. `RESEARCH_DIEM_DESCENT.md` must be read for whether the base-change case was recorded; the `ic` executable's bounded factor-base comparisons are the natural host. 10. **Lenstra-module and class-group exit maps.** $E(\mathbb{F}_p)\cong\mathcal{O}_K/(\pi-1)\cong\mathbb{F}_N$ as $\mathcal{O}_K$-module (Lenstra 1996, recalled), $\omega$ acting as the GLV scalar $\lambda$; likewise $\mathrm{Cl}(\mathbb{Z}+N^2\mathcal{O}_K)$ has order $N(N-(\frac{-3}{N}))/3$ with an additive $\mathbb{Z}/N$ piece. Both are order-$N$ groups with trivial DLP, but any homomorphism from $\langle P\rangle$ onto them *is* the DL; geometry supplies none. This is the neutrality statement of IDEA-20260727-005/-008. Exponent: 0 if the map were free — it is not. Test: none; cite. [known].

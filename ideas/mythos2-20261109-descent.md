1. **Obstruction versus transfer: the dictionary this angle needs** `[adaptation]`. Mechanism: a *transfer* is a homomorphism out of G = ⟨P⟩ ⊂ E(F_p) into something with a cheaper DLP (pairing, p-adic log, cover, isogeny); an *obstruction* is a cohomology class (Sha, Brauer–Manin, a torsor with no point) that prevents a transfer from being defined or surjective. Every cohomological object attached to a prime-order E/F_p falls in exactly one of three bins: (a) H^1(F_p, E) = 0 (Lang) — no torsors under E at all; (b) H^1(F_p, E[n]) ≅ E(F_p)/n, zero for gcd(n,N)=1 and ≅ Z/N for n=N, whose realisation is the Tate pairing (KR-RHO-53c89f); (c) classes over a global lift, invisible at p by definition of Sha. Reduction: none; this is a classification. Exponent: 1/2 unchanged. Strongest obstruction: Lang + local triviality. Test: the dictionary predicts that the transfer-lane record *Transfer-admissibility certificate* and the port-lane *Exit-dimension theorem* record need one extra clause (cohomological exits are either trivial or pairings); the next agent should read those two records and confirm no clause already covers H^1-valued exits. Separation achieved: leads 2–3 are transfers (closed), leads 4, 8, 14 are obstructions (zero bits).

The N-covering of class δ(Q) is π_Q: R ↦ [N]R + Q (constant on F_p-points); the finite torsor T_Q = [N]^{-1}(Q) has no F_p-point, and ECDLP is "[T_Q] = k[T_P] in H^1(F_p, E[N])". Reduction: to linear algebra in E[N] ≅ F_N² (project to the Frob-eigenvalue-1 line along the eigenvalue-p line), which needs N-division points in F_{p^k}, k = ord_N(p). Exponent: ≥ k ≈ N^1 versus 1/2; the eigenline projection *is* the Tate pairing (KR-RHO-53c89f).

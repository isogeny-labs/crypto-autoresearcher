## LEADS

1. **Isogeny descent by φ = π − 1 on the canonical CM lift over K = Q(√−3)** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: for E: y² = x³ + b over F_p with prime order N and Frobenius π ∈ Z[ω], the lift Ẽ: y² = x³ + b̃ over K carries φ = π − 1 ∈ End_K(Ẽ) of degree Nm(π−1) = N, the only degree-N descent any lift of secp256k1 admits (no rational N-isogeny over Q). The Kummer sequence gives Sel^φ(Ẽ/K) ⊂ H¹(K, Ẽ[𝔫]), 𝔫 = (π−1). At 𝔭 = (π), K_𝔭 = Q_p and φ is bijective on the formal group Ẽ₁(Q_p) (π acts there through the non-unit embedding, so π−1 is a unit) and zero on E(F_p): the local image is Ẽ(Q_p)/φ ≅ E(F_p), order N, and loc_𝔭 of the class of a global point is its reduction. Reduction: ECDLP becomes "find the F_N-coordinate of a Selmer class restricting to Q", i.e. the compatible-lift problem already filed (p-adic wave, "Compatible-lift advice equals the discrete log"). Cost: the Weber function on Ẽ[𝔫] generates the ray class field K_𝔫 with Gal(K_𝔫/K) ≅ (O_K/𝔫)^*/μ₆, degree (N−1)/6 ≈ 2^253, so writing one Kummer generator costs exponent ≥ 1 vs 1/2. Obstruction: localisation is tautological. Falsification: at p = 13, N = 7 (deg K(Ẽ[𝔫]) ≤ 6) compute Sel^φ and check |Ẽ(Q_p)/φẼ(Q_p)| = 7; any other order kills the mechanism statement. Extends the wave-2 record "Charged cost of rank and Selmer computation" (which costed only 2- and 3-isogeny descents); next agent reads `crypto/RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md` for the explicit π = a + bω and twist sign.

2. **Cassels–Tate and Brauer–Manin are blind at the prime above p** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: for any lift Ẽ/K with good reduction at 𝔭 | p, p ∤ N: |H¹(K_𝔭, Ẽ[N])| = |Ẽ(K_𝔭)[N]|² = N², the Kummer image has order N and is unramified, and unramified classes cup to H²(Ẑ, ·) = 0, so the Kummer image is maximal isotropic and the 𝔭-component of the Cassels–Tate pairing on Sel^N × Sel^N (hence of Manin's Brauer–Manin pairing on N-coverings) vanishes; Cassels' local factor |coker φ|/|ker φ| at 𝔭 equals N/N = 1 for φ = π − 1 of lead 1. Reduction: none; a barrier. Exponent delta 0. Obstruction to the attack idea "use a global obstruction to label points": the only global-to-local datum at 𝔭 is a point of E(F_p). Cheap test: verify |Ẽ(Q_p)/N| = N and |Ẽ(Q_p)[N]| = N on the p = 13 toy curves. Extends wave-1 p-adic lead "Local pairings over Q_p are trivial unless N | p − 1" and wave-2 "Local Tate duality at Q_p".

Reduction: shows concretely that "what Sha buys" is a local point you cannot relate to any other point: its discrete log is undefined globally. Exponent delta 0. 4. **The Lang torsor of Q is the cyclic group Z/N² inside E(F_{p^N}), with Frobenius step (p−1)⁻¹·Q** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: for R with NR = Q, c = (π−1)R satisfies (π̂−1)c = Q; on L₁ = E(F_p) the dual acts as p, so the L₁-part of c is (p−1)⁻¹Q (inverse mod N) and R can be normalised to have zero L_p-part, exactly the roots rational over F_{p^N}: π(R) = R + (p−1)⁻¹Q. Then v_𝔫(π^N − 1) = 2 and v_𝔫̄(π^N − 1) = 0 (needs ord_N(p) ∤ N, always true), so E(F_{p^N})[N^∞] ≅ Z/N² generated by any N-th root of P, and the N-th roots of Q are the k'·R_P with k' ≡ k (mod N). Reduction: ECDLP lifts to a DL in Z/N², which Pohlig–Hellman (`cryptanalysis/include/ca_pohlig.h`) splits into two Z/N problems; zeta functions of the root schemes [N]⁻¹(Q) are independent of Q ≠ O. Cost: representing one element of F_{p^N} already costs N log p, exponent ≥ 1 memory. Falsification: at p = 13, N = 7 (Frobenius step predicted 3Q) or N = 19, observe a different step or non-cyclic N-part. Extends the torsion-wave lead "Lang torsor is defined over F_{p^N} exactly"; toy primes from `crypto/docs/ECDLP_CALIBRATION_PRIMES.md`.

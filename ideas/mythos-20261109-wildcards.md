## LEADS

1. **Coppersmith small-root region vs decomposition-existence region for interval factor bases over F_p** — Import: Coppersmith / Jochemsz–May lattice root finding. Reduction (exact): decomposition of R over F_X = {P : 0 ≤ x(P) < X} at arity m is one modular equation S_{m+1}(x_1,…,x_m, x_R) ≡ 0 (mod p) with roots in a box, degree δ = 2^{m−1} per variable, full-box Newton polytope (IDEA-20260723-006). ECDLP → p^{1−γ} re-randomised lattice reductions per relation, B = p^{γ/m} relations, where X^m ≤ p^γ is the lattice region. Derived here: basic-strategy γ = 2/((m+1)δ) = 1/3, 1/8, 1/20, 1/48 for m = 2..5; charged exponent E(m,γ) = max(1 − γ(m−1)/m, 2γ/m) = 11/12 at m = 3 against rho's 1/2; beating rho needs γ > m/(2(m−1)) (3/4 at m = 3), impossible at m = 2 for any γ ≤ 1. 256-bit: lattice dimension (tδ+1)^3 ≥ 125, total p^{0.92}. Obstruction: the lattice region is fixed by degree, the existence region by p; the gap is paid per relation. One-day test: symbolic exponent sums for t = 1..6, fpylll on S_3 at p ≈ 2^40 inside/outside the predicted box. Artifact: RESEARCH_LLL_GS_ANALYSIS.md (check whether a Coppersmith bound for S_3 was already computed). `[adaptation]` → RECORD A.

At ECC2K-130 size: 2^{120} vs 2^{60.9} (KR-RHO-18cc42). Obstruction: same region gap; the lattice part becomes rigorous but lands far above the heuristic Gröbner/SAT descent (KR-IC-5765d2, KR-IC-1fcdbc) that works outside the region. 7. **Elliptic AG code: decomposition ⟺ weight-m codeword in the dual code; ISD imports** — Import: ISD (Prange, Stern/Dumer, BJMM); Cheng 2008 (minimum distance of elliptic codes is subset-sum-hard in E(F_q), recalled). Reduction (exact): ΣP_i = R ⟺ some f ∈ L((m+1)O) with f(−R) = 0 vanishes on {P_i} ⟺ the m×m minor of the m×B evaluation matrix G on those columns is singular ⟺ a weight-m word in ker G, the [B, B−m] dual elliptic code. This is the zero-minor object of IDEA-20260723-002. Prange = naive search (p/B trials × B^{m−1}); Stern/Dumer = half-arity claw (IDEA-20260731-007); BJMM needs a partial-syndrome filter, but the dependency coefficients λ ∈ (F_p^*)^m are fixed only by the whole support, so splitting the support destroys the collision — the only collision structure is the group law, and Z/N has no quotient. 256-bit: G is 3 × 2^{85}. Exponent ≥ 1/2 (floor of IDEA-20260727-003). One-day test: verify the equivalence at p ≈ 2^16 and count the (p−1)^{m/2} coefficient blow-up of a BJMM split. Cf. KR-RHO-5b1c0a. `[known]`

9. **3SUM / three collinear points and the singular-cubic family** — Import: Gajentaan–Overmars (3SUM ≡ 3 collinear points on y = x³) and the fine-grained n^{2−o(1)} barrier.

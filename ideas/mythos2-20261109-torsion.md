## LEADS

1. **E[N]-basis coordinates are the MOV map, with the second basis vector over F_{p^{k_N}}.** Mechanism: Frobenius π has char. poly (x−1)(x−p) mod N, so E[N] = L_1 ⊕ L_p with L_1 = E(F_p) = ker(π−1) and L_p = ker(π̂−1); P extends to a basis only with R ∈ L_p, defined over F_{p^{k_N}}, k_N = ord_N(p). The coordinate functional Q ↦ (coefficient of P) is e_N(·,R) followed by a log in μ_N ⊂ F_{p^{k_N}}^*. Reduction: ECDLP → DLP in F_{p^{k_N}}^*, L(1/3) in p^{k_N}, far above exponent 1/2 unless k_N is tiny. Obstruction: for secp256k1 the embedding degree is expected to be ≈ N/d for a small divisor d of N−1. Falsification test: factor N−1 (ECM, minutes) and compute k_N; k_N < 2^64 would reopen the lane. Extends the wave-1 transfer record "Transfer-admissibility certificate" and the port record "Exit-dimension theorem"; `crypto/RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md` / `secp256k1_cm_audit` should be read for the recorded trace and class-number data. `[known]` KR-RHO-53c89f.

2. **Level-ℓ Galois data (ℓ ∤ N) is discrete-log blind: the Schoof asymmetry.** Mechanism: Schoof reads t mod ℓ off E[ℓ] because π is given as a rational map on all of E; [k] is given only by its value on one cyclic subgroup, and k mod ℓ is not even a function of (E,P,Q) (k and k+N give the same Q, CRT on Z/Nℓ). Hence ρ_ℓ, X_0(ℓ)(F_p), Kummer/ℓ-descent, Tate-module coordinates carry zero information about k; torsion at level ℓ re-enters only through ℓ-isogenies, which preserve k. Exponent: no reduction possible, delta 0 by construction. Test: trivial toy enumeration (included in Record A). `[known]` — the port-wave lead "mod-ℓ Galois representations; ℓ-descent is vacuous" states the conclusion; this gives the one-line proof.

3. **ψ_N factorisation over F_p is the eigenline structure made polynomial.** Mechanism: [N] = (π−1)(π̂−1) in End(E), so ψ_N = h_1·h_p·g with h_1 = ∏_{χ(x³+ax+b)=1}(x−x) = gcd(ψ_N, x^p−x) (degree (N−1)/2, splits), h_p (degree (N−1)/2, irreducibles of degree k' = order of p in (Z/N)^*/±1), g (degree (N−1)²/2, exactly (N−1)²/(2k_N) irreducibles of degree k_N). Reduction: none; it is a certificate that every x-coordinate outside E(F_p) ∪ L_p lives in degree exactly k_N. Test: factor ψ_N at p ≤ 60 (degree ≤ 1800) and compare multisets. `[adaptation]` → Record A.

4. **X_0(N)(F_p) over j(E) is exactly two points, both with quotient ≅ E, swapped by w_N.** Mechanism: stable cyclic subgroups = eigenlines; E/L_1 ≅ E via π−1, E/L_p ≅ E via π̂−1; w_N(E,L_1) = (E,(π−1)L_p) = (E,L_p). Hence Φ_N(j(E),X) mod p has X = j(E) as a root of multiplicity exactly 2 (elements of norm N in End(E) are u(π−1), u(π̄−1)). Reduction: none. Obstruction: the point (E,L_1) ∈ X_0(N) forgets P and is k-blind. Test: PARI `polmodular(N)` at toy N ∈ {5,7,11,13}, p ≤ 40. `[adaptation]` → Record D.

5. **The X_1(N) fibre is a Frobenius-trivial (Z/N)^*/±1-torsor; the modular view adds exactly |Aut(E)|/2.** Mechanism: (E,P),(E,Q) are F_p-points of X_1(N) in one fibre over (E,L_1); diamond operators ⟨a⟩ act simply transitively; Frobenius fixes every point (all kP rational), so no Galois symmetry exists to quotient by; the only extra structure is Aut(E) = μ_6 ⊂ diamonds, i.e. the √6 of KR-RHO-0528e7. General barrier: any object built from (E,L_1) or symmetric in E(F_p) (ψ_N, h_1, X_0(N) points) is invariant under all ⟨a⟩ and therefore constant in k. Exponent delta 0. Test: toy orbit count of ⟨λ⟩ on x-values (size 3). `[known]` IDEA-20260806-0c9de1, KR-RHO-0528e7 → folded into Record D.

6. **Interpolation degree of the scalar graph Γ_k = {(x(P),x(kP))}.** Mechanism: Γ_k has (N−1)/2 points; the scalar polynomial C_{k'}(X,Y) = Yψ_{k'}(X)² − φ_{k'}(X) (total degree k'²) vanishes on Γ_k for k' ≡ ±k, and the reflected/μ_6-twisted versions for ±k^{-1}, ±kλ^j. Let k* be the least such representative. Bezout: any curve of degree d through Γ_k either contains C_{k*} (d ≥ k*²) or satisfies d ≥ (N−1)/(2k*²); generic interpolation gives d_gen ≈ √N. So D_k = k*² exactly when k*⁴ ≤ (N−1)/2, and (prediction) D_k = d_gen otherwise. Reduction: recovering k from the algebraic structure of its graph works only for k* ≤ N^{1/4}, the brute-force range. Test: rank of the monomial evaluation matrix at p ≈ 2^{12}–2^{14}. `[adaptation]` (adjacent to the wildcards "EDS and algebraic complexity lower bounds" lead) → Record B.

7. **Static-key sample reconstruction versus Cheon.** Mechanism: m pairs (P_i, kP_i) with one secret k support a degree-d interpolant only for m ≳ d²/2; by lead 6 the fitted curve identifies k only if k*² ≤ d, i.e. k* ≤ (2m)^{1/4}. Reduction: none beyond exhaustive small-k search. Contrast: Cheon (`cryptanalysis/include/ca_cheon.h`) genuinely beats √N but needs [k^d]P, i.e. a smaller auxiliary problem the plain static setting does not supply. Test: same matrix as lead 6 on random subsamples. `[known]` KR-IC-081a58, port-wave "Pohlig–Hellman on the scalar torsor".

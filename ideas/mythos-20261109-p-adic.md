## LEADS

**Notation used below.** $E/\mathbb F_p$, $p\approx 2^{256}$, $N=\#E(\mathbb F_p)$ prime with $p\nmid N$ (secp256k1: $p=2^{256}-2^{32}-977$, trace $t=p+1-N\approx 1.27\cdot 2^{128}$, so $N\ne p$). $\tilde E/\mathbb Z_p$ is a lift; $\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)\supset \tilde E_1\supset \tilde E_2\supset\cdots$ with $\tilde E_r=\{v_p(t)\ge r\}$, $t=-x/y$; $\psi:\tilde E_1\to p\mathbb Z_p$ the formal logarithm (an isomorphism for $p>2$); $\lambda=\psi\circ[N]/N$ the $p$-adic elliptic log on $\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)$, kernel = torsion. Structure fact used by every lead: $\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)\cong \mathbb Z_p\oplus\mathbb Z/N$, so reduction has a unique **homomorphic** section $s:E(\mathbb F_p)\xrightarrow{\sim}\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)[N]$, and $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb Z/N,\ \text{any }\mathbb Z_p\text{-module})=0$. The Smart/Satoh–Araki/Semaev attack is the degenerate case $N=p$, where $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb Z/p,\mathbb F_p)\ne0$. None of the files RESEARCH_EDS_RESIDUE.md, RESEARCH_MESTRE_HOWE.md, RESEARCH_EXOTIC_COORDINATES.md is quoted in the brief; where a lead touches one, it says what the next agent must read there.

1. **Trace-one $p$-adic attack and the exact gate it fails at.** Lift: any Weierstrass lift, points lifted by keeping $x$ and Hensel-lifting $y$ to $\mathbb Z/p^2$. Precision: $p^2$ (two digits; a 512-bit modulus at 256 bits). Log: $\psi(t)=t+\tfrac{2A}{5}t^5+\tfrac{3B}{7}t^7+\dots$, so to precision $p^2$ the log *is* $t$; $D(P):=t([p]\tilde P)/p \bmod p$ costs $\approx1.5\log_2p\approx384$ group ops mod $p^2$. DL: $k=D(Q)/D(P)\bmod p$ — polynomial, versus $2^{128}$ for rho. Why only when $N=p$: $[p]\tilde E_1\subset\tilde E_2$ kills the lift ambiguity $\tilde Q-k\tilde P\in\tilde E_1$. For secp256k1, $[N]$ is an automorphism of $\tilde E_1$, so $t([N](\tilde Q-k\tilde P))$ has valuation exactly 1 and is uniform over the $p$ lifts; structurally, $\mathrm{Hom}(\mathbb Z/N,\mathbb Z_p)=0$. The obstruction is not precision: the homomorphism does not exist. Test: CTRL-ANOMALOUS-TRACE1 (IDEA-20260805-cc4c2c, bits=20) with $[p]$ replaced by $[N]$ on a non-anomalous curve of the same size; the digit must become lift-dependent (lead 4). `[known]` KR-RHO-53c89f.

2. **Canonical-lift failure is the only precision obstruction of lead 1; the canonical lift is lost at 256 bits except for CM curves.** The hom $E(\mathbb F_p)\to\tilde E_1/\tilde E_2\cong\mathbb F_p$ of lead 1 is the Serre–Tate class $(q(\tilde E)-1)/p\bmod p$; it vanishes exactly for the canonical lift mod $p^2$, where $\tilde E[p]\cong\mu_p\times\mathbb Z/p$ and $\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)$ has a $p$-torsion $\mathbb Z/p$ surjecting onto $E(\mathbb F_p)$, so $[p]\tilde P\in\tilde E_2$ for every point and every point-lift (Smart's remark made exact). At $j=0$ the lifts $y^2=x^3+pcx+b$ run through the $p$ deformation classes: prediction fails iff $c\equiv0$, and $D_c=c\,D_1$ (record 4). For a non-CM curve at $p\approx2^{256}$ the canonical lift mod $p^2$ needs $\Phi_p(j,j')=0$ or a degree-$p$ isogeny — Satoh/Mestre-AGM is poly($p$), fine at $p\le2^{10}$, hopeless at $2^{256}$; for secp256k1 it is free ($y^2=x^3+7$ has CM by $\mathbb Z[\omega]$). ECDLP content: none; $p$-adic lifts compute $\#E$ (Satoh, Mestre, Kedlaya), not logs. Artifacts: RESEARCH_MESTRE_HOWE.md — read for whether canonical-lift/AGM computations were run and at what $p$; RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md and `secp256k1_cm_audit` for the CM constants. `[known]` KR-RHO-53c89f; "canonical lift for ECDLP" is a forecloses phrase of KR-IC-cd159a.

3. **Formal-group additivity radius: linearity on a small ball, quantified.** For $R\in\tilde E_1$ with $v(t(R))=r$ and $p\nmid m$: $t([m]R)-m\,t(R)=\tfrac{2A}{5}(m-m^5)t^5+\tfrac{3B}{7}(m-m^7)t^7+O(t^9)$, a formal identity. So $t$ is additive to valuation exactly $5r$ ($A\ne0$, $m^4\not\equiv1$) or exactly $7r$ ($A=0$, i.e. secp256k1 shape, $m^6\not\equiv1$), with jumps when $m$ is a 4th/6th root of unity mod $p$ (the $j=0$ automorphism $t\mapsto\zeta_6 t$ is exactly linear). This is the whole ball of linearity: inside the fibre, and never across fibres. Cost: nothing. Test: record 3 (precision $p^{10}$, 20-bit $p$). Artifact: RESEARCH_EXOTIC_COORDINATES.md — read to see whether $t=-x/y$ or Witt coordinates were catalogued; the lead extends that catalogue with an exact precision statement. `[adaptation]` of textbook formal-group theory; KR-RHO-53c89f adjacent.

5. **Torsion-section (Teichmüller-type) lift via the lifted CM Frobenius — the variant that survives precision analysis.** secp256k1: $\tilde E:y^2=x^3+7/\mathbb Z_p$ is the canonical lift; Frobenius $\pi=a+b\omega$, $a^2-ab+b^2=p$, $a,b\approx2^{128}$ from Cornacchia on $4p=t^2+3b^2$, sign fixed by $[a]P+[b]\omega(P)=P$ on $E(\mathbb F_p)$ (equivalently $a+b\beta\equiv0\bmod p$ for the Hensel-lifted cube root $\beta$). $\Phi=[a]+[b]\omega$ acts as the identity on $\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)[N]$ and as $[a+b\beta]$ ($v_p=1$) on $\tilde E_1$, so $\Phi^{j-1}(\tilde P)\equiv s(P)\bmod p^j$ for any lift $\tilde P$: one digit per step, $\approx400$ group ops mod $p^j$ (two 129-bit scalar mults plus one multiplication by $\beta$); the generic route $[e_j]\tilde P$, $e_j\equiv1\ (N)$, $\equiv0\ (p^j)$, costs $\approx384(j+1)$. Exact: $s(kP)=[k]s(P)$, so ECDLP $\cong$ DLP in $\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)[N]$. Precision: linear in $j$, no heights. DL extraction: none — every homomorphism to a $\mathbb Z_p$-module kills $s(E(\mathbb F_p))$; the extra digits are a deterministic jet of $kP$ (KN-FIND-002 closes jet/endomorphism oracles as GGM-simulable, as quoted in IDEA-20260806-530fdd). Exponent delta 0; falsified-as-barrier only if lead 4/11 find structure. Record 1. Artifacts: RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md, `secp256k1_cm_audit`, the GOAL-ENDO-001 lane (brief §1, commit "endomorphism goal routing"). `[adaptation]` of Satoh's Frobenius lift / Teichmüller fixed points.

6. **Frobenius-difference log $\mu=\psi\circ(\Phi-1)$ (Buium's order-1 $\delta$-character, recalled).** On the canonical lift $\mu:\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p)\to p\mathbb Z_p$, $\tilde P\mapsto\psi(\Phi\tilde P-\tilde P)$, is a homomorphism equal to $(a+b\beta-1)$ times the $\mathbb Z_p$-projection; kernel $\supseteq s(E(\mathbb F_p))$. 7. **Global lifts, xedni, Huang–Kueh–Tan: the height obstruction in numbers.** A lift $\tilde P\in\tilde E(\mathbb Q)$ with coordinates $\approx p$ has $\hat h(\tilde P)\approx\log p\approx177$ nats; $\tilde Q=k\tilde P$ exactly needs $\approx k^2\hat h\approx2^{510}\cdot256$ bits; the EDS over $\mathbb Q$ grows as $\log|W_n(\tilde P)|\approx n^2\hat h$, so $W_N$ has $\approx2^{520}$ bits ("growth of the EDS"). Completing at $p$ removes growth ($\mathbb Z_p$ is compact) but removes the homomorphism (lead 5). Reduction: ECDLP → finding a rank-deficient / height-small lift; JKSST show success probability negligible. `[known]` KR-IC-cd159a.

9. **EDS periodicity constants versus the Lauter–Stange association term.** For $R$ of order $N$, $W_R(n+N)=a\,b^nW_R(n)$ with $b^2=-W_R(N+1)/W_R(N-1)$ (Ward; Stange), computable in $O(\log N)$ net steps. Then $b(kR)\,W_R(k)^{2N}=b(R)^{k^2}$ exactly — in $\mathbb F_p$, and in $\mathbb Z_p$ for $R=s(P)$. Taking $\log_p$ on $1+p\mathbb Z_p$: $\log\langle b(kR)\rangle+2N\log\langle W_R(k)\rangle=k^2\log\langle b(R)\rangle$; taking $\mathbb F_p^\times$ dlogs (feasible at 256 bits, $L(1/3)$): the same congruence mod $p-1$. Both are one equation in two unknowns $(k,W_R(k))$, and $W_R(k)$ given $kR$ is EDS Association, equivalent to ECDLP up to subexponential factors (Lauter–Stange 2008). Reduction: ECDLP ⇄ EDS Association, delta 0. Test: verify the identity at toys over $\mathbb F_p$ and $\mathbb Z_p$; predict every $k\in\mathbb Z/N$ admits a $w$ solving the $p$-adic equation mod $p^2$ (the lift adds no constraint). Artifact: RESEARCH_EDS_RESIDUE.md — check whether the periodicity constants are recorded there. `[adaptation]`.

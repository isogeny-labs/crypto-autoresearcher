1. **Canonical birational maps with a pinned sign and their exceptional loci.** `[known]` (RFC 7748 §4.1; what is new here is only the pinning and the chart analysis.) Montgomery $v^2=u^3+Au^2+u$ $\to$ Edwards $-x^2+y^2=1+dx^2y^2$: $y=\dfrac{u-1}{u+1}$, $x=\dfrac{c\,u}{v}$; inverse $u=\dfrac{1+y}{1-y}$, $v=\dfrac{c\,u}{x}=\dfrac{c(1+y)}{(1-y)\,x}$. Sign convention: $c:=9^{-1}\,x_B\,v_B \bmod p$ with $x_B$ the even root in `base_x()` and $v_B$ the RFC 7748 base $v$; then $c^2=-486664$ and $(9,v_B)\mapsto(x_B,4/5)$ exactly. Affine cost $1I+2M+1c$ each way. Exceptional loci over $F_p$: $v=0$ only at $(0,0)$ (the other two $2$-torsion points need $\sqrt{A^2-4}$, a non-residue); $u=-1$ never (needs $\sqrt{A-2}$, non-residue: $(-1,\cdot)$ lies on the twist); Edwards side $x=0$ at $(0,\pm1)$, $y=1$ at $(0,1)$. Extensions by the group law: $\infty\leftrightarrow(0,1)$, $(0,0)\leftrightarrow(0,-1)$. Projective charts: u-line $(X_M\!:\!Z_M)=(Z+Y\,:\,Z-Y)$ ($2a$, total on all of $E(F_p)$, identity $\mapsto(2Z\!:\!0)=\infty$, $(0,-1)\mapsto(0\!:\!2Z)$). Full chart $E\to M$: $(U\!:\!V\!:\!W)=(X(Z+Y)\,:\,cZ(Z+Y)\,:\,X(Z-Y))$, $3M+1c$, fails exactly at $(0\!:\!-1\!:\!1)\mapsto(0\!:\!0\!:\!0)$; second chart $(-X^2(Z^2+dY^2)\,:\,-cXZ(Z^2+dY^2)\,:\,Z^2(Z-Y)^2)$ fails exactly at the identity; the two are combined by a constant-time select on $Y+Z=0$. Chart $M\to E$ extended: with $q=U^2+AUW+W^2$, $m=VW$: $(X\!:\!Y\!:\!Z\!:\!T)=(c\,m(U+W)\,:\,(U-W)q\,:\,(U+W)q\,:\,c\,m(U-W))$, $6M+2S+2c$, exception-free on $M(F_p)\setminus\{\infty\}$ ($q=0$ and $U+W=0$ are non-rational); $\infty\mapsto(0\!:\!1\!:\!1\!:\!0)$ by one select on $W=0$. 3. **The Montgomery ladder is the Edwards addition law on the $y$-line.** `[adaptation]` (Castryck–Galbraith–Farashahi 2008 give $y$-only Edwards formulas; the identification below with the RFC 7748 step and the identity-output property are the content.) Substituting $X_M=Z+Y$, $Z_M=Z-Y$ into the RFC step gives the $(Y\!:\!Z)$ differential addition with difference $y_1$, $u_1=(1+y_1)/(1-y_1)$:
 $S=Y_2Z_3+Z_2Y_3,\ D=Y_2Z_3-Z_2Y_3,\ Y_5=S^2-u_1D^2,\ Z_5=S^2+u_1D^2$ ($3M+2S$), i.e. affine $y_5=\dfrac{2y_2y_3-y_1(y_2^2+y_3^2)}{(y_2^2+y_3^2)-2y_1y_2y_3}$; doubling $Y_4=Y^2Z^2-(Z^2-Y^2)\big(Y^2+a_{24}(Z^2-Y^2)\big)$, $Z_4=Y^2Z^2+(Z^2-Y^2)\big(Y^2+a_{24}(Z^2-Y^2)\big)$ ($2M+2S+1c$). Step $5M+4S+1c$, identical to RFC. Derivation from the Edwards law: with $m=x_2x_3$, $n=y_2y_3$, $y(P\pm Q)=\frac{n\pm m}{1\mp dmn}$, so $y(P{+}Q)+y(P{-}Q)=\frac{2n(1+dm^2)}{1-d^2m^2n^2}$ and $y(P{+}Q)\,y(P{-}Q)=\frac{n^2-m^2}{1-d^2m^2n^2}$ depend on $m^2=\frac{(y_2^2-1)(y_3^2-1)}{(1+dy_2^2)(1+dy_3^2)}$ only — the $x\mapsto -x$ quotient. Completeness: inherits the Montgomery ladder's set exactly (base $y_1=-1$, i.e. $u_1=0$, produces $(0\!:\!0)$ states); but a Montgomery output $(X\!:\!0)$, $X\neq0$ maps to $(X\!:\!X)$, $y=1$ — the genuine identity — so clamped multiples of the order-4/8 points come out as $y=1$ with no $0^{p-2}$ convention. Symmetry: $y\mapsto$ Kummer quotient by $[-1]$. Surface: `src/ecc/ed25519.rs` new `ladder_y`; RECORD D.

4. **Kummer obstruction: no coordinate change turns a differential addition into a full Edwards addition.** `[adaptation]` A differential addition is a morphism $\kappa(E)\times\kappa(E)\times\kappa(E)\to\kappa(E)$ on the Kummer line $\kappa:E\to E/\langle-1\rangle\cong\mathbb P^1$. Full addition would need a morphism $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1\to\mathbb P^1$ with $\kappa(P+Q)=f(\kappa P,\kappa Q)$; but $\kappa(P+Q)\neq\kappa(P-Q)$ for generic $P,Q$ while both pairs have the same $\kappa$-images, so $f$ is not well defined. Any coordinate change is an isomorphism of $\mathbb P^1$ and cannot repair this. What is recoverable is exactly one more bit of data: the third input $\kappa(P-Q)$ (Lead 3) or one $v$/$x$ (Lead 5). Surface: proof note in RECORD D; no code.

With $P=(u_1,v_1)$ affine, $(X_2\!:\!Z_2)=[k]P$, $(X_3\!:\!Z_3)=[k{+}1]P$ (the repo's `x2,z2,x3,z3` after the final swap): $v_2=\dfrac{(u_1u_2+1)(u_1+u_2+2A)-2A-(u_1-u_2)^2u_3}{2v_1}$. Projective sequence: $t_1=u_1Z_2$, $t_2=u_1X_2$, $s_1=(X_2+t_1)(Z_2+t_2)$, $s_2=(X_2-t_1)^2$, $z_{23}=Z_2Z_3$, $N=s_1Z_3+(2A\,t_2)\,z_{23}-s_2X_3$, $\lambda=2v_1z_{23}$, output $(\lambda X_2:N:\lambda Z_2)$: $10M+1S+1c$. 6. **The explicit 4-isogeny $\psi:E_{-1,d}\to E_{1,\,d+1}$, $d+1=1/121666$.** `[adaptation]` (Hamburg's Decaf/Goldilocks isogeny $E_{a,d}\to E_{-a,d-a}$ instantiated and re-derived.) $\psi(x,y)=\Big(\dfrac{2xy}{x^2+y^2},\ \dfrac{y^2-x^2}{2+x^2-y^2}\Big)=\Big(\dfrac{2xy}{x^2+y^2},\ \dfrac{1+dx^2y^2}{1-dx^2y^2}\Big)$. Projective: $A_1=X^2,B_1=Y^2,C_1=Z^2,E_1=(X+Y)^2-A_1-B_1,G_1=A_1+B_1,H_1=B_1-A_1,F_1=2C_1-H_1$; $(E_1F_1:H_1G_1:G_1F_1)$, $3M+4S$, no constant multiplies. Kernel $\{(0,1),(i,0),(0,-1),(-i,0)\}$ (rational $E[4]$), all regular in the formula. Codomain $x^2+y^2=1+\frac{1}{121666}x^2y^2$ is incomplete ($a'=1$ and $d'$ both squares; four rational points at infinity). Exceptional locus: exactly the four order-8 points ($x^2+y^2=0$), sent to the order-2 points at infinity $(\infty,\pm\sqrt{121666})$; on $2E(F_p)\cup$ prime-order subgroup the affine formula is total. Montgomery form: $u\mapsto\dfrac{(u+1)^2(u^2+Au+1)}{(2-A)\,u(u-1)^2}$ onto $(2-A)w^2=z^3+\frac{2(A+6)}{A-2}z^2+z$ ($B'$ a non-residue). Dual $\hat\psi(x',y')=\Big(\dfrac{2x'y'}{y'^2-x'^2},\dfrac{y'^2+x'^2}{2-x'^2-y'^2}\Big)$, $\hat\psi\circ\psi=\pm[4]$ (sign fixed by script). RECORD B.

7. **$\psi$ and $[2]$ share every squaring; isogeny quadrupling buys nothing.** `[speculative]` With HCD names ($E=2XY,\ G=B-A,\ H=-A-B,\ F=G-2Z^2$): $[2]P=(EF:GH:FG)$ and $\psi(P)=(-EF:-GH:HF)$, so the pair costs $4M+4S$ instead of $6M+8S$. 8. **Torsion and base-point correspondence; a $2S$ small-order test.** `[adaptation]` $u=9\Rightarrow y=\frac{9-1}{9+1}=\frac45$ exactly; $x_B=c\cdot9/v_B$.

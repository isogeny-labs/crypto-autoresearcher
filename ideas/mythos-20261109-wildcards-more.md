# Mythos ECDLP ideation — wildcards (20261109)

Repository brief: `cryptanalysis/ecdlp-ideas/repo-brief.md` at the commits named in it. Records filed: see the `IDEA-*` ids in this PR.

## LEADS

Wave-1 wildcards imported solvers (SMT, CDCL, ISD, Coppersmith), proof complexity *over the native field* (Polynomial Calculus), tensor rank, fine-grained reductions, TFNP, theta groups and étale cohomology. It did not import (a) the exact-algebra pseudorandomness literature on the DL *function*, (b) cross-characteristic proof systems that govern the LP/MILP arm already run in `cryptanalysis/experiments`, (c) SAT as a *counter* rather than a solver, (d) symmetric-key algebraic attacks, or (e) zero-sum theorems as a non-Fourier existence route. The five leads below fill exactly those gaps. All five have exponent delta 0 against KR-RHO-13bf67; they are barriers and discriminating measurements, not attacks.

1. **Exact interpolation degree and sparsity of the DL function on structured factor bases (the missing frontier row).** `[adaptation]` Forced by: sweep's "Learned predictor of k-bits from x(kP)" treated statistical learning only; no §3 row records the exact algebraic version. Mechanism: for a set $S$ of $s$ x-coordinates of $E(\mathbb F_p)$ let $\ell(x)=\min(k,N-k)$ where $x=x(kP)$; $d(S)$ is the least degree of $f\in\mathbb F_p[X]$ with $f=\ell$ on $S$ (computed exactly by Newton divided differences in $O(s^2)$), and $w$ is the monomial weight of the unique degree-$<p$ interpolant of $\ell$ extended by $0$ off $x(E)$. Lange–Winterhof (2002/05, recalled) and Coppersmith–Shparlinski (2000, recalled; $\mathbb F_p^*$ case) prove degree and weight lower bounds that are near-maximal on large sets; what no record states is whether *structured* bases (x-interval, $\mu_B$ with $x^B=1$, $\lambda$-closed on $j=0$) are exceptions. Reduces ECDLP to: nothing; it forecloses "fit a polynomial/sparse model of the log on the factor base, then descend". Cost exponent needed vs best: a polynomial model useful for descent would need $d(S)\le s^{1-\epsilon}$; prediction $d(S)\ge s-2$, delta 0 vs $1/2$. Obstruction: dimension count makes $d(S)=s-1$ generic; only a theorem for structured $S$ is missing, not a phenomenon. Cheap test: calibration primes from `crypto/docs/ECDLP_CALIBRATION_PRIMES.md`, $p\le2^{14}$, $S_{\rm int},S_{\mu},S_{\rm rand}$ of equal size; killed if any structured $d(S)\le0.9(s-1)$ while the random control stays $\ge s-2$. Extends IDEA-20260805-c4f675 (H-PSEUDO character-sum norm of the small-x base) from Fourier to algebraic degree. → Record 1.

2. **Cross-characteristic proof-degree barrier for bit-blasted $\mathbb F_p$ PDP (Sherali–Adams/SoS over $\mathbb R$, PC over $\mathbb F_2$).** `[adaptation]` Forced by: wave-1 "Polynomial-Calculus refutation degree of descended PDP systems" measured PC *in the native characteristic*; the repo's MILP arm `cryptanalysis/experiments/g2-n31-four-add-width2-milp-r7-20261010` and `...width3-milp-r8-20261010` live in a different characteristic (LP over $\mathbb R$) with no barrier stated. Mechanism: any bit-blasted encoding of $a\cdot b\equiv c\pmod p$ over 0/1 variables is a system of modular counting constraints; Grigoriev (2001) and Buss–Grigoriev–Impagliazzo–Pitassi (2001) prove $\Omega(n)$ degree for SoS/Positivstellensatz and for PC over $\mathbb F_q$, $q\ne p$, on mod-$p$ counting principles. Hence LP-hierarchy/MILP refutation of "no decomposition of $R$ in the box" needs level linear in $\log_2 p$ regardless of factor-base structure: node counts $2^{\Omega(\log p)}$ i.e. $p^{c}$, which for the PDP sub-instance is already worse than univariate enumeration ($2^{m-1}$ root checks per tuple). Reduces ECDLP to: nothing; it closes the LP/MILP/CP-SAT family as an exponent lever and reclassifies the `g2-n31` MILP results as expected. Needed vs best: a sub-$1/2$ route would need bounded SA level; delta 0. Obstruction: the transfer from counting principles to the PDP instance is heuristic (the instance is not a Tseitin formula), so it must be measured. Cheap test: minimal SA level $\ell^*$ refuting gadget-only UNSAT instances of width $n=6..14$; killed if $\ell^*$ is constant in $n$. → Record 2.

3. **Hash-based approximate model counting (ApproxMC-style XOR hashing) of per-target decomposition counts beyond enumerable $p$.** `[adaptation]` Forced by: wave-1 "QF_BV SMT scaling exponent" and "Bit-blasted SAT … matched kill test" used SAT to *find* one decomposition; barriers' "Incidence barrier: no hub targets" is a theorem only at $m=2$ and synthesis' "decomposition-count histograms" stop at the exhaustive-log-table range ($p\le2^{26}$). Mechanism: on the bit-blasted CNF of `RESEARCH_SAT_SEMAEV.md` / the Stage-26 CaDiCaL–CryptoMiniSat pipeline, add $t$ random XOR parity constraints over the $m\log B$ interval bits and solve under assumptions; the smallest $t$ at which the residual instance becomes UNSAT estimates $r_m(R)\approx 2^t$ within $(\varepsilon,\delta)$ (Chakraborty–Meel–Vardi, recalled). Sampling $10^3$ targets at $p\approx2^{32}$–$2^{40}$, $m=3$ gives the tail of $r_m(R)$ against Poisson with exact mean $\mu=\binom{2B+m-1}{m}/N$ (IDEA-20260805-a25f11) where enumeration ($B^m\approx p$) is unaffordable. Reduces ECDLP to: nothing directly; a heavy tail (hub targets with $r_m\ge\mu\,p^{\epsilon}$) is the one observation that would make target-randomisation $Q\mapsto rQ+sP$ an exponent lever. Needed vs best: tail mass $\Pr[r_m\ge\mu p^{\epsilon}]\ge p^{-\epsilon'}$ with $\epsilon'<\epsilon m$; delta 0 expected. Obstruction: XOR-augmented 40-bit modular CNF may exceed solver budgets; CryptoMiniSat's native XOR handling (KR-IC-5765d2) is the only reason it is plausible. Cheap test: run at $p\approx2^{24}$ against the exhaustive histogram; killed as a tool if the hashed estimate disagrees with exact counts by $>2\times$ on $>10\%$ of targets.

4. **Cube attack / higher-order differential on the keyed map $\delta\mapsto\mathrm{LSB}\,x(Q+\delta P)$.** `[adaptation]` Forced by: wave-1 imported no symmetric-key cryptanalysis; barriers' "Hybrid-sum flatness" covers statistical distinguishers but not algebraic superpolys. Mechanism: the attacker *can* evaluate $G_k(\delta)=\mathrm{LSB}(x((k+\delta)P))=\mathrm{LSB}(x(Q+\delta P))$ for chosen public $\delta$, which is exactly the cube-attack setting (Dinur–Shamir, recalled) with secret $k$. A cube $C$ of $c$ bit positions yields the superpoly $p_C(k)=\bigoplus_{\varepsilon\in\{0,1\}^C}G_k(\delta_0+\sum\varepsilon_j2^j)$; an attack needs some $p_C$ linear (or low-degree) in the bits of $k$. The cube sum is a Boolean-bit sum over an arithmetic progression of $2^c$ points; Kohel–Shparlinski/Lange–Shparlinski exponential-sum bounds (recalled) make it balanced only for $2^c\gg\sqrt p\log p$, and for small $c$ the degree of $G_k$ in $\delta$-bits is expected full, so superpolys of small cubes are dense in $k$. Reduces ECDLP to: solving $\approx256$ linear superpolys, which would cost $256\cdot2^c$ group operations if $c=O(\log p)$ cubes were linear — exponent $0$ vs $1/2$, hence almost certainly false. Obstruction: Weil-type flatness plus maximal ANF degree. Cheap test: $p\approx2^{16}$, $\delta<2^{16}$; compute the full ANF of $G_k$ in $\delta$ for 20 random $k$ (degree, monomial count) and BLR-test linearity in $k$ of every superpoly for $c\le10$; killed-as-barrier (i.e. interesting) only if a cube with $c\le12$ passes the linearity test on $\ge90\%$ of 200 $k$-triples. Next agent must read `crypto/src` cryptanalysis registry (`cargo run -- cryptanalysis list-ciphers`) to see whether a cube/HOD attack is already registered that could take the EC map as a "cipher".

5. **Zero-sum and restricted-sumset theorems (Olson; Dias da Silva–Hamidoune) as unconditional, non-Fourier relation-existence certificates at $\beta=1/2$.** `[adaptation]` Forced by: barriers' "Fourier certification barrier" record names character sums as the rigorous supply route and prices it at exponent $\ge3\omega/4$; wave-1 omitted the additive-combinatorics *existence* theorems that need no Fourier input. Mechanism: for $A=\log(F)\subset\mathbb Z/N$, Olson (1968, recalled) gives a nonempty zero-sum subset whenever $|A|\ge\lceil\sqrt{4N-3}\rceil$, and Dias da Silva–Hamidoune (1994, recalled) gives $|h^{\wedge}A|\ge\min(N,h(|A|-h)+1)$, so with $|F|\approx2\sqrt N$ and $h\approx\sqrt N$ *every* target is unconditionally an $h$-fold sum of distinct base points — rigorous supply at $\beta=1/2$ with no heuristic. Reduces ECDLP to: a relation matrix of size $B\approx2\sqrt N$ with row weight up to $h\approx\sqrt N$ plus a subset-sum search per relation; charged cost $\ge B^2h=N^{3/2}$ for linear algebra alone, exponent $3/2$ vs $1/2$. Obstruction: the theorems are tight only for arithmetic progressions, and arity is uncontrolled. Cheap test (information lies in the gap): at $N\le2^{20}$ compute by DP over $\mathbb Z/N$ the least $h^*$ such that every residue is an $h^*$-fold restricted sum of the interval base of size $\lceil\sqrt{4N-3}\rceil$, versus a random set of equal size; prediction $h^*\le4$ for both, far below the theorem's $\sqrt N$; killed-as-uninteresting if $h^*_{\rm int}\le h^*_{\rm rand}+1$ (then the interval base is random-like at $\beta=1/2$, consistent with the sweep "Additive energy" record and IDEA-20260805-a9a95d), interesting if $h^*_{\rm int}\gg h^*_{\rm rand}$ (progression-like logs would be structure).

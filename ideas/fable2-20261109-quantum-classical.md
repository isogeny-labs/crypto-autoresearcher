1. **The one provable non-dequantizability statement, and where it stops** `[known]`. Mechanism: Shor-ECDLP = (reversible group arithmetic) ∘ (QFT over Z_N²) ∘ (Fourier sampling of H^⊥ = {(u,uk)}). Only the arithmetic touches the F_p representation; the Fourier step is generic. Relative to the group oracle any classical dequantization is a generic algorithm and needs Ω(√N) operations (Shoup; KR-RHO-13bf67). In the standard model no proof is possible: algebraic adversaries over F_p have no lower-bound tool (complexity-wave lead). Reduction: none; exponent 1/2 vs 1/2, delta 0. Repo tie: anything written against the generic interface `cryptanalysis/include/ca_group.h` is by construction inside the oracle model and bounded by √N; the next agent should confirm `ca_group.h` exposes no coordinate access. Falsification: exhibit a classical sampler of H^⊥ using only `ca_group.h` calls in o(√N) ops — impossible by the theorem. Already classified in the wave-2 quantum-classical record "Generic-model classification of three quantum ECDLP ingredients".

2. **Entanglement and magic floors of the Shor-ECDLP ladder** `[adaptation]` → RECORD. Mechanism: after j controlled steps of the windowed ladder the state Σ_a |a⟩|a·2^{n−j}P⟩ has Schmidt rank exactly min(2^j, ord P) across the control|point cut (x-only: min(2^j,(N+1)/2)); exact tensor-network/MPS simulation needs bond dimension ≥ (N+1)/2, cost ≥ N (Vidal 2003, recalled); Toffoli count τ ≈ 10^11 at 256 bits (Roetteler–Naehrig–Svore–Lauter 2017, recalled) gives stabilizer-rank simulation cost 2^{Ω(τ)}; the post-QFT path sum has average sign 1/N. Reduction: none; a classical simulation of the circuit costs ≥ N > N^{1/2}. Obstruction: injectivity of a ↦ aP is the whole proof. Test: state-vector Schmidt ranks at p ≤ 2^10 and a gate census of `crypto/quantum_circuit_secp256k1` (directory listed in the brief; contents unread).

3. **Kuperberg labels are generically hard: χ_q(k) costs √(2εN)** `[adaptation]`. Mechanism: the label of the hidden-shift reading (wave-2 QC lead 3) is a power-residue character χ_q of the log k. In the generic model the adversary's view is independent of k unless two linear forms a_i + b_i k collide, probability ≤ T²/2N, so advantage ε on any balanced predicate of k — including χ_q(k) — needs T ≥ √(2εN). Kuperberg obtains a label in 2^{O(√log N)} quantum work: non-dequantizable generically. Contrast: Cheon's auxiliary input [k^d]P (`cryptanalysis/include/ca_cheon.h`, `ca_pohlig.h`) makes the class of k mod q-th powers classical at √(N/q): aux-input is the classical price of a label. Delta 0. Test: five-line Shoup argument; nothing to compute. The standard-model readout is already filed (wave-2 QC record 3).

4. **Gowers U³ norm of the orbit sequences as the quadratic-structure hidden-shift lever** `[adaptation]` → RECORD. Mechanism: classical hidden-shift algorithms need structure of F(t) = x(γ^tP) on Z_M, M=(N−1)/2: homomorphic (→ DLP in the image), Fourier-sparse (→ large U², derivable from the spectrum M(χ,b) of wave-2 QC record 2), or quadratic-phase (→ U³ = 1; G(t)/F(t) is then a linear phase and two samples give s). Inverse U³ (Green–Tao, recalled) makes ‖f‖_{U³} the exact obstruction for that class. Reduction: if f correlates ρ ≥ M^{-1/8} with a quadratic phase, shift recovery costs ρ^{-2} ≤ M^{1/4}. Best known exponent 1/2; needed: any correlation ≥ N^{-1/4}; the exact U³ computation detects only ρ ≥ M^{-1/8}. Obstruction: random-level U³^8 ≈ 3/M. Test: O(M² log M) exact computation at p ≤ 2^17.

5. **Small-order autocorrelation of the quadratic-character orbit sequence (non-Weil regime)** `[adaptation]` → RECORD. Mechanism: A(λ) = Σ_R χ_2(x(R)x(λR)); Weil controls it only for λ ≤ p^{1/4} as integers. Multipliers λ of small multiplicative order q | N−1 are large integers, hence uncontrolled, and a correlation |A(λ)| ≥ εM at such λ is an approximate period of the sequence under the order-q subgroup: s mod q becomes a classical distinguisher at cost ε^{-2}, a Pohlig–Hellman shadow on the scalar torsor (torsion-wave "Coverings of the scalar torsor are Cheon"). Exact positive control: λ ∈ μ_6 on j=0 gives |A| = M. Reduction: ECDLP → s mod q then lift, cost ε^{-2}q + √(M/q); exponent moves iff ε ≥ N^{-1/4}; random level ε ≈ M^{-1/2}. Test: one FFT gives all M shifts at p ≤ 2^26.

6. **Walsh low-degree concentration of x(kP)-bits as a Boolean function of the bits of k (LMN/Kushilevitz–Mansour shadow of Bernstein–Vazirani)** `[adaptation]` → RECORD.

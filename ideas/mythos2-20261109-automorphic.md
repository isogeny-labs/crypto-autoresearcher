## LEADS

1. **The Hecke eigenform of y² = x³ + 7 knows the Frobenius polynomial and nothing finer** `[known]`. Mechanism: secp256k1 is the reduction of a CM Q-curve; its L-function is L(ψ,s) for a Grössencharacter ψ of Q(√−3) with ψ(p) = π, so every automorphic datum at p is a function of π, i.e. of t = p+1−N. Reduction: ECDLP on E(F_p) is reduced to nothing; the only congruence available is a_p ≡ p+1 (mod N), which is the statement N | #E. Exponent needed 1/2 → delta 0. Obstruction: all L-function data are isogeny-class invariants (KR-RHO-755e35). Falsification: exhibit any quantity in the Hecke-character packet of E that differs between (E,P,Q) and (E,P,Q′). Artifacts: `RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md` and `secp256k1_cm_audit` must be read for the normalisation of π and λ used below.

2. **Diamond operators on X_1(N) and the Lefschetz count of curves whose Frobenius has eigenvalue k on an N-point** `[adaptation]` (RECORD A). Mechanism: (E,P) ∈ X_1(N)(F_p); the DL is the diamond operator ⟨k⟩ on the (Z/N)^×/±1 torsor over j(E) (IDEA-20260806-0c9de1). The trace formula for Frob∘⟨k⟩ counts pairs (E′,P′) with Frob_p P′ = kP′, which exist iff N | k² − tk + p for some |t| ≤ 2√p; for N > 4√p at most one t, so the population is non-empty for ≤ (4√p+1)/N ≈ 4p^{−1/2} of all k, and its weighted size is the Kronecker class number H(t²−4p). Reduction attempted: ECDLP → locate E in that population; blocked because every F_p-isogeny commutes with Frobenius, so the eigenvalue-1 line G is isogeny-invariant and no algebraic map connects E to any curve with eigenvalue k ≠ 1. Delta 0. Falsification test: at calibration primes, enumerate all curves, compute the coverage fraction and the class-number identity; any k-dependence of a statistic computed from E alone kills the barrier.

3. **Spectral statistics on the fibre of X_1(N) → X_0(N) are ⟨k⟩-invariant** `[known]`. Mechanism: pulling the universal curve back along ⟨k⟩ relabels the point and does not change the curve, so Frobenius angles, Katz–Sarnak vertical distributions and one-level densities over any ⟨k⟩-orbit coincide. Treated as barrier evidence: no vertical statistic of Frobenius over the family of (E′,P′) separates DL classes. Delta 0. Falsification: a Frobenius-derived statistic on X_1(N)(F_p) that is not ⟨k⟩-invariant. No compute needed; folded into Record A as a control.

4. **Cuspidal degeneration of X_1(N) is the pairing transfer** `[known]`. Mechanism: at the cusp the universal curve becomes the Tate curve with N-torsion μ_N × q^{Z/N}; the torsor DL on the cuspidal fibre is the DL in μ_N ⊂ F_{p^k}^×, k = ord_N(p). The modular picture reproduces MOV/Frey–Rück exactly (KR-RHO-53c89f) and the wave-1 transfer-admissibility certificate: cost L(1/3) in F_{p^k} only for small k; secp256k1 has k astronomically large. Falsification: compute k for the target (the wave-1 certificate already does this).

5. **Galois representation on the N-torsion of the canonical lift separates nothing** `[known]`. Mechanism: for the lift Ẽ/Z_p and the torsion-section lifts P̂, Q̂ ∈ Ẽ[N] (wave-1 p-adic record), Frob_p acts as diag(1,p) and Q̂ = kP̂ lies on the same eigenline, so Q(P̂) = Q(Q̂) and every Chebotarev or Frobenius-trace statistic over primes ℓ is identical for the two points. Barrier, delta 0. Falsification: a prime ℓ whose Frobenius class in Gal(Q(Ẽ[N])/Q) acts differently on the two cyclic subgroups — impossible since they coincide.

6. **Analytic rank of Q-lifts: the GRH rank bound of any lift exceeds the number of forcible rational points** `[known]`. Mechanism: a plane-cubic lift through r reductions of points of E(F_p) has r ≤ 9 (Weierstrass: ≤ 5) and conductor ≤ p^{O(1)}; Mestre's GRH bound rank ≤ (2+o(1))·log N_Ẽ/log log N_Ẽ exceeds 9 for every cryptographic p, so dependence among lifted points is never forced; xedni-type lifts (KR-IC-cd159a) stay rank-full with probability 1 − o(1), and the analytic-rank computation itself costs ≥ √(conductor) ≥ p^{3}. Delta 0. Test: at p ≤ 2^7, force r = 5 points, compute 2-Selmer rank bounds with Sage's mwrank; predicted rank ≥ 5 in > 95 % of lifts. Artifact to read: `RESEARCH_MESTRE_HOWE.md` (what rank heuristics it already records).

7. **Heegner points: an L-function that knows a specific DL, and how little it covers** `[adaptation]` (RECORD B). Mechanism: on a rank-1 Ẽ/Q, Gross–Kohnen–Zagier give y_D = c(D)·y_0 + torsion with c(D) Fourier coefficients of a weight-3/2 form, c(D)² ∝ L′(Ẽ,1)L(Ẽ,χ_D,1)√|D|. Reducing mod p gives DL(ȳ_{D₂} : ȳ_{D₁}) ≡ c(D₂)/c(D₁) (mod N) computed from L-values with no group operation on E(F_p). Reduction: ECDLP for arbitrary Q is NOT reduced, since reachable DLs are ratios of integers ≤ |D|^{1/2+ε}; covering a uniform k needs |D| ≳ N, and computing one Heegner point of discriminant D costs ≥ h(D) ≈ |D|^{1/2} ≥ N^{1/2}: exactly rho. Delta 0. Falsification: a DL mismatch on 37a1 (kills the recalled theorem) or a coverage count exceeding X^{1+ε} for |D| ≤ X.

8. **Function-field analytic rank of K3 elliptic surfaces and the bounded-coefficient reach of specialisation** `[adaptation]` (RECORD C). Mechanism: for y² = x³ + a(t)x + b(t) with χ = 2, Tate's conjecture is known, so MW rank = ord_{T=1/p} L(E/F_p(t),T), computable from point counts; but sections have Shioda height ≤ 2χ = 4, so any relation among r+1 sections in a rank-r lattice has |c_i| ≤ (4√r)^r·d_T (Hadamard on the Gram cofactors). Specialisation of a relation supported on sections through (t₀,P) and (t₀,Q) yields k = −c₁/c₂ only for k in a set of size ≤ (2·(4√r)^r d_T)² ≪ N. Extends IDEA-20260805-5a9d2c/-e5dd09/-4ddf4e (rational surfaces: forced norm-1 relations) to χ = 2 where the L-function is non-trivial. Delta 0. Test: measure max |c_i| and analytic = algebraic rank at p ≤ 2^10.

Artifact: `RESEARCH_SECP256K1_CM.md` for the λ normalisation. Falsification: a section pair whose DL is not such a ratio.

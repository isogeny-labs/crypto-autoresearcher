1. **Hybrid-sum flatness of the discrete-log graph** `[adaptation]` — barrier (b). For a ∈ F_p, b ∈ Z/N put F(a,b) = Σ_{0<k<N} e_p(a·x(kP))·e(bk/N). Theorem (Weil–Deligne; Grothendieck–Ogg–Shafarevich with one puncture and Swan conductor 2 at O gives dim H¹_c = 3): |F(a,b)| ≤ 3√p for all a ≠ 0 and all b, and F(0,b) = −1. The graph {(k, x(kP))} ⊂ Z/N × F_p is therefore 3√p-quasirandom, whereas a random subset of the same density has sup-norm ≈ √(N·ln(Np)) ≈ 4√N. Reduction: none; it bounds attack families. Precise lower-bound statement: any F_p-additive-character statistic of x averaged over an arithmetic progression S of scalars has normalised bias ≤ 3√p(1+ln N)/|S|. Rules out: bit-bias, Legendre-sequence, PRNG-distinguisher and Fourier-localisation attacks on k that use scalar sets longer than p^{1/2+o(1)}; it is silent below √p, exactly the sub-rho regime. Exponent delta 0. Obstruction to positive use: the b-direction needs discrete logs to evaluate. Cheap test: compute the full matrix at p ≤ 2^14 (Record A); IDEA-20260805-c4f675's C(p) is a weighted column average of this matrix, and the odd-characteristic absence of a group morphism E(F_p) → F_p (unless N = p, KR-RHO-53c89f) is the analogue of the trace equation in KR-IC-f5c584.

6. **Elliptic Burgess problem for the rank-2 trace function K_b(x) = Σ_{Q over x} χ_b(Q)** `[speculative]` — algorithm/barrier (a,b). K_b is a trace function of bounded conductor on F_p (pushforward of a character sheaf along the degree-2 map x). Pólya–Vinogradov is a theorem: |Σ_{x∈I} K_b(x)| ≤ 3√p(1+ln p) for every interval I. Burgess-type cancellation for |I| = p^θ, θ < 1/2, is open and the classical amplification step is unavailable: multiplicative dilation preserves x-intervals in F_p but not on E, and translation on E scatters x-intervals, which is itself a sum–product phenomenon. Status of the statement: theorem above √p, open below. If cancellation fails with a bias, lead 2's target-set boost is live; if it holds, the yield heuristic at β = 1/4 is plausible but still uncertifiable (lead 3). Cost: exponent unchanged either way (delta 0); only the heuristic status moves. Test: identical to Record B at β = 1/4 and 1/3; a measured slope α > 0 is the discriminating event.

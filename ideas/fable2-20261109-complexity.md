## LEADS

1. **Degree equals yes-size: a provable floor for algebraic tests on a single target** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: a nonzero $f\in\mathbb F_p(E)$ of degree $d$ has at most $d$ zeros on $E(\mathbb F_p)$, and $[b]$ has degree $b^2$, so a test "$f(aP+bQ)=0$" is answered *yes* by at most $d\,b^2$ targets $Q$. The always-"no" adversary gives: any tree of rational tests correct on $\varepsilon N$ targets has a path of total degree $\ge\varepsilon N-1$; any 0/1-valued $g\in\mathbb F_p(E)$ has $\deg g\ge\max(s,N-s)\ge N/2$. Reduces nothing; prices the class. Exponent delta 0; it is *weaker* than KR-RHO-13bf67 in the window regime (constant-degree tests on $L$ consecutive multiples are allowed down to $(N/d)^{1/3}$). Obstruction: bit-extraction/interval tests escape the accounting. Falsification: a function in $L(dO)$ with more than $d$ zeros at a calibration prime (`crypto/docs/ECDLP_CALIBRATION_PRIMES.md`). Refines the wave-2 complexity lead "algebraic adversaries have no lower-bound tool" and reuses the Bézout ingredient of `IDEA-20260801-021`/`IDEA-20260803-e2f5bd`.

2. **The algebraic / bit-level dichotomy and the $(N/d)^{1/3}$ gap the degree budget leaves** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: every attack lead in waves 1–2 is either group-law-only (priced by KR-RHO-13bf67), algebraic-test (priced by lead 1), bit-level (SAT/SMT/MILP, priced only by proof complexity) or lifting (priced by height, KR-IC-cd159a). Lead 1 alone does not exclude a window method with $L=(N/d)^{1/3}$ multiples and degree-$d$ tests; the only tool that reaches there is Weil/Deligne flatness (barriers-wave "Hybrid-sum flatness"), which is itself only $p^{1/2}$-tight. Reduces nothing. Exponent delta 0. Obstruction: no lower bound below $N^{1/3}$ for constant-degree window statistics exists. Test: re-cost the barriers-wave "Short-window fingerprints" lead under $\sum d_ib_i^2\ge N-1$; it must land at $L\ge N^{1/3}$ or declare its tests non-algebraic.

3. **Direct-ECDLP CNF scaling slope with the scalar restricted to $b$ bits** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: Tseitin-encode a projective double-and-add circuit over bit-blasted $\mathbb F_p$ arithmetic ($p\approx2^{20}$) asserting $\exists k<2^b:[k]P=Q$; SAT arm ($k<2^b$) and UNSAT arm ($k\ge2^b$); slope $s=\mathrm d\log_2T/\mathrm db$. Reduces interval ECDLP to CDCL search; an interesting result needs $s<1/2$ (generic interval cost $2^{b/2}$, KR-RHO-898783); predicted $s\approx1$. Obstruction: ABRW-type hardness of range statements for resolution. Falsification: $s\le0.6$ on the UNSAT arm not matched by a structure-free iterated-quadratic-map control. Extends KR-IC-5765d2 and the Koblitz CaDiCaL pipeline (`crypto/RESEARCH_SAT_SEMAEV.md`, `cryptanalysis/experiments/a2-n23-pdp-sat-20261009`); the gate decomposition in `crypto/quantum_circuit_secp256k1` is the circuit source to read.

4. **Nullstellensatz degree of non-decomposing membership systems is exactly $\deg\mathrm{NF}(S^{-1})+\deg S$, blind to the factor base's coordinate structure** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: for grid ideal $(g_1(x_1),\dots,g_m(x_m))$, $g_j=\prod_{a\in X}(x_j-a)$, and a target with no zero of $S_{m+1}$ on $X^m$, every certificate $1=h'S+\sum h_jg_j$ has $h'\equiv h$, $h$ the reduced inverse, so $D_{NS}=\deg h+\deg_{\mathrm{tot}}S$; $\deg h=m(B'-1)$ iff the Combinatorial-Nullstellensatz top coefficient $\sum_{x\in X^m}S(x)^{-1}\prod_j g_j'(x_j)^{-1}\neq0$, which fails for $\approx1/p$ of targets whatever $X$ is. Reduces XL/Macaulay linearisation cost to a closed form, a factor $m$ in degree above the F4/PC wall $\approx B'$. Exponent delta 0 (enumeration floor confirmed). Obstruction: PC/F4 degree is lower and is measured, not derived. Test: evaluate the sum for $\mu_B$, interval and random bases of equal size at $p\le2^{12}$. Extends the complexity-wave PC record, `crypto/RESEARCH_FFD_PROOF_COMPLEXITY.md` (next agent checks whether it measured first-fall or Nullstellensatz degree) and the echelon method of `IDEA-20260801-002`.

5. **Flattening-rank drop locus of the $x_R$-specialised $S_4$ tensor and the special-target barrier** `[adaptation]` — Mechanism: $S_4(\cdot;x_R)$ is a $5\times5\times5$ tensor whose $5\times25$ flattening has generic rank 5; its $5\times5$ minors are polynomials of degree $\le20$ in $x_R$, so the drop set $D(E)$ has $|D(E)|\le20$ or is everything. Randomising $Q'=Q+rP$ onto any special set $Z$ costs $N/|Z|$ trials, so a special-target speedup needs $|Z|>\sqrt N$, and by lead 1 an algebraically defined $Z$ has $|Z|\le$ its degree. Reduces nothing; closes "special targets". Exponent delta 0. Obstruction: the drop locus might coincide with the IDEA-20260723-006 exceptional set and be exactly the targets whose decomposition is already free. Test: compute $D(E)$ for all $x_R$ at $p\le2^{13}$ on four curves and intersect with the IDEA-20260723-006 set; extends the wave-2 flattening-floor record.

6. **Bilinear rank of the biquadratic addition law prices constants only** `[known]` — Mechanism: the complete addition law is a tensor in $\mathrm{Sym}^2V^*\otimes\mathrm{Sym}^2V^*\otimes V$ ($6\times6\times3$); flattenings give rank $\ge6$, known formulas give $\le12$ multiplications (Bosma–Lenstra/Renes–Costello–Batina, recalled). A lower bound $r^*$ forces $\ge r^*$ field multiplications per projective step, i.e. a floor on the 1129-cycle constant of KR-RHO-825bca and on `cryptanalysis/include/ca_rho.h`'s affine+Montgomery step (KR-RHO-cb1c58). It rules out nothing about the exponent since KR-RHO-13bf67 counts group operations. Test: exact rank of the tensor at $p=7$ by Gröbner over the rank-$r$ parametrisation; already a wave-2 lead.

7. **Group-algebra Wedderburn rank reads the embedding degree** `[known]` — Mechanism: $\mathbb F_p[E(\mathbb F_p)]\cong\mathbb F_p\oplus\mathbb F_{p^{k}}^{(N-1)/k}$ with $k=\mathrm{ord}_N(p)$ the MOV embedding degree, so its bilinear complexity is $\ge2N-1-(N-1)/k$ and a diagonal (rank-$N$) presentation exists only over $\mathbb F_{p^k}$. ECDLP is the tensor isomorphism $\mathbb F_p[E]\to\mathbb F_p[\mathbb Z/N]$, input size $N$. Reduces to nothing cheaper; restates the port-wave exit-dimension record and KR-RHO-53c89f. Test: none needed; a counting identity.

8. **No algebraic families of relations: the summation fibre is an abelian variety** `[known]` — Mechanism: $X_R\cong E^{m-1}$ has no nonconstant rational curves (Lang–Néron, recalled), so every algebraic one-parameter family of decompositions over the *constant* curve is constant; relation supply must come from group-law sampling or from solving. Prices the XEDN lane (`IDEA-20260805-4ddf4e`, `-d426f9`): non-constant surfaces give points on different fibres, never on $E$. Exponent delta 0. Test: the height control of `run_census.sage` (`IDEA-20260805-68c69a`) applied to the constant surface must return $E(\mathbb F_p(t))=E(\mathbb F_p)$.

9. **Time–space version of lead 1 for $L$ targets** `[speculative]` — Mechanism: Borodin–Cook progress argument in the rational-test branching-program model: in $t$ steps from any state the program answers correctly for at most $\sum d_i$ of the inputs reaching that state, suggesting $T\cdot S\gtrsim L\cdot N/d$ for degree-$d$ tests without arithmetic. Would price table-driven multi-target lookups below the generic $\sqrt{2LN}$ (KR-RHO-46c2c6) and $S T^2\ge N$ (KR-RHO-ea34b8) only in the restricted model. Obstruction: multi-target tests $f(Q_1,Q_2)$ have codimension-1 zero sets, breaking the finite-fibre count. Test: check the inequality against the fixed-base lookup experiments (`cryptanalysis/experiments/a1-n53-fixed-base-query-20261012`, read to see whether its queries are algebraic tests) before any derivation is attempted.

10. **Nonscalar complexity of the discrete-log function: $\sqrt p$ from Paterson–Stockmeyer, no tool for the specific function** `[speculative]` — Mechanism: the DL as a univariate polynomial in $x(Q)$ of degree $\approx p$ needs $O(\sqrt p)$ nonscalar multiplications (PS) and $\Omega(\sqrt p)$ for almost all polynomials by counting straight-line programs, but total cost is $\Theta(p)$ scalar work and no lower bound for a *specific* polynomial over $\mathbb F_p$ exists. Reduces nothing. Exponent delta 0. Test: at $p\in\{5,7\}$ enumerate SLPs exhaustively and compare the DL function's nonscalar complexity with random functions; a systematic gap would be the first sign of algebraic structure in the function itself.

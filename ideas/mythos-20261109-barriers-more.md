## LEADS

Wave-1 barriers covered relation supply (Fourier, energy, incidence, sum–product), the discrete-log graph (hybrid sums, windows, sumsets), and the Macaulay column count. It left untouched: deterministic size functions on points, the branching-solver family, the linear-algebra stage, the "hidden correspondence" family, and the composition of barriers as the arity grows. Each lead below names the wave-1 item that forced it.

1. **Quasimorphism barrier: no bounded-defect height on E(F_p)** `[adaptation]` — Forced by the wave-1 sweep lead "CM-module / class-group port", which asserts "no norm smoothness on points" without a theorem, and by barriers "Energy–Freiman tautology", which treats only the statistical side. Mechanism: if $h:G\to\mathbb R$ on a finite group has defect $D=\sup|h(ab)-h(a)-h(b)|$, then $|h(g^n)-nh(g)|\le(n-1)D$; with $n$ a multiple of $\mathrm{ord}(g)$, $h(g^n)=h(e)$, $|h(e)|\le D$, so $\sup|h|\le D$. Hence any size function on $E(\mathbb F_p)$ with spread $H$ has defect $\ge H/2$: the defect-0 log-norm NFS and the linear sieve use on $\mathbb Z$ (degree on $\mathbb F_q[x]$) has no analogue on points. Reduces ECDLP to nothing; closes the "define a norm, sieve small-norm sums" family deterministically and routes every probabilistic version to the filed energy/spectral measurements. Exponent delta 0 vs $p^{1/2}$ (KR-RHO-13bf67). Obstruction to the barrier: heights on lifts $\tilde E(\mathbb Q_p),\tilde E(\mathbb Q)$ exist, but are quadratic (KR-IC-cd159a). Cheap test: at $p\approx2^{20}$ histogram the defect of $h=x(P)/p$ against the discrete-log height $\tilde k/N$ from one log table; prediction: coordinate height behaves as three independent uniforms, DL height has atoms of mass $1/2$ at $D=0$ and $D=-1$. The next agent must read `RESEARCH_PKM_CRITERION.md` for any criterion phrased as a size function and re-check it against the theorem. **Record A.**

2. **Dyadic-subcube flatness floor for branching solvers on prime-field PDP** `[adaptation]` — Forced by wave-1 wildcards "QF_BV SMT scaling exponent $c_m$" and relations "SAT on the F_p μ_B system", both of which measure solver exponents with no mechanism, and by the `cryptanalysis` commits "Measure triple-sum affine prefix saturation on N41 and N131" (a prefix statistic in the Koblitz lane never ported to $\mathbb F_p$). Mechanism: for the fibre $X_R=\{S_{m+1}(x;x_R)=0\}\subset\mathbb A^m$ the nonzero Fourier coefficients of its indicator are $2^{O(m)}p^{(m-1)/2}$ (Weil, as in the wave-1 hybrid-sum record), so counts in any box $C$ equal $|C|\cdot|X_R|/p^m$ up to that error times $\log^m p$: flatness is a theorem for $|C|\gg p^{(m+1)/2}$, and the claim below that scale is that solution counts over bit-prefix subcubes are Poisson-dispersed. A branching solver (CDCL, bit-vector SMT) then gets no prefix signal above the depth where the mean count is 1 and must refute $\approx|I|^{m-1}/\mathrm{polylog}$ subcubes: the enumeration floor, i.e. $c_m=(m-1)\beta$. Delta 0 (KR-IC-5765d2 already says SAT changes no exponent on binary curves). Obstruction: CDCL learns clauses that are not prefix-local. Cheap test: exhaustive $X_R$ at $p\approx2^{24}$, $\beta=1/2$, $m=3$, dispersion index per depth vs Poisson null, random-polynomial control, and a log-aligned positive control. **Record B.**

3. **Minimal-polynomial degree of prime-field relation matrices (closing structured LA)** `[adaptation]` — Forced by the absence of any wave-1 barrier on the linear-algebra stage (Minkowski column count concerns the Macaulay solve); extends `IDEA-20260727-001` (displacement rank $O(1)$, $j=0$ only) to a second invariant and to non-GLV bases. Mechanism: Wiedemann/Lanczos cost $2\deg\mu_M$ matvecs; sub-$B^2$ LA needs $\deg\mu_M\ll B$; but a relation matrix that determines the logs has rank exactly $B-1$ over $\mathbb Z/N$ (kernel $=\langle\log\rangle$), so a short minimal polynomial would require a highly structured spectrum of a full-rank sparse $\pm1$ matrix. Prediction: $\deg\mu_M=B-1-\delta$, $\delta\le2$, for interval, $\mu_B$ and $\lambda$-closed bases, identical to a random $(m+1)$-sparse $\pm1$ control. LA exponent fixed at $2\beta$; delta 0. Obstruction: LA is never the bottleneck (relation exponent 1 dominates), so even a surprise moves nothing without a decomposition gain. Test: Berlekamp–Massey on a Krylov sequence mod $N$ for $B\le4000$ matrices from the `crypto` `ic` executable; the next agent must read `cryptanalysis/include/ca_indexcalc.h` (README: structured elimination + Lanczos) for whether iteration counts are exposed.

4. **Correspondence barrier: $\mathrm{Pic}(E\times E)$ modulo fibral classes is $\mathrm{End}(E)=\mathbb Z[\omega]$** `[known]` — Forced by `IDEA-20260805-51996a`: "hidden correspondences" is priority family (3) with no idea produced in two batches. Mechanism: $\mathrm{NS}(E\times E)\cong\mathbb Z^2\oplus\mathrm{End}(E)$ (textbook), so every algebraic correspondence $E\vdash E$ is $a+b\omega$ composed with translations; on secp256k1 $\mathrm{End}=\mathbb Z[\omega]$, rank 2, $h(-3)=1$, so the family is exhausted by GLV (KR-RHO-0528e7, constant $\sqrt6$) and translations; Hecke correspondences $T_\ell$ leave $E$ into the isogeny class (KR-RHO-755e35). Delta 0. Obstruction: correspondences $E\vdash C$ into other curves are the transfer lane, not closed here. Test: on a toy ordinary $j=0$ curve compute $\#E(\mathbb F_{p^k})$, $k\le4$, and read $\mathrm{rank}\,\mathrm{NS}(E\times E)=4$ from the zeta function of $E\times E$ versus 3 for a non-CM toy curve. Lead only: zero compute, closes a goal item by citation.

5. **Arity-growth floor: Macaulay-only Semaev lands at $L_N(2/3)$; prime-field predicate degree returns exponent 1** `[adaptation]` — Forced by wave-1 barriers "Minkowski column count" (fixed $m$) and sweep "Scheme-degree barrier" ($|F|\le3\deg$), never composed over $m\to\infty$. Mechanism: at yield one, $B=N^{1/m}$; if a decomposition costs $2^{cm^2}$ (Macaulay columns $\approx(2^{m-2})^m$), relation cost $B\cdot2^{cm^2}=\exp(\log N/m+cm^2\ln2)$ is minimised at $m\approx(\log N)^{1/3}$, giving $\exp(\Theta((\log N)^{2/3}))=L_N[2/3]$ — Diem's exponent (KR-IC-64659b) recovered from a column count, a check on the model. Over $\mathbb F_p$ the variables must be cut to $B$ values by a predicate of degree $\ge B/3$, so the Macaulay degree is $\ge B$ and columns $\ge B^m=N$; the enumeration alternative costs $B^{m-1}$ per trial, total $B\cdot B^{m-1}=N$. So every algebraic Semaev route over $\mathbb F_p$ has relation exponent $\ge1-o(1)$ unless the per-trial exponent falls below the relations-lane $e_k$ threshold. Delta 0 vs KR-IC-5931ee. Obstruction: $2^{cm^2}$ is a Macaulay heuristic, not a lower bound over all solvers. Test: fit $\log_2(\text{columns})$ against $m^2$ for $m=3..7$ from the wave-1 Minkowski count ($c\ge0.5$ confirms); any $\mathbb F_p$ predicate of degree $\le B^{1-\delta}$ cutting $\ge B$ points would falsify the composition and `IDEA-20260801-021` together.

## Learning and applicability

Deleting a 1/N fraction of each coordinate subspace can delete a central-binomial fraction of their union. Per-component retention is not global coverage retention.

- Highly overlapping affine coordinate subspaces
- Local and global denominators must be distinguished
- Finite witnesses do not independently prove the all-exponent asymptotic claim

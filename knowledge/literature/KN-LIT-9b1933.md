## Contribution (as reported)
- `ω(1,k,1) ≤ 2` for `0 ≤ k ≤ 1/2`, and `ω(1,k,1) ≤ 1+k+1/(4k)` for `k ≥ 1/2`. So the dual exponent satisfies `α ≥ 1/2`.
- It extends OpenAI's 9/4 analysis to rectangular products. The applications include APSP in `O(n^{2.5})`.
- **Characteristic 0 only:** the bounds "hold over every field of characteristic zero". Characteristic 2 is not claimed.

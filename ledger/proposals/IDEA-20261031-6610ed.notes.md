## Corrections found by the design probes (foreground PARI/GP, 2026-10-31; probes, not results)

The report's construction recipe was wrong in two places:

1. **The group order is 4l, not prime.** Frobenius minus 1 is divisible by both alpha and its conjugate, and the
   full 2-torsion is rational. The experiment concerns the order-l subgroup.
2. **The prime is N(1 +- 2 delta)**, not N(1 +- gamma).

Taken exactly as written (l dividing 2^h + 1 - L_h), only h = 19 (l = 262543) and h = 23 (l = 4196903) give an
instance with h between 16 and 24. Allowing a small cofactor m <= 40 gives 45 usable (l, conjugate) pairs, but only
h = 19 and 23 are equality cases of the orbit-size bound; the others have h / log2 l between 2 and 5. Velu's alpha
matched [lambda]P on 10 of 10 points at h = 19 and h = 23, and only one of the two alpha-type kernels has
eigenvalue order h.

## Prediction recorded before any data

Net loss of about 4 to 10 times in total counted cost at h = 19 to 23, growing roughly as 1.2 to 2 times sqrt(h).
Break-even would need an alpha evaluation costing about 1.2 field multiplications at h = 19, which a degree-2 map
cannot reach. The iteration saving of about 1/sqrt(h) is the positive control and is expected to be real.

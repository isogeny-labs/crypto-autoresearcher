## 2. Mechanism and derivation

**Object frame.** Representation R1: x_k = L_k(d_k) := a_k + sum_{j<s} c_{k,j} d_{k,j} with
Boolean digits d_{k,j}^2 = d_{k,j} and nonzero weights c_{k,j} in F_p (base-2 digits,
windows [a, a + 2^s), binary-coefficient GAPs). Operation set Sigma_IC: translation by
factor-base points (index calculus, Class I). Inside the oracle Sigma_PC = {multiplication by
a variable, linear combination, Boolean-axiom reduction, restriction by constants}; PC degree
is monotone under restriction, which is the Sigma-compatibility the proof uses. Trichotomy:
partial-action (the relation vector propagates only under factor-base translation,
IDEA-20260806-c5d183); T1 bounds the oracle's refutation cost, not the relation object.
KN-OPEN-020 class: implicit membership. Representation class R1 of RQ-ECDLP-623a32; stage:
relation decomposition.

**Lemma K_p (IPS 1999 Thm 4.1 in characteristic p).** Hypotheses: F a field of
characteristic 0 or p; c_1..c_n in F^x; rho in F (any extension of F_p allowed); char F = 0
or p > ceil(n/2). Conclusion: {rho - sum c_i x_i} together with {x_i^2 - x_i} has no PC_F
refutation of degree <= ceil(n/2).
Proof: IPS (pp. 8-9, retrieved by the scouts; the theorem there is stated over the reals)
use the field in two places only. (1) Row and column scaling of the inclusion matrix D_k^n
by products of the c_i: needs c_i != 0. (2) Full row rank of the k-set vs (k+1)-set
inclusion matrix for k <= ceil(n/2) - 1, cited by IPS from Gottlieb over the reals. In
characteristic p, Wilson's diagonal form at (t, k) = (k, k+1) has entries
C(k+1-i, k-i) = k+1-i in {1..k+1}, so the rank is full iff p > k+1; over all
k <= ceil(n/2) - 1 this is p > ceil(n/2). (de Caen 2001, retrieved by the SAT gate, gives the
same full-rank conclusion for characteristic p > k; Ghorbani arXiv 0709.3144 Cor. 1-2,
retrieved at secondary level by the commutative-algebra gate, supports it too.) Lemma 4.2
then holds verbatim: every line of a degree-<= ceil(n/2) derivation is ML(q (rho - sum c_i x_i))
with deg q <= ceil(n/2) - 1, and 1 is not of that form. This transfer is the candidates' own
step; IPS do not state it.
- Remark (a): if rho is in F_p and the instance is UNSAT, Cauchy-Davenport gives n <= p - 2,
  so the hypothesis is automatic; it matters only for rho in an extension.
- Remark (b), the nearby object on which the conclusion fails: characteristic 3, c_i = 1,
  rho in F_9 \ F_3. Then (sum x_i - rho)^3 = sum x_i^3 - rho^3 = sum x_i - rho^3 modulo the
  Boolean ideal, and a degree-3 derivation reaches the nonzero constant rho - rho^3, for
  every n. In general the Frobenius gives degree-p refutations when the c_i lie in F_p and
  rho does not. This is also the characteristic-2 (Weil descent) case, where membership is
  F_2-linear and the barrier is absent.

**Theorem T1.** Hypotheses: p prime with p > ceil(s/2); E/F_p nonsingular; m >= 2; target
x_R; each leg presented as L_k(d_k) above. Systems: (a) direct {S_{m+1}(L_1..L_m, x_R)} with
the Boolean axioms; (b) chained {S_3(L_1, L_2, u_1), S_3(u_{i-1}, L_{i+1}, u_i),
S_3(u_{m-2}, L_m, x_R)} with Boolean axioms and field variables u_i (no field equation).
UNSAT means no solution over Fbar_p with Boolean digits (the only case in which a PC
refutation exists). Conclusion: every PC refutation over F_p or a finite extension has
degree >= ceil(s/2) + 1 for (b) at every s, and for (a) when 2^{m-1} <= ceil(s/2); with
unequal blocks, >= ceil(s_max/2) + 1.
Quantifier order: forall p, s with p > ceil(s/2), forall E, x_R and Boolean-affine
presentations, forall Fbar_p-UNSAT instances in the stated regime, forall PC refutations over
F_{p^d}: degree >= ceil(s/2) + 1.
Proof.
1. Restriction. Keep leg k0 free. Assign Boolean values to every other leg; choose chain
   abscissae forward from leg 1 and backward from x_R so every equation without L_{k0}
   holds (values in a finite extension; each step solves a quadratic). Substitution maps a
   degree-D refutation to a degree-<= D refutation of the residual {g(L_{k0}(d))} with the
   Boolean axioms; g is univariate of degree e (e = 2 chained, e = 2^{m-1} direct) and has
   no root among the cube values of L_{k0}, else the full system would have an Fbar_p
   solution. Non-degeneracy: the x^2 coefficient of S_3(X, a, u) is (u - a)^2, and when u = a
   the X coefficient is -4(a^3 + Aa + B), so g degenerates only at a rational 2-torsion
   abscissa, impossible when #E(F_p) is odd.
2. Linear core. With rho a root of g (characteristic p extension), g(L) = h(L)(L - rho) is
   derivable from rho - L in multilinear degree <= e (IPS Lemma 3.1 style), so a refutation
   of rho - L of degree max(D, e) exists. When e <= ceil(s/2), Lemma K_p forces
   D >= ceil(s/2) + 1. Chained: e = 2 <= ceil(s/2) for s >= 3, and for s <= 2 the bound holds
   trivially (every S_3 axiom has degree >= 2). Direct: exactly 2^{m-1} <= ceil(s/2).
Scope line (twist decompositions): targets with no F_p decomposition but an Fbar_p solution
(twist abscissae in the box, intermediate sums over an extension) have no algebraic
refutation; a Groebner solver returns spurious solutions there; T1 says nothing about them.

**Corollaries.**
- Solver independence (H3): by the Groebner-to-PC simulation (IPS Sec. 5, retrieved;
  Clegg-Edmonds-Impagliazzo 1996, recalled) Macaulay/XL at degree D, F4, F5, MutantXL and
  Buchberger reach degree >= ceil(s/2) + 1 on every UNSAT instance in the regime, for every
  curve, target and monomial order. With H2 (Caminata-Gorla identity, recalled), d_lf >=
  ceil(s/2) + 1 too; at B ~ (m! p)^{1/m} this is >= (log2 p)/(2m) + 1. The bounded-d_lf branch
  is impossible for these solvers.
- Not covered: lattice reduction (IDEA-20261024-1b76a4's lead), cutting planes, SoS and
  pseudo-Boolean solvers (the binary value principle is easy for semi-algebraic systems,
  AGHT 2020, recalled), carry-circuit encodings with auxiliary carry bits, enumeration,
  satisfiable attempts beyond L1-D.
- Characteristic contrast: over F_2 the one-block restriction of a Weil-descended system is
  F_2-affine and refutes at degree 1; T1's hypothesis is exactly what fails there.

**Commutative-algebra additions (L1-D, L1-E; derivations, not reviewed by any gate).**
- L1-D: with t solutions over Fbar_p, fixing |J| <= log2 t + 1 bits to a value matched by no
  solution gives D >= ceil((s - log2 t - 1)/2) + 1 for satisfiable attempts. No gate
  examined it.
- L1-E (direct presentations): D >= deg ML(S_{m+1} in digits) = m min(2^{m-1}, s), using the
  curve-free leading form +-prod x_i^{2^{m-1}} (commutative-algebra C4, H-TOP); for s < 2^{m-1}
  the top coefficient is +-c(s,k)^m, computed nonzero mod p for P-256 and secp256k1 at
  m = 7..10 (`scout/ca2/a1_check.py` -> `a1_check.out`). This floor is the same object as
  EV-PFDR-f71d7f's generator degree.
- Gate condition (commutative-algebra gate, H2): the restricted axiom meets Lemma 4.2's
  hypothesis only when the multiplier degree stays below ceil(s/2); for the direct system this
  is T1's 2^{m-1} <= ceil(s/2). On the planned toy ladder, only m = 2 with s = 3..8 and m = 3
  with s = 7, 8 test the claimed regime; m = 2, s = 2 and m = 3, s <= 6 are corner cells and
  are reported separately.

**Open check (both gates), recorded as a dominance question, not asserted.** If the
refutation degree of an UNSAT digit system is at least its first fall degree, the recorded
law d_ff = m 2^{m-1} + floor((s - 2^{m-1})/2) + 1 already dominates ceil(s/2) + 1 on the direct
presentation (62 vs 34 at m = 4, s = 66), and EV-PFDR-f71d7f already gives d_lf >= m 2^{m-1} + 1
there. What T1 then adds is: (i) a PC refutation floor, which bounds every PC-simulable
solver on UNSAT draws (a first fall degree does not); (ii) no H-TOP or top-form analysis;
(iii) the chained presentation and any F_p-affine Boolean presentation; (iv) targets in
extension fields. The checker reports PC degree and d_ff side by side on every cell.

**Literature delta.** IPS99 prove the floor over the reals. FSTW16 (arXiv 1606.05050,
Cor. 5.4; retrieved by both gates) give a characteristic-p floor for a weaker measure: any
polynomial agreeing with 1/(sum x_i - beta) on the cube has degree >= n, for characteristic
>= poly(n), unit weights (static, Nullstellensatz-type; better constant, weaker proof system).
Behera-Limaye-Ramanathan-Srinivasan (arXiv 2506.16397, Lemma 2.2; retrieved at secondary
level) is an IPS-fragment bound for random weights. A dynamic-PC characteristic-p statement
was not found in the gates' searches; absence from a search is a floor, not proof. Novelty
status: unverified (adaptation-shaped; may be folklore).

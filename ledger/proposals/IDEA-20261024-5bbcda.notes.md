## 2. Mechanism and derivation

### 2.1 Objects and hypotheses

- Field and curves: p prime and F_p generic, with E: y^2 = x^3 + ax + b, generic j,
  arity m, K = 2^(m-1).
- Digits: x_i = sum_{j<s} W^j d_ij. At W = 2 the digits satisfy the field equations
  d^2 = d, and S~ is the multilinear (squarefree) reduction of
  S_{m+1}(x_1(d), ..., x_m(d), x_R).
- Representation and operation set: R1, with Sigma = translation by factor-base points
  (Class I, partial-action). The factor base is the digit box: KN-OPEN-020's
  implicit-membership class, with degree-W membership per digit variable.
- H1 (H-TOP at m = 5, 6): the coefficient c of prod_i x_i^K in S_{m+1} is a nonzero
  constant mod p. This holds at m = 2, 3, 4 (KN-OPEN-02200b).
- H2: sparse linear algebra on weight-m rows over F_N costs at least m*B^2 for B columns.
- H3 (= the dossier's H4w): in the cells where floor A + H2 stays below rho, at least a
  2^-80 / 2^-155 / 2^-226 / 2^-302 fraction (at 128 / 256 / 384 / 521 bits) of reduced
  monomials of block degree <= K has a nonzero coefficient mod p. The dossier's H4 is
  the strong form, "all such monomials are nonzero".
- Solver class: anything that consumes S~ in the 0/1 monomial basis. Such a solver pays
  at least |supp S~| per attempt (input-size bound).

Quantifier order. For all log2 p in {128, 256, 384, 521}, all m <= 14, all s with
2^s <= p, and every solver in the class:

  [H2 and (m >= 5 implies H1(m)) and (cell in the H3 band implies H3)] implies
  log2(attempts * |supp S~| + m (2^s/2)^2) - log2 sqrt(pi N/4) > 2.

H3 is quantified over the curve and target outside a Schwartz-Zippel exceptional set.

### 2.2 Uniqueness and restriction (Moebius)

Functions {0,1}^n -> F_p correspond one-to-one to multilinear polynomials over F_p. The
coefficient at J is

  sum_{I subset J} (-1)^{|J \ I|} f(1_I).

So supp S~ is an invariant of the function d -> S_{m+1}(x(d), x_R): no rewriting of the
same reduced system in the 0/1 basis is sparser (Nisan-Szegedy p. 302, read by the gate).

Setting a set of digits to 0 keeps exactly the monomials that avoid them, with their
coefficients unchanged. Two consequences:
- An exact support count on a subcube is a certified lower bound.
- The s'-digit presentation is the restriction of the s-digit one to d_{i,j} = 0 for
  j >= s'. One evaluator at s_max therefore serves every s' <= s_max, by sampling
  monomials supported on the first s' digits.

### 2.3 Floor A

S_{m+1} has degree K = 2^(m-1) in each variable. Its only monomial with all exponents
equal to K is c * prod x_i^K.

Substitute and reduce: a term x_1^{e_1} ... x_m^{e_m} contributes only to reduced
monomials whose block-i degree is at most e_i. Within one block,

  (sum_j 2^j d_j)^K, reduced,

has coefficient K! * prod_{j in J} 2^j on each K-subset J of [s]. Only the multinomial term
with K distinct factors reaches full degree; terms with repeats reduce to lower degree.

So the block-degree-(K, ..., K) part has exactly C(s, K)^m terms, each with coefficient
c * prod(K! prod 2^j). These are nonzero for p > K, p odd, given H1. For general W the
count is #{e in [0, W-1]^s : |e| = K}^m, with p > K and p not dividing W.

Baseline check: 5 * log2 C(54, 16) = 221.31 (recomputed by the gate and by the filer),
which equals EV-PFDR-f71d7f O-3.

Basis note (gate A3): floor A is unchanged under a per-variable affine re-encoding
d = a + b*u. The top block-multidegree part maps to itself times a nonzero scalar. This is
the gate's derivation sketch.

### 2.4 Floor B (needs H3)

With every reduced monomial of block degree <= K present, the count is
(sum_{t<=K} #{|e| = t})^m. For s <= K this is the full grid 2^(ms). Floor B is a statement
about the 0/1 basis only. In the +-1 encoding the same function can be sparse (Nisan-Szegedy
p. 302).

### 2.5 Cost model

Scripts: `qc/qc2_digit_writability_v2.py` and `qc/qc2_floorA_by_m.py`. In logs, base 2:

- lrho = (log2 N + log2(pi/4))/2
- relations: lrel = s*log2 W - 1 (W^s/2 relations, a lower bound)
- yield: lpi = min(0, m(s log2 W + 1) - log2 m! - log2 N) (yield from 2W^s signed points,
  an upper bound)
- attempts: latt = lrel - lpi
- linear algebra: lla = 2*lrel + log2 m (H2)
- total = attempts * support (+) LA, with the solver priced at zero
- margin = log2 total - lrho, minimised over m <= 14 and all s
- cells within +-2 bits are undecided

For m >= 15, K > s always, so floor B is the full grid. The total is then
>= B * m! * N / 2^(m+1) > rho under the dossier's H4. That is a model statement.

### 2.8 Certifying H3 by sampling, per cell (*derived (filer)* from the v2 formula)

The need of a band cell is need(m, s) = lrho - latt. It peaks where pi reaches 1, near
s* = (log2 m! + log2 N)/m - 1, not at s_max.

The dossier compared C(63, <=3)^7 = 2^107.4 (the count at s = 63) with the band maximum
2^90.8. That maximum sits at s = 38, where the block-degree-<=3 count is only 2^92.1. The
check has to be made per cell.

Tightest cells, comparing the count of block-degree-<=3 monomials with the need at the
same (m, s):

| log2 p | m | s at max need | need | block-degree-<=3 count | min nonzero fraction |
|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| 256 | 7 | 38 | 2^90.8 | 2^92.1 | 2^-1.3 (0.40) |
| 256 | 8 | 33 | 2^95.8 | 2^100.4 | 2^-4.6 |
| 256 | 9 | 30 | 2^98.8 | 2^109.3 | 2^-10.5 |
| 128 | 6 | 22 | 2^42.8 | 2^64.9 | 2^-22.0 |

Every other 128- and 256-bit band cell has at least 10 bits of room. At 384 and 521 bits
the needs (up to 2^163.5 and 2^222.3) cannot be met by monomials of block degree <= 3 or 4
at feasible evaluation cost, so those cells stay conditional (dossier).

Cost (*derived (filer)*):
- The coefficient at J costs 2^|J| evaluations, so block degree <= 3 means at most
  2^(3m) evaluations per coefficient: 2^18 to 2^24 for m = 6..8 (the dossier's figure).
- Each evaluation is a product over 2^(m-1) sign patterns, so a coefficient costs about
  2^(4m-1) point additions in F_{p^2}:

  | m | point additions per coefficient | per cell at n = 40 |
  |--:|--:|--:|
  | 6 | 8.4e6 | 3.4e8 |
  | 7 | 1.3e8 | 5.4e9 |
  | 8 | 2.1e9 | 8.6e10 |
  | 9 | 3.4e10 | 1.4e12 |

- At m >= 9 this is out of reach, which is why the sampler covers m = 6, 7, 8 only (the
  dossier's own scope). The 128/256-bit cells at m = 9..14 stay on unverified H3, and
  m >= 15 rests on the dossier's full-grid H4 argument.
- The dossier's "hours" of compute does not survive this count. At 1e6-1e7 point
  additions per second, the m = 8 cell alone takes roughly 2.4-24 core-hours per size.
  This is parallel work, and compute is rated medium.

Sample size (*derived (filer)*):
- n is set from each cell's need, not from a detection target. With n sampled
  coefficients all nonzero, the one-sided Clopper-Pearson 95% lower bound on the nonzero
  fraction is 0.05^(1/n).
- The tightest cell needs at least 0.40, which n = 4 already clears (0.47). n = 40 per cell
  (20 draws x 2 monomials) gives 0.928 and detects a zero fraction of 7.2% or more with
  probability at least 0.95.
- At (256, 7, 38), 0.928 x 2^92.15 = 2^92.04, above the need of 2^90.8.
- This is a statistical bound for the drawn curves and targets, not a certificate. Only
  exact subcube supports certify.
- The Moebius sum for a top monomial J also yields every coefficient below J in the same
  subcube at no extra cost. Those by-products are reported descriptively. They are not
  uniform over the band, so they do not enter the bound.

Evaluator requirement (checkable basis: S_3 = (x_1 - x_2)^2 x_3^2 - 2((x_1 + x_2)(x_1 x_2 + a) + 2b) x_3 + (x_1 x_2 - a)^2 - 4b(x_1 + x_2)):
- The product over the 2^(m-1) sign patterns of (x_R - x(P_1 +- ... +- P_m)) is monic in
  x_R. S_{m+1} is not: S_3 has x_3^2-coefficient (x_1 - x_2)^2.
- An evaluator must therefore multiply by the x_R-leading coefficient of S_{m+1}, or
  evaluate S_{m+1} exactly some other way. Otherwise it computes a different function, and
  every Moebius coefficient is wrong.
- The positive control (2.7 reproduced at m = 2, 3) guards against this.

## 4. Draft PROTOCOL (first step)

PROTOCOL-QC2-1, draft. `approved_by: null`. Two arms: Arm A gates Arm B's reporting.

**Arm A: H-TOP at m = 5 (H1).**
- Method: compute the coefficient of prod x_i^16 in S_6 by the one-variable
  specialisation that settled m = 4 (KN-OPEN-02200b positive route), over Z[a, b, x_R], or
  in 6 random (a, b, x_R) draws with x_R symbolic and then with a, b symbolic.
- Second implementation: an independent extraction route, as was done at m = 3.
- Output: total degree, per-variable degree, the top-form monomial list, and the
  coefficient. Predicted: total degree 80, per-variable degree 16, a single monomial with
  coefficient 1 (KN-OPEN-02200b). This is a prediction.

**Arm B: point-evaluation Moebius sampler (H3).**
- Instances: prime-order E/F_p with p near 2^127 and 2^255; random (a, b) and random
  target x_R; 20 (curve, target) draws per cell.
- Cells: m = 6, 7, 8 at 128 and 256 bits, each at its tightest s (section 2.8): (256, 7, 38),
  (256, 8, 33), (256, 6, 33) and (128, 6, 22); (128, 7) and (128, 8) at their tightest s,
  which are 19 and 17. m >= 9 is not sampled (cost table, section 2.8).
- Evaluator: one at s_max per m. Smaller s are reached by sampling monomials supported on
  the first s digits (restriction, section 2.2).
- Monomials: 2 per draw (n = 40 per cell), uniform over monomials of block degree <= 3
  supported in the cell's digits. Subcube by-products are recorded descriptively.
- Coefficient: sum over I subset J of (-1)^|J\I| S_{m+1}(x(1_I), x_R), so at most 2^(3m)
  evaluations.
- Exact S_{m+1} evaluation in F_{p^2}, including the x_R-leading coefficient (section 2.8).
- Controls, each a stop gate:
  - B0: reproduce `qc/qc2_h4_check.out` counts at m = 2 (s <= 8) and m = 3 (s <= 6).
  - B1: evaluator agrees with explicit S_3 and with S_4 = Res(S_3, S_3) at 1,000 random
    points.
  - B2: a planted zero (subtract the computed monomial) reads zero.
  - B3: at p = 65537 the zero rate is within Poisson limits of count/p.
  - B4 (descriptive): the +-1 re-encoding at m = 2, 3, reporting its support beside the
    0/1 support.
- Counting: zeros per draw; per-cell pooled zero fraction; one-sided Clopper-Pearson 95%;
  implied nonzero lower bound = CP bound x block-degree-<=3 count; compared with the
  need(m, s) recomputed from the v2 formula.
- Statistics: the cell is the unit; no pooling across cells; a structured-zero check
  tabulates zeros by block-pattern type.
- Isolation: exact arithmetic, deterministic seeds; any host; no timing claims.
- Freeze, before Arm B runs: sampler source SHA, seed list, cell list, monomial-sampling
  seed, primes, and the Arm A result.
- Evidence: per-draw JSONL (curve, target, J, coefficient), control outputs, the CP
  table, script hashes, and a second-implementation replay of 10% of coefficients.
- Stop: any failed control halts the run, and no H3 statement is made.
- Cost: about one day to implement (dossier). Compute is the dominant term: roughly
  2.4-24 core-hours for each m = 8 cell at 1e6-1e7 point additions per second
  (*derived (filer)*), and parallel.

1. **Moebius uniqueness and restriction.** Over a field F, the map from multilinear
   polynomials in n variables to functions {0,1}^n -> F is a linear bijection, with the
   coefficient at J given by the alternating sum. Setting a variable set to 0 sends the
   representation to its sub-sum over monomials avoiding that set.
2. **Floor A counting lemma.**
   - Inputs: a field of characteristic p > K, p odd; P in F[x_1..x_m] with deg_{x_i} P <= K
     for every i and coefficient c != 0 at prod x_i^K.
   - Claim: the multilinear reduction of P(sum_j 2^j d_1j, ..., sum_j 2^j d_mj) has exactly
     C(s, K)^m nonzero coefficients of block degree (K, ..., K), each equal to
     c * prod_i (K! prod_{j in J_i} 2^j).
   - H-TOP enters only as the hypothesis c != 0.
3. Not proposed for Lean: the cost-margin tables (a numeric model) and H-TOP at m = 5 (a
   symbolic computation, Arm A).

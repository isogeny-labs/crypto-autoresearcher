## Derivation A: absent-variable restriction

Let G be the ordered list of cleaned residuals in Fp[b_0,...,b_(n-1)]/(b_i^2-b_i), with p prime and nonnegative masks below 2^n. Define A as the union of supports of every monomial of every G_i, including generators above the chosen row-degree cap. Let I be its complement. The homomorphism pi setting all b_i for i in I to zero fixes every G_i. Therefore an identity 1=sum q_i G_i maps to 1=sum pi(q_i)G_i, and Boolean degree cannot rise. Line-by-line specialization of a PC derivation retains addition and scalar multiplication; active-variable multiplication remains valid and inactive-variable multiplication becomes zero. This is the familiar restriction principle, not new mathematics.

The converse certificate inclusion is immediate: a certificate in the active subring is also one in the full ring. Yet neither statement says that a particular incomplete algorithm discovers every such certificate. The size formula M(n,D)=sum_(j=0)^D binom(n,j), with impossible binomial terms zero, becomes M(|A|,D) for the active universe. This is a universe bound, not a measured matrix size or runtime law.

The model projection must retain all inactive bits as free extensions. Assigned prefix bits are fixed exactly as before; an unassigned bit absent from residuals is not fixed to zero in the SAT problem. Keeping original labels avoids a compact-map bug. Any later compact relabelling needs an explicit bijection, preservation of the chosen term order, inverse lifting of each certificate mask and direct checking against the original ordered residual list. The original full-assignment inverse-of-nonzero-constant fallback and original reference remain. Empty A with all-zero generators is UNKNOWN to refutation; a nonzero constant can have an inverse certificate subject to the declared cap. No cap bypass is smuggled into the local simulation claim.

## Derivation B: stronger cold-source simulation

This strengthening was supplied by root as an UNREVIEWED prompt hypothesis, then assessed against the bound source here. It is retained as a source-specific derivation awaiting independent proof and falsification controls. It is not an empirical result or a primary-source theorem.

For a monomial mask m write S=m intersect I. Call a nonzero polynomial S-homogeneous when every term has the same inactive mask S. Initially every G_i is empty-mask homogeneous; the unit multiplier in its initial certificate also is, and all other entries are zero. Inductively a queued pair (row, certificate vector) has a common inactive mask S for every nonzero term in every component. OR multiplication by an active variable preserves S; multiplication by an inactive variable replaces S by S union {i}. Exact lookup by pivot mask can only match a basis row in the same S block. Subtraction/scaling preserves the common mask, including cancellation to zero. A zero row is not queued. Thus no nonempty block can acquire constant pivot0 or change any empty-block row or multiplier.

The empty-block subsequence of initial insertions and FIFO processing has the same order as a cold active-only routine that loops through A in the original increasing variable order. Nonempty blocks append only nonempty children and cannot contribute an empty child. Filtering the full FIFO therefore produces the same accepted empty rows, normalized pivots and certificate arithmetic in the same order. Empty-block products also have the same degree tests. If the baseline reaches a refutation within its global attempted-row cap, active-only reaches that identical certificate after at most as many insertion attempts. It can use the saved budget to find a certificate where the baseline returned UNKNOWN. Hence neither equality of UNKNOWN outcomes nor equality of rank, multiplier-degree summaries, attempted-row summaries or whole-SAT trajectories follows.

This induction requires valid p/masks, the literal cold initializer, exact pivot selection/lookup, original generator and variable order, FIFO and unchanged cap semantics. It does not apply automatically after mixed-mask warm starts, row combinations chosen across blocks, randomized pivots, global rank-triggered policies, adaptive degree schedules or changed ordering. Even with the invariant, support extraction, set construction and branch work add cost. At A=all variables, there are no removable blocks: this is the required overhead-only null and defeats universal total-cost dominance. Arithmetic saved on one fixed residual does not establish a whole-search win when additional certificates change learned clauses and future SAT assignments.

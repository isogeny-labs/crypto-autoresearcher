Each arm's cost at price w is S_X(w) = G_X + w U_X, where G_X is the counted GAE and U_X

| Frobenius MITM / strong rho | 4.2709 | 4.1318 | 3.1951 | none in [0, 1] | holds | rankable: strong rho below Frobenius MITM at every band weight (*derived (filer)*) |

Arithmetic:
- w* = (G_rho - G_SAT)/(U_SAT - U_rho) = 5,987.014 / 2,742,178. If rho's natives are left
  unpriced, w* = 5,987.014 / 2,755,966 = 0.0021724.
- Crossing against Frobenius MITM: (174,894.135 - 34,963.343) / 2,755,966.

At which weights the SAT arm is above strong rho:
- **Above**, at w = 0.1 (7.34) and w = 1 (51.0), and for every w > 1/458.
- **Below**, at w = 0 (0.854; the cluster interval excludes 1) and for w < 1/458.

Because propagations are unpriced, "above" is a closure-direction statement (the true
SAT cost is higher still). "Below at w = 0" holds on counted work, since at w = 0 every
unpriced count is priced at 0 anyway.

# EXP-PEXT-bf8f96: prospective C5 review roster

The [selector](../../implementation/c5_selection.py) consumes one complete, canonical-order metadata row per published class/T unit. It refuses missing or reordered units, wrong torsion/T identity, malformed certificate hashes, inconsistent special-j or curve-counterexample flags within an actual curve, and duplicate witness ordinals. It selects the first, lower middle and last class/T unit in every occupied `(cohort, torsion_count, T_index, descent_status)` stratum. Every unit flagged as a unit or curve counterexample, every T unit of a special-j class, and every unit carrying a Type2 hyperelliptic witness is also selected. It retains all flagged structure ordinals and binds the roster to hashes of the join plan and metadata table.

Two focused software tests pass on a synthetic seven-class plan, checking canonical picks, forced witnesses and rejection of incomplete or inconsistent metadata. A shape-only exercise over the four published join plans gives 504, 1,378, 5,322 and 974 class/T units respectively, matching 8,178 total. With source-derived special-j flags, placeholder `unresolved` descent status and no counterexample or Type2 flags, each plan has seven occupied torsion/T strata and the roster sizes are 31, 32, 29 and 22. Those placeholders are **not** certified metadata or an actual C5 sample; real descent states and flagged cases can change the roster substantially. The separate [source-derived special-j report](../c5-special-j-20261105/report.md) records the exact flag check.

The selector deliberately receives no producer genus or minimum values. The future supervised stage must derive and independently validate certificate-bound metadata, write it once, validate all 8,178 entries and their hashes, seal the blind roster before exposing producer outputs to the second implementation, and retain independent recomputation and comparison records under the stage deadline. The current code does not implement those producers, custody steps or scientific checks. The experiment remains `review_required` and unfrozen.

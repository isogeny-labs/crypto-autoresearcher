# Exceptional descent: exact classification and admission obligations

**Scope:** Prospective design correction for H-PEXT-c3a11d / EXP-PEXT-bf8f96. No scientific cohort or benchmark ran. The experiment remains `review_required` and unfrozen.

## Order-n lift is an existence obligation, not a negative test result

Let K/k be the prescribed finite cyclic extension of odd degree n in {3,5,7}, and L=K(x)(sqrt(f_0),...,sqrt(f_(n-1))) the field of the checked coherent square-class span. Coefficient Frobenius permutes the square classes, so L/k(x) is normal and the restriction map from its Galois group onto Gal(K/k)=C_n has a 2-group kernel. Pick any element over the generator sigma. Its order has the form n*2^t. By the Chinese remainder theorem, some power of that element is congruent to 1 modulo n and 0 modulo 2^t; this power is an order-n lift of sigma. This is also the normal-Hall complement consequence of [Schur–Zassenhaus](https://people.brandeis.edu/~igusa/Math131b/SZL.pdf), Theorem 13.7. The argument requires the exact Frobenius-stable Kummer extension and does not by itself certify geometric connectedness or a particular model.

The current `candidate_lift` tries signs for one coherent relation-witness presentation. A solver miss cannot certify that no descent exists. It now raises an instrument error; `source_radical_binding` refuses any non-exhibiting result. Positive order-n certificates remain checked as before. A formal Reviewer must challenge the field-extension premise, sign solver completeness, and the use of the existence theorem before this is promoted to a final descent classifier.

## Complete exceptional-row bridge

`source_fixed_unit.assess` already checks one published class/T/ordinal exceptional row, its exact source map, fixed multiplication, component geometry and independent replays. It takes a free row argument. The missing production stage must consume sealed candidate bytes and their full independent review, check exact row/raw-hash/plan identity for every exceptional ordinal, retain each checked outcome, and bind the generic complement certificate. A separate checker must replay the full ordinal partition and digests before deriving a unit admissible minimum or C5 status. The blind C5 reviewer receives identities and flags, with producer genus and minima withheld until independent recomputation is sealed.

A full fixed multiplication table for every exceptional row is not yet resource-qualified. The q11/n3 plan assigns 5,322 T units to a 28,800-second stage, allowing 5.41 seconds per unit for all construction, checking and storage. Applying existing *synthetic* candidate counts by matching factor pattern yields a workload scenario of 81,749,543 exceptional rows before the [1,3] and [4] units. This is neither an actual-cohort count nor a lower bound. A compact source map, square-relation/component-genus and positive order-n action certificate for each row, with full fixed-model replay on C5-selected rows, is the next design route. Its independent checker, exact status semantics, source-bound throughput and storage fit remain to be established under the unchanged six-run, 4 GB, 24 CPU-hour, 24-hour, zero-cloud-dollar and no-retry ceilings.

# EXP-PEXT-bf8f96: generic branch-support certificate

The exact support classifier now separates a verified Frobenius orbit of squarefree binary quartics into a generic disjoint case and an overlap case. In the generic case, every nonempty product of the (n) forms has a simple branch point unique to one selected form. The geometric and arithmetic square-class ranks are therefore both (n), the elementary (2^n)-cover is connected, and Riemann–Hurwitz gives geometric genus ((n-1)2^n+1). This is a checked component construction for (n=3,5,7), conditional on the input forms being the already bound conjugate orbit of an actual quotient.

The classifier checks each form's finite squarefreeness and simple projective infinity, exact Frobenius conjugacy and closure, then the polynomial gcd of every pair plus their common-infinity condition. It reports the colliding pair and finite gcd degree for an exceptional case. Exceptional rows receive no inferred rank, genus or descent label; those require the full calculation.

On the complete **F27 fixed software fixture**, 1,358 of 1,638 joined rows had disjoint supports and 280 had an overlap. For every disjoint row, the existing separate square-relation implementation returned only the zero relation, matching rank three and genus 17. Repeated-root, shared-infinity and broken-conjugacy inputs were rejected or routed to the exceptional case as appropriate. [The source-bound software log](software-tests.json) records commands, Python/Rust versions, source hashes and output; [the editable figure source](render_figure.py) draws [the SVG](figure.svg) from that log. These are control results, not a full P3 cohort or a throughput estimate.

This lemma makes a count of the generic complement sufficient for its **geometric** rank and genus. The next construction is an exact generator for every exceptional structure index with an independent completeness certificate, duplicate elimination and target/curve provenance. Only then can the generic complement be counted without visiting each of the billions of class/T/structure positions. The complete P1–P4 and H1 contract still needs branch-type, exceptional rank, connectedness, twist, descent and Type2 checks. EXP-PEXT-bf8f96 remains `review_required`; no protocol was frozen and no scientific trial was launched.

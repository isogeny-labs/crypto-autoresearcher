# Deviations (SA-08b)
1. Reduction: BKZ-20 (4 loops, no pruning strategies file available in this fpylll build; mpfr/300-bit GSO when bits*K >= 130 after the double-precision run segfaulted with "infinite loop in babai" at 96 bits, K=3,4; those two configs were rerun). lambda_1, lambda_2 are the two shortest vectors of the reduced basis, not exact SVP/SVP_2. Calibration at 48 bits, K=4 (LLL vs BKZ-30 vs BKZ-20): the 50% crossing moves by < 0.001 in delta.
2. Scale: K up to 6 at 48 and 64 bits (dim 169), K up to 4 at 96 bits (K = 5 at 96 bits did not finish in 300 s; K = 6 at 96 and K >= 7 not run). 2 instances per (point, planted/random); at 64 bits K=6 only 1 instance per type on a narrowed grid 0.12-0.16. Grid step 0.01 (linear interpolation), so a crossing is good to about +-0.002.
3. "All down-closed supports": only two families run (box [0,2K]^2 and the truncated simplex u+v <= 3K); not all down-closed sets. Simplex runs: 48 bits, K = 2..5, no 64/96-bit simplex.
4. Crossing definition (fixed before analysis): smallest delta (on the grid) at which mean(log lambda_2) - log(p^K / sqrt(n)) turns >= 0, n = dimension, B = floor(p^delta), Euclidean norm of the B^(u+v)-scaled coefficient vector. Random instances = random r (no small solution); the lattice depends on r only through f.
5. Curve fixed to y^2 = x^3 + 7 (as in SA-08); SA-08 scripts planted.py reused (imported unmodified).
6. A second extrapolation estimator (1/6 + extrapolated excess over the exact determinant threshold) was added after seeing that a plain 1/K fit of the crossings is biased low by about 0.01 (it does the same to the exact determinant values). The frozen criterion is judged on both; the verdict is the same.

# Deviations from the frozen SA-03 protocol

1. **Target counts shrink with B.** The prereg asks for 200 targets. 200 planted targets (plus 30 random controls) were run whenever the expected square-leading count 4B^10/p was <= 1e7; 30 targets for <= 4e7; 6 targets for <= 3.5e8. Larger B were not enumerable on this host share.
2. **Exact counting without enumeration for larger B.** For B beyond what point-by-point enumeration allows (up to B=128 at p~2^32, B=91 at 2^28, B=64 at 2^24) only N_sq was counted exactly (`census2 mode 2`, 12 planted targets per cell; identical to the enumerating mode on the cells where both were run, checked on 6 instances). N_both and N_dec for those cells were NOT enumerated; there the ratio is only bounded as 1/N_sq (planted vector gives N_both >= 1). Enumerated cells show N_dec = 1 on 100% of planted targets.
3. **N_pos and N_Dall (all-leading-coefficient counts) only at small B** (mode 1, expected N_pos <= 3e6). For larger B the "small forms" count uses the square-leading subset only, which is the denominator of the frozen criterion anyway.
4. **Extra primes for (b).** The by-discriminant sweep was run at p ~ 2^32 (in the prereg range) and, additionally, at p ~ 2^48 and 2^64 so that B^5 << p and the pure per-Delta cost could be separated from the candidate blow-up that appears at 2^32 for B >= 64.
5. **p chosen as the first prime >= 2^k with p = 3 (mod 4)** (cheap square roots). At p~2^28 and some B, fewer than two x <= B have f(x) a QR, so the cell was skipped (28/B=6,8,11); the 2^28 series therefore starts at B=16.
6. **Sieve (b-ii) is a cost model on measured survival, not an implemented coset enumerator.** Survival fractions of square-leading lattice vectors under the constraint "(c0,c1,c2) mod q lies in the image of (x1,x2) -> S3 coefficients" were measured for q = 3,5,7,11,13 on the census vectors and match the exact prediction; the cost of sieving with a prime set was then modelled as (#image cosets = product of |Im_q|, each charged 1 operation) + (surviving candidates). This is a lower bound on the cost of any coset-based implementation.
7. **S_B uses pairs x1 < x2** (x1 = x2 gives c2 = 0, outside the c2 > 0 normalisation). Forms are normalised to c2 > 0 (g and -g identified); c2 = 0 forms are excluded.
8. **Gamma_0(p) correspondence** is derived and cited (elementary orbit count); only its computable consequences were tested numerically. No Heegner-point or closed-geodesic computation was run.
9. The prereg's prediction sentence says "p^{-o(1)} fraction"; this reads as a typo for "vanishing fraction". The verdict uses only the frozen kill criterion (B^{-c}, c >= 1).

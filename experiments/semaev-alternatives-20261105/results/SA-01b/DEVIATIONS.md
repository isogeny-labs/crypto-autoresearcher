The protocol's fallback (exact MILP at t >= 4 with Groebner generators plus the polytope formula) was used instead. Elapsed in this step: a few minutes, not 30; the install route itself was closed.
2. **Same substitute tool as SA-01.** All "certified" values are finite-t optima of the SA-01 shift-set MILP/LP class (down-closed M, product-of-powers shifts, one leading-monomial choice = lex lead of each generator; for (ii) the all-ones weight lead). They are constructions in that class, not cuso/sumset certificates.
3. **Scale reached below the protocol's t = 4..6 for (iii) and (iv).** (iii)-Groebner (m = 3, variables a2,e1,e2,e3 after eliminating a1 linearly) reached t = 3 (t = 4 ran out of memory in the LP: dim 30345, `std::bad_alloc` at 2.5 GB). The t = 4 point in the (iii) series is the Vieta-only value (the e-only Groebner element is in the ideal, so (iii) >= (ii) by ignoring a2); it is a bound, not a computed (iii) value. (iv)-Groebner computed t = 2, 3 with extra generators; t = 4..6 are the (i) values, valid lower bounds because S_4(x) is in the chain ideal, not computed with u.
4. **Subsets of Groebner generators.** (iii) at t = 3 used generators {8, 9} (lex-GB indices; 9 = Vieta S_4, 8 = a2 e1^4-type element); all 9 e/a2 generators ran at t <= 2 (6237 grid points) and ran out of memory at t = 3 (killed). At t = 2 only generators 7 and 8 beyond Vieta raise the LP (0.01852 -> 0.01922); generators 1-6 do not. For (iv): generators {2, 7} (u-linear element, S_4) at t = 2, 3; t = 2 with 2..7 gave LP 0.01758 vs 0.01711 for {2, 7}.
5. **MILP time limit.** 60 s per MILP call. (ii) m = 3 t = 4 value 0.02755 is a lower bound (MILP at 0.02784 returned no solution within the limit; LP upper 0.03141).
6. **(iv) raw chained generators.** At t = 4 the LP was evaluated at one value, delta = 0.002 (ratio 0.54 < 1). For generators whose cost is delta times a fixed exponent vector the LP ratio is exactly proportional to 1/delta, so ratio < 1 at delta = 0.002 means the LP ceiling is below 0.002. t = 5 did not finish in 480 s. The "ratio tends to 0.8" statement is a fit to four points and is not a bound.
7. **m = 4 (i) by orbit reduction.** The LP is symmetrised over S_4 (valid: the LP and its objective are symmetric, so an optimal solution can be averaged). Checked against the full LP at t = 2 (0.39283533 both) and against SA-01's m = 3 t = 3 value (0.021620 vs 0.021533 from SA-01's bisection, tolerance 2e-4). Reach = ratio x delta (exact for pure-delta costs). t = 4 build ran out of memory. The box LP equals the MILP (checked in SA-01 at m = 3).

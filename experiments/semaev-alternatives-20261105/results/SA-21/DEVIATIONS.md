# SA-21 deviations from the frozen pre-registration (all declared before the verdict was formed)

1. **Reference engine not used.** `isogeny-labs/crypto` (prime_fast.rs, Table B) was not cloned. All arms are new C (`ic.c`), sharing one Montgomery field and one affine group law with batch inversion. t_GAE is measured in the same code (12.7 to 17 ns, batched, W = 256).
2. **Q is placed in every relation; no separate descent phase.** A relation is c(a + b k) = e1 l_v1 + e2 l_v2 with R = aG + bQ. The weight-2 system is solved by weighted union-find elimination (exact sparse Gaussian elimination over F_l), which yields k from the first cycle that involves k. The "descent" phase is therefore only the final check [k]G = Q, which every solve passes. This is cheaper than factor-base-logs-then-descent, so it favours IC.
3. **Curves.** Weierstrass family: y^2 = x^3 + x + 1 over F_p, #E prime. Edwards family: Montgomery v^2 = u^3 + 6u^2 + u (twisted Edwards a = 8, d = 4, SA-19 curve), implemented as y^2 = x^3 - 11x + 14 with x = u + 2 and y_E = (x - 3)/(x - 1); group order 4 l with l prime, G of order l. The lattice arm for this family uses the cofactor trick 4R = 4P1 +/- 4P2 so vertices are classes of 4P. Group sizes: l has k or k+1 bits (curves.json records the exact l); p is about N for W and about 4N for E. Only one coefficient set per family (small constants are required by the form lattices); the 3 curves per size differ in p. Sizes 20..40 in steps of 2 (rho, pair); lattice arms only 20/22..32 (see 5).
4. **B for the lattice arms** is calibrated per curve in C: planted pairs from the factor base, 3000 trials per B, largest B whose recovery is at least 50% with the stop rule "4 consecutive B below 50%", and a factor base of at least 3 entries (a 2-entry base gives a single pair and a 0/1 recovery rate). Where no such B exists the arm is not run on that curve (recorded as `skipped` in lat.jsonl: latW at k = 22 (2 of 3 curves), 24 (2/3), 28 (1/3); latE at k = 20 (1/3); latW was not attempted at k = 20, where 2 of 3 curves have no admissible B). The calibration is not charged to the solve.
5. **Lattice arms were not run at sizes 34 to 40**; one solve at k = 32 costs 6 to 14 minutes. Sample sizes: latW k = 22/24: 5, 26: 15, 28: 10, 30: 9, 32: 3; latE k = 20: 10, 22/24/26/28: 15, 30: 9, 32: 3. Sizes 34 to 40 are given only as a clearly labelled model (analysis.md), fitted on the measured hit rates.
6. **Oracle implementation.** The 3-dimensional form lattices (W: (A, B, C) coefficient vector of S_3 in x_R, budget 9; E: SA-19 degree sequence [2, 2, 4], budget 8) are reduced with a native 3-dimensional LLL (double-precision Gram-Schmidt, delta 0.99; checked to give the same recovery rates as a long-double version). The reduced vector is mapped to (x1, x2) by a hash of normalised primitive coefficient vectors over all factor-base pairs, instead of solving a polynomial system; a hit is then verified in the group (P_i +/- P_j = +/-R). All hits were verified and every solve is checked by [k]G = Q.
7. **IC-coppersmith-E (arm 5) was not run end to end.** Per-call cost is measured (fpylll LLL only, dim 13, t = 2, 32-bit prime: 0.30 ms, 420 to 500 times the form-lattice call) with its planted reach; the total cost is a model. The model is a lower bound (lattice build and root recovery excluded).
8. **Tuning, all on the measured curves.** IC-pair F = 0.5 N^(1/3) (pilot over kappa in 0.125..4 at k = 24..40 on W curves, flat optimum between 0.35 and 0.7; F capped by memory at about 7000, not reached). Rho: table 2^5/2^7/2^9 entries by size, walker count 8..256, DP rate sqrt(N)/(8W), cycle escape. The rho first version had a large fixed overhead (DP table allocation, 512 slow-built table entries); it was fixed and rho was re-run (all rho rows in results/rho.jsonl are from the second version). Pair, rho and lattice runs were not mixed across binaries except as listed in item 9.
9. **Re-run of one censored solve.** latE k = 32 curve 0 target 0 hit the 600 s cap in the first batch; it was re-run with the same seed and the cap raised to 6000 s (same RNG stream, completed in 839 s). The completed record replaces the censored one; the 600 s record is kept in lat.jsonl with `timeout: 1`.
10. **Pricing.** The prereg band is implemented as cost_pi = group additions + (lattice oracle wall + linear-algebra wall) / t_GAE + pi * (other unpriced field operations), pi in {0, 0.1, 1}. For IC-pair the other native work is one lookup per attempt, one insert per table entry, plus counted field multiplications beyond 6 per addition (slow inversions, Euler tests); for rho it is the counted multiplications beyond 6 per addition. Total wall time / t_GAE is reported separately ("wall") and is not part of the band. Field multiplications in the raw rows are analytic (6 per batched addition, 70 per slow inversion, 140 per square-root test).
11. **Host.** Two executors shared the host; load0/load1 are recorded per solve (0.2 to 1.06). Every timed solve ran single-threaded under `nice -n 10`, one batch at a time. The pair rows were measured while the load was 0.45 to 0.86; wall times of the memory-bound pair arm at k >= 36 carry host noise that cannot be separated from the arm.
12. **Protocol 3 per size x 5 targets** holds for rho (3 curves x 5 targets at every size and family) and IC-pair; lattice arms have fewer targets (item 5). Bootstrap: two-level (curves, then targets within a curve), 1500 resamples; alpha is fitted on log2 of the per-size mean cost against mean log2 l.

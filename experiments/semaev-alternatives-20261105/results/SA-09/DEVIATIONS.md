# Deviations from the frozen SA-09 protocol

1. Curve: only y^2 = x^3 + 1 (36a1, X_0(36) = E, modular degree 1). y^2 = x^3 + 7 (conductor 1728) was not run. Reason: scope and host load; the structural counting does not depend on the curve.
2. Heegner points were not computed with ellheegner (rank-1 curves over Q only) or bnrclassfield/nfinit. Instead: complex-analytic evaluation of the modular parametrisation at CM points (ellan + ellztopoint), Galois labelling by form class group composition, Lagrange interpolation, and rational recognition over K = Q(sqrt(-23)). Reason: ellheegner returns a single point over Q, and number-field machinery of degree 2*h(O_35) = 288 is infeasible.
3. Primes are about 2^19 (530251, 535481, 539009), not about 2^20, to keep every prime inside the first admissible hits for K[35]. 
4. Conductors: {1,5,7,35} only (n <= 30 coprime to 6, l inert and <= 7 gives n = 1,5,7 with the chain to 35; n = 11, 13, ... with l = 5 or 7 give h >= 216 and were not attempted). Single imaginary quadratic field K = Q(sqrt(-23)).
5. Conductor 35 (144 points) not reduced to F_p. Recognition of the Lagrange coefficients at 700 digits failed (errors ~1e-461, i.e. garbage-level bestappr output, heights above ~230 digits); a 1600-digit rerun (`build35.py`) was launched in the background under host load.
   Status after resume: the 1600-digit run recognised the level-35 coefficients (max error 1e-1558, at most 446 digits) but was cut off before writing its output (container restart). The resumed executor re-ran the level-35 algebraisation as `build35b.py` (1600 digits), reusing the conductor-35 neighbour lists from `nb_struct.json` (120 digits) with a full-precision spot check of the first fibre of each edge, and reduced it mod p with `analyze35.py`; outcome in REPORT.md, Table 1b.
6. "Homogeneous rank" interpreted as the rank over Q of the relation matrix with the y_n columns included (a_5 = 0 makes the l = 5 relations purely homogeneous; the l = 7 relations are inhomogeneous in y_n).
7. The hidden-relation control uses LLL on lattice coordinates in C/Lambda (260 digits), not an F_p computation.
8. The wrong-embedding control was run at level 7 rather than level 5, because a_5 = 0 makes the level-5 relations insensitive to the embedding.
9. Interrupted and resumed. The original executor was cut off by an API usage limit; the run was resumed on 2026-11-05 by a different model (Claude Opus 5.5) acting as SA-09. The resumed executor reused all finished outputs, completed item 5 above, and checked REPORT.md against the raw outputs.

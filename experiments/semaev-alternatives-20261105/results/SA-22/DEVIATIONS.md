2. Pricing band reading: "unpriced work" = linear algebra and descent (charged lambda in {0, 0.1, 1} GAE per field op). Measured oracle calls (LLL, msolve) are charged at the physical host rate (GAE = 14 field mults). A free-oracle bound is reported separately because lambda = 0 on the oracle makes any m >= 4 decomposition oracle win trivially.
3. Convention: B counts factor-base points (both signs), B/2 columns and relations, attempts m!N/B^m. Reach cap is x <= N^delta, so B <= 2 N^delta. Cross-checked against SA-15's break-even table (3.1/22/80/452 vs 6.8/26/84/456 GAE at 40/56/72/96 bits; the 40-bit gap is the linear-algebra term SA-15 omits).
4. Field-op speed (60 ns at 256 bits, scaling n^1.6) is a gmpy2 loop with interpreter overhead removed by estimate; msolve field op taken as 3 ns (31-bit). No SA-21 clean timing used. Time was short (micro-benchmarks only, one core, about 4 minutes).
5. m=3 ladder dimension (t+1)^3 is a favourable underestimate; the m=3 and msolve laws are extrapolations of 3 to 4 points.

# Index-calculus leaderboard

Generated by `scripts/build_ic_leaderboard.py` from the frozen ranked evidence subset; the page is [`docs/ic-leaderboard.html`](../ic-leaderboard.html).  Curves are named by ICV1 slug ([`docs/curves/ICV1.md`](../curves/ICV1.md)).  No row of tables A–C is below its reference; the GHS-weak section is a special family outside them and is below its matched rho.

**There is no single unit on this page.**  The board is three tables, A, B and C.  Each is priced in its own unit, against its own reference and floor, stated at its head.  `S`, `× reference` and `× floor` are comparable within a table and nowhere else.  The same slug can appear in two tables with different `S`, because the unit, the reference and the number of targets differ. Cold IC / rho is a cost ratio: **1.0× is parity**, greater than 1.0× means IC costs more. `2^x` GAE appears only where frozen whole-pipeline counted group-addition equivalents exist; a dash is not zero operations.

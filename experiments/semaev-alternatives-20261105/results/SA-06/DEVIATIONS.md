# Deviations (SA-06)

1. Automated Coppersmith calculators were not used. One honest attempt each: `pip install cuso` (no matching distribution on the index in this sandbox) and `pip install flatter` (wheel build failed). As instructed, the asymptotic Jochemsz-May reach was derived from the Newton polytopes by hand (closed form delta = 1/((n+1) * sum of centroid coordinates), derived in REPORT.md and checked against exact finite-t lattice-point sums, agreement within 1.2% after 1/t extrapolation). The sumset calculator, Ehrhart check and LP cross-check of Idea 1 were not run. Consequence: numbers are "reach of the JM-basic lattice plus a numerical search over extended shift regions", not provably optimal-shift certificates. The strategy-independent bounds (1/21.8 for S_4, 1/70.8 for S_5) are quoted from the campaign report, not re-derived.
2. The extended-shift search (l2.py) is a Monte Carlo optimisation over regions K = Newt(f) + box, with 3 leading-vertex choices and ~25 Nelder-Mead iterations per cell (reduced from 6 vertices / 40 iterations after the first attempt was too slow on the loaded host and was stopped). It was run only where the ambient dimension is at most 4; the 5-variable all-small S_5 cells have JM-basic values only. MC noise on the ratio is about 0.1%.
3. Planted fpylll runs cover S_3 only (as instructed), at one prime size (96 bits), JM-basic levels t = 2 and 3, 24 trials per point, six cells. Earlier pilot runs at 64 and 80 bits (a buggy first version of the lattice builder that reduced rows mod p^t, and a timing test) were discarded and are not reported.
4. The symmetrised polynomials were obtained by tensor-grid interpolation modulo a 61-bit prime with a random target x_R (interpolation verified at a fresh point in every cell), not by symbolic elimination. Supports are therefore "nonzero mod a 61-bit prime", which equals the integer support except with probability about 1e-17 per coefficient.
5. Automorphism (mu_3 at j=0, mu_4 at j=1728) symmetrisation was not computed; only the analytic degree count is given (REPORT.md, Limitations), marked sketched.
6. Supports of plain S_3..S_5 were computed for numeric (a, b) per cell (generic (3,5) and (-7,11), j=0 b=7, j=1728 a=7), not with symbolic a, b. The two generic curves gave identical monomial counts (13, 439, 54777).

# DEVIATIONS (SA-15b)

1. **msolve cannot take the instances.** msolve 0.10.1 rejects characteristic >= 2^31 ("characteristic must be 0 or < 2^31", verified), and the SA-15 primes are >= 2^40. Substitute: msolve on the SA-04 norm presentation with the vanishing-polynomial base, m = 4, on a scaled replica with p = nextprime(B^5) < 2^31 (B = 4, 8, 16, 32, 64; random curve from SA-04's builder, not y^2 = x^3 + 7; 60 s cap, 3 seeds until first timeout). Recovery on the real set is recorded as 0 because the solver is not executable there.
2. **S_5 lattice not executed.** The t = 1 lattice has dimension 9^4 = 6561 (S_5's projection onto x_1..x_4 is the full box [0,8]^4, SA-06). Estimated basis 1.1-2.3 GB, over the ~1.5 GB limit, and LLL at that dimension is far outside the 60 s cap. Instead the exact Howgrave-Graham margin is computed (s5_analytic.py): -128 bits (2^40) and -179 bits (2^56) of log2(p) - log2(det^(1/dim)). SA-01 certifies reach 0.0 for presentation (i) at m = 4, t = 1, and the asymptotic reach is 1/80, against the 1/5 needed.
3. **Vieta lattice run on subsets**, not all 100 per cell: 60 instances at 2^40 and 20 at 2^56 (about 8 s each under host load). The lattice shape and the margin depend on (p, B) only, so every instance has the same margin. t = 2 would have dimension C(20,4) = 4845 and was not attempted.
4. **SA-16 solvers (vieta_cvp, L2diag, L3) are two-unknown solvers** that need a degenerate conic (vieta_cvp) or R = [2]P (L2diag). They were (a) run as positive controls on their native planted families at B = floor(p^(1/5)) (40 trials per cell, fresh primes p = 3 mod 4 near 2^40 and 2^56, not the SA-15 primes), and (b) applied directly to all 400 SA-15 instances; a 2-decomposition cannot count as a 4-decomposition, and none was returned either way.
5. **Pair table scope.** Pairs with equal x (a doubling or P + (-P)) are skipped, so decompositions with a repeated x are not searched; the planted instances have distinct x. The scan stops at the first hit. Cost is counted in group additions (one add per pair-table entry, one add per scanned entry). The 2^72 and 2^96 cells were not run: the pair table has about B^2/2 = 2.3e8 entries at 2^72 (over 7 GB), and the prereg only asks for those sizes if a non-generic solver recovers something at 2^56, which none did.
6. Pair-table solver is a C program (pairtable.c, gcc -O2) driven from Python; the group-law verification of every hit is done independently in Python.

# SA-19 (new direction from SA-17)

**Why.** SA-17 showed that the lattice wall is the degree budget Σ_l deg c_l of the relation polynomial written as Σ c_l(small unknowns)·M_l(target coordinates): 2 + 3 + 4 = 9 for S_3 in x_R (reach 1/9 at m = 2) and 50 for S_4 (reach 1/50 at m = 3), in both F_p and F_q[t]/(π). Any lattice route must use a relation with a smaller budget. This experiment searches for one.

**Protocol.**
1. Define the budget precisely: for a polynomial F in the ideal I_R = {(x_1, …, x_m; target coordinates) : P_1 + … + P_m = R}, written F = Σ_l c_l(x)·M_l(R) with monomials M_l in the target's coordinates (x_R, y_R, or any coordinate functions of R computable from R), the form lattice has dimension = number of M_l and predicted reach δ < 1/Σ_l deg c_l (weighted by smallness). Planted vector = (c_l(x)).
2. Search, at m = 2, over:
   a. S_3 in other curve models (twisted Edwards in y, Montgomery x-only, Hessian, Jacobi intersection, Huff), with the small factor base defined in that model's coordinate;
   b. relations using both x_R and y_R (target fully known), including Gröbner-basis elements of I_R that are linear or quadratic in (x_R, y_R) with coefficients in x_1, x_2 only;
   c. relations using a different target coordinate (e.g. x(2R), x(R + T) for a fixed known T, or the pair (x_R, x(R + T))) so that several known coordinates of R give several equations for the same small x, i.e. a multi-equation lattice;
   d. symmetric-function coefficients (Vieta) as a control (expected budget 9).
3. For each candidate compute the budget exactly (Gröbner basis / elimination with a degree-weighted order, mod a 61-bit prime) and the predicted reach. For every candidate with budget < 9, run planted fpylll recovery at 64 and 96 bits, 20 trials per δ, with the analogue of SA-17's form lattice, and verify every recovered (x_1, x_2) in the group.
4. Repeat the best candidates at m = 3 against the budget 50.
5. Prove a lower bound on the budget if you can (for example via the genus of the fibre or degree of the map (x_1, x_2) → R), marking proved, sketched or conjectured.

**Prediction (frozen).** No relation in families a–d has budget < 9 at m = 2; multi-equation lattices (c) can lower the effective budget only by sharing unknowns across equations, and the gain is at most a constant factor below 2.

**Criteria (frozen).**
- SUCCESS: a relation or multi-equation lattice with measured planted-recovery threshold > 1/9 + 0.02 at m = 2 (64 and 96 bits, ≥ 10/20 recovery), or > 1/50 + 0.005 at m = 3.
- KILL: every candidate has budget ≥ 9 (m = 2) and no planted threshold exceeds 1/9 + 0.01, and a proved or well-supported lower bound of 9 is given.
- INCONCLUSIVE: otherwise.

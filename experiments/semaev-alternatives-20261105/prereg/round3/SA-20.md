# SA-20 (follow-up to SA-19)

**Why.** SA-19 found that the twisted-Edwards y-coordinate lowers the single-relation form-lattice budget from 9 to 8 (measured reach 0.125–0.127 vs 0.112–0.114). The modulus-p^k Coppersmith ladder has a ceiling of 1/6 for Weierstrass S_3 (SA-01/06/08/18) and was outside SA-19's scope. Its ceiling is set by the Newton polytope of the polynomial in the small unknowns, and the Edwards polynomial's polytope differs: all odd-degree monomials in (y_1, y_2) vanish.

**Protocol.**
1. Take SA-19's integer Edwards S_3 (a = 8, d = 4; y_1²y_2²y_3² − (y_1²y_2² + y_1²y_3² + y_2²y_3²) + 2(y_1² + y_2² + y_3²) − 2y_1y_2y_3 − 2) and its S_4 (m = 3). Compute the Newton polytope in the small unknowns and the asymptotic Jochemsz–May reach with the SA-06 centroid formula, for the box support and for the lattice of even-degree monomials (substituting z_i = y_i² where the support allows).
2. Exact finite-t certified reach with SA-01's MILP (`model.py`, `coppersmith_lp.py`) at m = 2, t = 1..8, and m = 3, t = 1..4; fit D − a/t − b/t² with leave-one-out ranges.
3. Planted fpylll recovery at m = 2 on SA-19's Edwards curve, 64 and 96 bits, at shift depth t = 2, 3, 20 trials per δ in steps of 0.005, with the Weierstrass lattice at the same t as control.

**Prediction (frozen).** The Edwards ladder ceiling at m = 2 equals 1/6 (the even-monomial structure does not change the centroid-weighted reach), and the finite-t values track the Weierstrass ones within 0.01.

**Criteria (frozen).**
- SUCCESS: asymptotic or extrapolated reach > 1/6 + 0.01 at m = 2 (or > 1/24 + 0.005 at m = 3), confirmed by planted recovery ≥ 10/20 at a δ above the Weierstrass control's 50% crossing by ≥ 0.01 at the same t.
- KILL: asymptotic reach ≤ 1/6 + 0.003 at m = 2 and ≤ 1/24 + 0.002 at m = 3, and planted crossings within 0.01 of the Weierstrass control at the same t.
- INCONCLUSIVE: otherwise.

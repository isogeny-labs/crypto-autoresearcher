Title: Machine-checked first-fall-degree reasoning in Weil-descent ECDLP
Keywords: first fall degree, last fall degree, Weil descent, summation polynomials, ECDLP, Groebner bases, Lean, formal verification
Category: Foundations

Subexponential ECDLP estimates over small-characteristic fields rest on small
first fall degrees of Weil-descent systems, and several arguments replace the
true first fall degree with an easier quantity. We give three Lean-checked
results with explicit scope. Under the equality reading of its definition,
Lemma 4 of Nagao (ePrint 2015/984) fails: two systems over F_2 have first fall
degree 3 and fake first fall degree 2; a short argument recovers the inference
per instance, and the paper's own uses satisfy it. We formalize Caminata-Gorla
Theorem 2.8 (the last fall degree is the largest fall), so one observed fall
gives only a lower bound. On a Boolean Semaev S3 system, the direct-row and
closure first-fall statistics agree on grade but differ in multiplicity (1
versus 12 at degree three), and an earlier uniform degree-two inference is
withdrawn. No ECDLP algorithm or cost estimate is claimed.

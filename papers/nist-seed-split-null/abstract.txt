Title: Small-prime splitting of the NIST/ANSI prime-field curves: a prime-order null, a cutoff sweep, and a held-out test
Keywords: elliptic curves, NIST curves, seed provenance, Elkies primes, Chebotarev density, preregistration
Category: Public-key cryptography (applications)

The published 1997-98 NIST/ANSI prime-field curves were reported to split at
fewer odd primes l <= 691 than eleven named prime-order seed candidates that
were passed over. An exact Chebotarev null conditioned on prime order shows
that the prime-order confound is worth only 0.5-1.4 splits. On the named
curves, a perfect 25/25 within-field score appears only on a narrow cutoff
plateau, with p ~ 0.006 after correcting for the cutoff choice. A test fixed
before data on 42 held-out unpublished draft-eligible curves does not
replicate the association (0.574 of 102 pairs, p = 0.29). One low outlier
remains, prime239v1, and no mechanism links split counts to discrete-log
hardness.

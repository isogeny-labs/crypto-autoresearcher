## Full degree-three closure and the counterfamily

Iterating A->U(A) intersect B_(<=2) reaches the least fixed point containing the original rows (or W2). At stability, every eligible source for further degree-three multiplication is already in A, so U(A) is closed. This proves equality with full W3, not merely a one-pass Macaulay approximation.

The cubic coefficient at a distinct triple is the sum of the three indicated quadratic coefficients. The low-part formulas correctly include Boolean contractions from the two repeated-index quadratic terms and the diagonal linear term. The rank-difference formula is exactly the kernel dimension of cubic projection on the row space. Stability plus omission of a known affine vanisher is consequently a valid degree-three nonmembership certificate. Iteration counts follow from the available degree-two dimensions, with the two-point ideal subtracting its codimension two.

The counterfamily f_i=x_i*y+1, h=x_1*x_2+1, p=u+x_1*y+1 has exactly the two claimed points with t free. Its initial span and u-times-affines have dimensions N-1 and N, intersecting in span{u}; all other quadratic supports are distinct. Hence its full W2 has the same stated profile as the Semaev family. At cap three, x_i*f_i+f_i=x_i+1 and y*f_i+f_i=y+1 derive the affine ideal. Therefore its exact LFD is three. The multiplication requires cap three under the strict convention even though the reduced result has degree at most two. This is a valid barrier to deducing larger LFD from W2 dimensions alone. The initial-Macaulay row-count bound likewise cannot replace saturation.

## Trace slice and unique-zero system

On V=F2*1 direct_sum W, with W trace zero, the trace equation parametrizes x=t+u, y=t+1+v. Direct expansion gives F=H(u,v)+t*A(u+v), where H=(uv)²+uv+u+v² and A(w)=w²+w. The two branches are H(u,v) and H(v,u); the original exactly-two-point hypothesis makes each branch uniquely zero. The tensor coefficients and trace-zero output claim are correct.

The unique-zero ideal is generated by all u_i,v_i. At least one quadratic coefficient is nonzero: for nonzero w in W, A(w²) cannot vanish because w is not 1. The span of at most 2r outputs therefore cannot supply all 2r independent linears at degree one. Thus h>=2, as required for the gated lifting threshold.

Restricting derivations from the original system gives h<=d. Conversely, lift each branch derivation with its selector gate, at one higher cap, and add the two gated coordinate consequences. Because h>=2, original quadratic input eligibility is accounted for. The trace relation is already available at cap two, so recovering the original coordinates adds no further cap. Thus h<=d<=h+1 is valid.

For each inconsistent coordinate-one slice, restricting a derivation of that coordinate gives e_j<=h. Lifting a slice refutation through its selector requires cap max(2,e_j)+1; deriving all coordinates gives h<=max(3,e+1). The constant threshold is essential and is correctly retained.

The original-system degree-three dichotomy follows from the affine-ideal characterization and the already established d>=3. The H-system test has the separate one-degree uncertainty stated in the note. Swapping u,v alone changes branches; treating H(v,u) as an unconditional generator of H would be invalid. The report correctly excludes that shortcut.

# Independent review: closure transport and non-amplification

Date: 2026-11-09. Source-only review of `closure-transport-and-amplification.md`. No execution.

**The degree-controlled transport, SAT disjoint-product theorem, and tensor nonrefutation theorem are correct with the stated eligibility and bridge qualifications.**

For a degree-s substitution, the image of a source polynomial of degree at most D-1 has degree at most s(D-1). Expanding the substituted multiplier into degree-at-most-s monomials permits successive strict variable multiplications at cap max(sD,c); the pre-multiplication degree never exceeds that cap minus one. The same argument applies to a certificate kernel, because all intermediate image polynomials remain within its checked low-degree domain. Input images in W_c are annihilated when c is at most the target cap. Pullback can reduce rank, so the report correctly refuses to transport a numerical codimension without inspecting the image.

Field-tower descent retains the same coordinate equation span and hence the same W_D for D>=2. It cannot itself increase Boolean dimension or late LFD. Restricting an enlarged V to the old one transfers nonmembership at the same cap, but does not transfer the required ideal-consequence property automatically: a polynomial that vanished on all old solutions may fail on new ones. The note's independent source-ideal/UNSAT requirement is essential here. The enlargement paragraph would be clearest if it repeated this qualification for missing consequences as explicitly as it does for unsatisfiability.

For two disjoint satisfiable systems, the union of their reduced degree-compatible Gröbner bases is a Gröbner basis because cross leading monomials are relatively prime. Degree-compatible division uses products of total degree at most deg(f). Applying each component's LFD property and multiplying at cap max(d_F,d_G,deg f) proves the upper bound. Strictly, only basis elements actually used in that division need to be available: these have degree at most deg(f). Unused higher-degree basis elements need not lie within that cap. Evaluation at a genuine solution of the other component supplies the reverse bound, preserving original generator eligibility. Thus the maximum-LFD theorem is sound, including the zero convention for field-equation-only factors.

For the truncated tensor certificate, a combined monomial of degree at most D-1 has each factor of degree at most D-1. The factor partial multiplication maps therefore apply wherever needed. Acting by T_i tensor identity or identity tensor T_j transports every kernel relation to the shifted relation, proving full kernel closure. The constant tensor is nonzero and the actual total-degree image may have less than product dimension. This yields the stated equivalence of nonrefutation at cap D and the minimum refutation degree for disjoint UNSAT conjunctions in the stated range. It supplies no degree amplification.

Selector branching gives an upper derivation bound and cannot reverse into a lower bound merely because individual branches are hard. Likewise nonlinear substitutions retain their explicit degree factor. These scope statements correctly preserve the strict multiplication cap and the independent ideal-consequence bridge.

No substantive mathematical correction is required. Two wording refinements are recommended: explicitly condition enlarged-V missing-consequence transfer on that polynomial still being a source ideal consequence, and say 'the Gröbner basis elements used in the division' rather than implying unused higher-degree elements must all fit the current cap.

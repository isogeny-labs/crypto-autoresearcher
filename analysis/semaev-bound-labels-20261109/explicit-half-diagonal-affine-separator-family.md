# An explicit infinite half-diagonal UNSAT family with degree-three certificates

2026-11-09. Source-only construction; no experiment, solver, build, or cloud job. The construction solves the affine-separator equations for an infinite permitted family. It establishes a uniform upper bound on that family, not a general bound on Semaev LFD.

## Theorem

For every j>=0 let n=3*7^j and K=F_(2^n). There is an explicitly specified Frobenius-module construction of a subspace V with

 dim V=(n+1)/2,   Tr|V nonzero,   1 not in V,

such that the m=2 Semaev system with z=a6=1 and x,y in V is UNSAT and has a strict Boolean refutation of degree at most three. Consequently its last fall degree is at most three. Exact degrees are not asserted. A compatible curve is E: y²+xy=x³+1 (a2=0,a6=1), with rational target (1,0), whose x-coordinate is the target parameter z=1. The Semaev equation does not require choosing a point-selector encoding.

The construction uses polynomial factor kernels of Frobenius, so it specifies subspaces deterministically once one reciprocal factor from each pair has been selected (for example by coefficient order). It does not assert that these choices are fast to compute at large n.

## 1. A Frobenius-stable isotropic subspace

Let F denote Frobenius x->x². Its minimal polynomial is X^n-1, by a self-contained argument: a nonzero relation sum_(i=0)^d c_i F^i=0 of degree d<n would give a nonzero linearized polynomial sum c_i Z^(2^i) of degree at most2^d<2^n vanishing on every element of K, impossible by the root bound. Since F^n=I, the minimal polynomial has degree n and equals X^n-1. The characteristic polynomial also has degree n and is divisible by it, so equals it. Thus each irreducible factor occurs exactly once in the Frobenius module. Equivalently K is the regular module F2[X]/(X^n-1). The usual normal-basis formulation is independently documented in Michael H. Mertens, Galois Theory, Theorem5.2.1, printed pp106-107: https://www.math.rwth-aachen.de/~Michael.Mertens/GaloisTheory.pdf ; the present proof does not require importing that theorem.

Since n is odd, X^n-1 is squarefree. Its irreducible factors correspond to Frobenius orbits of roots of unity of orders dividing n. Such a factor is self-reciprocal exactly when -1 belongs to the subgroup generated by2 modulo the root order.

For every h>=1, the order of2 modulo7^h is odd: the binomial induction from 2³=1+7 gives 2^(3*7^(h-1))=1 mod7^h. Thus -1 cannot be a power of2 modulo7^h. It follows that for root orders divisible by7, including3*7^h, no irreducible factor is self-reciprocal. The only self-reciprocal factors of X^n-1 are therefore X+1 and X²+X+1.

The first factor gives F2*1. The second gives

 S={u in F8 : Tr_(F8/F2)(u)=0},

a two-dimensional subspace of K. Since n/3=7^j is odd, its absolute trace agrees with its F8 trace.

Choose one factor from every remaining reciprocal pair, and let L be the direct sum of the corresponding irreducible Frobenius submodules. Then

 dim L=(n-3)/2,   F(L)=L,
 Tr(L)=0,   Tr(L*L)=0,   Tr(L*S)=0.                         (1)

For completeness, the orthogonality assertion follows from Frobenius invariance of the trace pairing. After extending scalars to a splitting field, eigenvectors with eigenvalues alpha,beta pair to zero unless alpha*beta=1. A chosen component pairs only with its reciprocal component, which was omitted. The eigenvalues of S and1 are in different components, so L is orthogonal to both as well. This argument descends to F2.

## 2. The factor base

Choose independent nonzero w,eta in S. Set

 W=F2*w direct_sum L,
 e=1+eta,
 V=W direct_sum F2*e.                                      (2)

Then dim V=(n+1)/2, W is its trace-zero part, and Tr(e)=1. Also1 is not in V: otherwise subtracting e would put eta in W, contradicting independence of w,eta and the direct decomposition S plus L.

Let J invert A=F+I on K0=ker Tr and H=F J=J+I. Since L is F-stable and has no fixed nonzero vector, J and H preserve L. They also preserve S. Therefore

 Tr(l H(l))=0 for l in L,
 Tr(s H(l))=Tr(l H(s))=0 for s in S,l in L.                 (3)

The first assertion uses isotropy in (1), not an unproved multiplicative closure of L.

On S, J(u)=u² and H(u)=u4: the relation u4+u²+u=0 holds for trace-zero F8 elements. For every nonzero u in S,

                         Tr(u H(u))=1.                     (4)

Indeed u³=u+1, so u5=u²+u+1, whose absolute trace is1. The zero element gives0.

## 3. Explicit affine separator

On the trace slice use independent x in V,t in W and the original epsilon-free system

 F_t(x)=pi(x²+(t+e)x)+H(t)+g,
 pi(v)=v+Tr(v),   g=H(e+1)=H(eta).

Take the affine separator

                         lambda(t)=t+eta.                    (5)

It lies in K0. Its annihilator coefficient is

 lambda(t)^(2^(n-1))+(t+e)lambda(t)
       =lambda(t)^(2^(n-1))+lambda(t)²+lambda(t)
       =T(lambda(t)),
 T=F^(-1)+F+I.                                             (6)

T vanishes on S and preserves L. Hence the expression in (6) lies in L. By (1)-(2), L is orthogonal to every element of V, so (6) lies in V_perp. This proves annihilation of the x-dependent part of F_t.

For the constant part, H(t)+g=H(t+eta)=H(lambda(t)). Write t=bw+l with b in F2,l in L. Then lambda=eta+bw+l, and eta+bw is always a nonzero element of S. Equations (3)-(4) give

 Tr(lambda(t)(H(t)+g))=Tr(lambda(t)H(lambda(t)))=1.            (7)

Combining (6)-(7) yields the literal Boolean polynomial identity

                         Tr(lambda(t) F_t(x))=1.            (8)

This proves UNSAT directly; the construction is not based on an assumed absence of solutions.

Since lambda is affine and the F_t coordinates are quadratic, (8) is a strict degree-three refutation. The trace relation was already available at cap two; affine elimination and the field-linear row changes defining F_t cost at most two. Lifting the affine multiplier therefore remains within cap three in the original presentation.

## 4. Why the chosen field degrees matter

For a general trace-active V not containing1, the same natural candidate lambda=t+e+1 can work only if3 divides n. Its annihilator condition forces T(W) into V_perp intersect K0. The latter space has dimension r-1: Tr on V_perp is nonzero because1 is not in V. If3 does not divide n, T is invertible (F²+F+I has no kernel), so dim T(W)=r, a contradiction.

When3 divides n, T has the two-dimensional kernel S. The construction above uses exactly that kernel and supplies a compatible stable isotropic complement. This is the structural reason the natural affine separator succeeds for n=3*7^j.

## Scope

This is an infinite modular, half-diagonal, trace-active UNSAT family with a constructive degree-three upper bound. It is not the earlier subfield factor-base case: V is built from selected Frobenius modules, and the ambient degree grows. Some members or choices may already refute at degree two; no lower bound excluding that is claimed.

The system here is the normalized Boolean Weil descent over the full vector-space domain V×V. It is not a finite rational-point list with additional nonlinear selector equations; no degree-three transport through an arbitrary selector encoding is asserted. Deriving1 at cap three makes the ideal the whole Boolean ring and gives LFD<=3 directly.

The result shows that trace-active UNSAT status and growing half-diagonal dimension alone do not force LFD growth. A growing lower-bound family must avoid this affine-separator mechanism or otherwise establish that no low-degree full-closure refutation exists. The theorem does not rule out such a different family.

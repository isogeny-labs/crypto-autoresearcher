**The rank lower bound for the first-feedback pure-x block and the feedback-cycle identity are correct.**

Projecting Tr(a*xG) to the pure-x quadratic component leaves Tr(a*x³), because all other surviving pure-x terms have Boolean degree at most one. Its polarization is the alternating Gold form C_a. The previously reviewed radical calculation for odd n gives the unique nonzero radical a^(-1/3). If that form vanishes on V, maximal isotropic dimension forces its radical into V. The radical map is a permutation of K*, so the kernel of Phi contains at most |V|-1 nonzero parameters and at most |V| parameters in total. Hence dim ker Phi<=r+1 and rank Phi>=r.

The original coordinate span Q0 has zero pure-x quadratic projection. Therefore the first-feedback quotient Q1/Q0 has dimension at least r. This does not identify those directions with r dimensions beyond full W2 unless the extra no-affine condition is supplied. If span(VW)=K, the quadratic top map on output trace functionals is injective, so Q0 contains no nonzero affine consequence and is itself saturated at cap two. The optional strengthened interpretation is consequently valid.

For G=xt+R with R affine, tG is already an available quadratic consequence. Multiplying it by x is permitted at cap three; multiplying G by R is also a sum of permitted affine multiplications. Their sum is (xt+R)G=G², whose coordinates are an output-Frobenius transformation of the original rows. Thus this particular cancellation adds no new coordinate-span direction. It does not exhaust scalar trace cancellations or prove stability.

No mathematical correction is required. The dimension result is a necessary size condition for a proposed invariant, not a growing degree bound, nonrefutation certificate, or universal closure-collapse theorem.

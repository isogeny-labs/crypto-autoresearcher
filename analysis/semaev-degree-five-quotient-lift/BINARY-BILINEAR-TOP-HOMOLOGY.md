# The graded binary bilinear multiplication complex

This is a structural lemma for the characteristic-two lower-bound problem,
not a last-fall-degree bound. It identifies the exact low-degree kernel for
**one** quadratic coordinate combination. The full Semaev m=2 system has
many coordinate equations and additional cross-equation syzygies; those must
be controlled before this lemma can support a closure separator.

Let `A` be the associated graded algebra of the Boolean ring in `N`
variables over `F2`. Thus

    A = F2[x_1,...,x_N]/(x_1^2,...,x_N^2).

Let `q` be a bilinear form in two disjoint variable blocks, with matrix
rank `r` over `F2`. Multiplication `d(h)=q*h` has degree two and `d^2=0`:
every positive-degree element of `A` has square zero in characteristic two.
The resulting graded complex has

    H^j(A,d) = 0                         for j<r,
    dim H^j(A,d) = 2^r * binom(N-2r,j-r) for every j,

with an out-of-range binomial interpreted as zero. In particular, for
`j<r`, every homogeneous relation `q*h=0` of degree `j` is the obvious
relation `h=q*t` for some `t` of degree `j-2`. The convention is that no
such nonzero `h` exists when `j<2`.

**Proof.** Independent invertible linear changes in the two blocks put
`q` into `u_1*v_1+...+u_r*v_r`. Such changes are valid in `A`: the square
of every linear form is the sum of the squares of its variables and is
zero. Separate the `r` paired two-variable algebras from the `N-2r`
unused variables. In one pair, multiplication by `u*v` sends `1` to `u*v`
and sends `u`, `v`, and `u*v` to zero. Its complex is the direct sum of a
contractible pair `1 -> u*v` and the two degree-one homology classes `u,v`.
The total multiplication by `q` is the tensor-product differential (the
signs disappear over `F2`). Over a field, tensoring the contractible
summands remains contractible, while choosing one of `u_i,v_i` from each
pair yields `2^r` homology classes, all in degree `r`. Every unused
variable has zero differential and contributes the ordinary square-zero
algebra on `N-2r` variables. Taking its degree-`j-r` component gives the
formula. Vanishing of homology below `r` is exactly the stated equality
of kernel and image. QED.

For the Semaev m=2 presentation, Proposition 2.5d of
`../semaev-m2-last-fall-degree/theorem-dossier.md` writes the quadratic
part of the coordinate combination indexed by `mu in K` as the bilinear
form with entries

    M_mu[i,j] = Tr(mu * ( (v_i*v_j)^2 + z*v_i*v_j )).

The lemma applies to each nonzero `M_mu` with its **own** rank. It says the
prime-field proof's raw injectivity of top-form multiplication cannot hold
in binary degree two and higher: `q*q=0`. It also gives a stronger usable
replacement than that obstruction alone: below rank `r`, every single-form
kernel element is generated by this explicit square-zero relation.

The next proof obligation is joint rather than single-form. One must compute
the low-degree syzygies of the entire space `{q_mu : mu in K}` and show that
all resulting lower-degree falls are already represented inside the full
iterated closure. Only then could a dimension gap plus an exact solution
count or UNSAT bridge yield a binary LFD lower bound. No such joint claim is
made here.

For the frozen `fresh-n47-rand-00` plan,
`rank_binary_top_forms.py` evaluated the matrix formula exactly over its
named `GF(2^47)` representation. The 47 basis trace functionals `mu=2^i`
have rank histogram `22:4, 23:21, 24:22`; selected combinations give
rank `24` for `mu=1`, `22` for `mu=2`, `24` for `mu=3`, and `23` for
`mu=z`. The input SHA-256 and machine-readable output are in
`validation/n47-rand00-top-form-ranks-001.json`. Thus the single-form
complex is exact below at least degree 22 for **each of these 47 basis
forms**. This does not bound the ranks of all `2^47-1` combinations or
control relations between different forms; in particular it is not an
n47 D5 closure result.

## Exact joint linear-multiplier syzygies on the named n47 input

`joint_binary_top_syzygies.py` now computes the **whole** degree-one
multiplier map for the same named input. Its domain consists of 47 top
equations times each of 48 Boolean variables, so has dimension 2,256; its
image in squarefree cubics has rank 2,162. The kernel therefore has
dimension 94. The source independently reconstructs all 576 bilinear
coefficients by four-point interpolation of the original S3 expression,
checks the product-span rank 47 and the target's membership in that span,
and verifies the predicted quadratic-equation rank 46.

The kernel is **explicitly exhausted** at this degree. One trace relation
between the 47 top equations gives 48 variable multiples. The annihilator
`V^perp` has dimension 23 and supplies 23 `X1`-weighted and 23
`X2`-weighted syzygies of Lemma 2.3 in the theorem dossier. To construct
them, the script solves the linear map

    T(gamma)=sqrt(gamma*z^2)+gamma*z^2

for each coefficient of `lambda*X1` or `lambda*X2`. In the trace-dual
identity, the top equation indexed by `gamma` is
`Tr(T(gamma)*X1*s/z)`, where `s=X1+X2`. The weighted cubic tops reduce to
`Tr(lambda*X1^2*s/z)` and
`Tr(lambda*(X1^2*s+X1*s^2)/z)`, respectively; both have Boolean degree at
most two, so their cubic parts vanish. The script checks each resulting
vector against the complete 17,296-column cubic map and finds the 94
exhibited vectors independent. Since this equals the computed kernel
dimension, they span it. The exact input hash, ranks and control counts are
in `validation/n47-rand00-joint-top-syzygies-001.json`.

This closes the **linear-multiplier top syzygy classification for this one
input**. It does not classify degree-two or higher multipliers, the iterated
bounded closure, or the ideal's solution count. The next proof calculation
should lift these 94 syzygies through the lower-degree equation terms and
test whether their falls generate all low-degree closure mutations before
attempting a parameter-uniform invariant. A dimension match at degree one
alone is not an LFD lower bound.

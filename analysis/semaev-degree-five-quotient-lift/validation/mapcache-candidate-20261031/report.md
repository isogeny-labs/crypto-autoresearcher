# Provisional-map checkpoint candidate: software and custody controls

The degree-five worker reconstructs the triangular provisional map before loading a relation-matrix checkpoint. On the retained n=47 random02 source, the first relation checkpoint appeared about three hours after `produce5.started`; the later restored job published no new checkpoint before it failed. That sequence motivates a separate map checkpoint candidate, but it does not establish the failed pod's process-level cause or a performance gain.

The candidate saves final suffixes of the provisional map in chunks of at most 65,536 rows. In the triangular recurrence, every target used for row *i* has a greater column index than *i*. Once a descending block is complete, later blocks cannot change it. A chunk binds its interval and dimensions to the seed and binary hashes, carries a SHA-256 footer, and is published by conditional S3 PUT followed by complete GET and hash verification. A restore fetches and verifies each contiguous suffix chunk before the relation-matrix restore; a missing next chunk is recomputed. The hook prunes each local chunk after publication or restore, so the largest n=47 chunk is about 1.43 GiB rather than the roughly 42 GiB complete map.

[Machine-readable validation](summary.json) records two generic degree-five controls under the optimized candidate build: fresh and restored certificates are byte-identical to the frozen checkpoint worker and pass independent small-system conditions. A separate n=6 Semaev worker control under the O2 build completed fresh, relation-restore, and map-only-restore paths. In all three, the original checker accepted the same degree-five certificate SHA-256 `7cf2d46d96fe00dfddd1277935c7f0b6577c7ab9cbdc774151c81f56fee82d73`, with checked codimension 3 and exact solution count 3. A flipped map byte was rejected; an absent later chunk was recomputed. The S3 hook was also exercised with two generic chunks, including publication, full readback, local pruning, fetch, and certificate identity. The [raw control bundle](.artifacts.json) is content-addressed and passed remote hydration.

These are software and small-system custody controls. The optimized build has not yet passed the full worker/pod qualification, and no frontier input has run with the candidate. The old worker's relation checkpoints bind its binary hash, so they cannot be restored by this candidate without a separate, checked conversion. A new frozen request and protocol, capacity/readback qualification at scale, and original-checker acceptance remain necessary before any degree-six lower-bound inference.

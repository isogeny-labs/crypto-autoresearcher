# Public custody of certified D4 panel certificates

The [publication map](publication-coverage.json) links each of the 59 named n=33–45 panel inputs with a recorded LFD ≥ 5 lower bound to its certificate SHA-256, public URL, and verification receipt. The audit matched certificate hashes to 45 separately compressed certificates, 12 evidence archives, and two members of the published panel-worker bundle. For six older evidence archives, it additionally rehashed the local archive and its certificate member; it did the same for both bundled n=42 certificates.

The map reuses earlier anonymous-download receipts for public availability. This audit did not redownload every asset today and does not recheck the mathematical certificates. The [panel coverage audit](../panel-coverage-20261031-001/coverage.json) states the exact population and checks the associated lower-bound inference records.

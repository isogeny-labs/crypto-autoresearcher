# Proof obligation exposed by the D5 quotient runs

Let `B_d` be the degree-at-most-`d` Boolean polynomial space and `W_d(F)` its bounded closure. The independent checker accepts a D4 quotient map `pi_4` with kernel `U_4` and proves `W_4(F) ⊆ U_4`. It does not prove equality. The D5 quotient producer starts from `U_4`; consequently `projection_kills_one` is not by itself a refutation in `W_5(F)`.

There is an exactness bridge worth certifying. **Conditional proposition:** if `U_4 = W_4(F)` and the quotient-lift construction reaches its fixed point, then its final kernel is `W_5(F)`. The construction puts `U_4` and the products `x h` for `h ∈ U_4 ∩ B_3` in its kernel. Starting from equality, each such generator lies in `W_5`. Every subsequent low-coordinate row is a combination of earlier generators; multiplying it by a variable is a legal `W_5` closure step. Thus every generated relation lies in `W_5`, while the existing soundness argument gives the reverse inclusion. The quotient's triangular rewrites also have the form `x h` and lie in `W_5`. This proves equality of the final kernel. The proposition assumes that the actual producer executed the declared construction correctly; a result claim additionally needs a separately checked transcript or witness.

A checkable `U_4 = W_4` certificate needs the missing inclusion `U_4 ⊆ W_4`. It suffices to exhibit `dim(U_4)` independent D4 rows with valid provenance from the defining equations and legal bounded multiplication. The existing checker already establishes `W_4 ⊆ U_4` and the quotient dimension, so matching the rank completes equality. The dossier's independent implementation B, `verify/indep_verify.py`, replays the D4 Macaulay plus mutant closure with Python integers and a different monomial order; it previously matched the R35a rank. A separately frozen n47 execution could compare its exact rank with `dim(B_4) - codim(U_4)`. Rank alone from the original producer log is insufficient.

This bridge is immediately relevant to the completed zero-solution n47 polynomial-00 and polynomial-03 runs, and the n48 polynomial-04 run: each returned `projection_kills_one`, and each has an accepted D4 seed. If D4 equality and D5 transcript correctness are independently established, those runs could give exact degree-five refutation bounds. They currently give neither a D5 lower nor an upper bound. For a degree-six lower bound, the direct route remains a checked D5 quotient with positive codimension exceeding the exact solution count, or an independently checked dual functional nonzero on 1 in the zero-solution case. That is the priority for a new n51 computation.

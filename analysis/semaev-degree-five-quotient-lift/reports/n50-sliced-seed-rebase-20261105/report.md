The affine-sliced n50 polynomial03 quotient map has been rebased into the unit-triangular format required by the degree-five worker. The new 1,120,585,872-byte certificate has SHA-256 `2f0facb6b047c857908e7f44e8558248af426dae10454263294df1a93d39cce8`. An independent checker accepted it at degree 4 with codimension at least 38,629, including a nonzero image of 1. This is a checked seed for the next lift, not a degree-five certificate.

The starting [published original n50 certificate](https://github.com/isogeny-labs/crypto-autoresearcher/releases/download/semaev-closure-certificates-20261029/fresh-n50-poly-03-D5-cascade01-bounded-D4-001.D4.cert.gz) has raw SHA-256 `6a25ce310435e564b5487975c17de90f2903a401e8721d95781cf7049f688719`. The relevant transfer statement is Proposition 2.5b of the [theorem dossier](../../../semaev-m2-last-fall-degree/theorem-dossier.md).

# Retrofit claims, 2026-10-19

These are algebraic claims for the Lean retrofit. The word-action and kernel
claims refine `research/THM_COMMUTATOR_KERNEL1.md`, Lemmas 1 and 2(b), and
Proposition 1. Source provenance: internal, read by the authoring session.
The cyclic and transport claims make explicit the mathematical obligations
identified by the 2026-10-18 independent review. Their proofs are derived;
independent correspondence review is pending until its separate report exists.

1. For every natural B, every integer tuple a, either sign e in {1,-1}, and
   every natural d, there is a chronological word in the B translation letters
   and negation, length at most d and shadow (e,a), exactly when
   sum_i |a_i| + m(e,a) <= d. Here m is zero for positive sign and nonnegative
   tuple, one for negative sign, and two otherwise. The construction attains
   this minimum, including B=0 and d=0. `shadow_reachable_iff` is both directions
   of the frozen target, not only a lower bound.
2. In any additive commutative group, the same word sends X to
   wordSign(w) • X + sum_i signedCounts(w,i) • P_i. Concatenating u then v
   executes v after u. Any additive encoding commutes with this action when
   translations are encoded. These assertions refer to actual group laws.
3. In the free associative integer algebra, (v*t_i)^2-1 belongs to the actual
   affine-action evaluation kernel and lies outside the two-sided ideal of
   all commutators. This supplies the companion witness, without proving the
   full group/path-algebra presentation or general surjectivity claim.
4. For a point P killed by n in an additive commutative group, there is a
   canonical additive homomorphism from ZMod n with integer residue z mapping
   to z • P. Its image is the subgroup generated by P. If P has exact order n,
   this encoding is injective. For n nonzero, its value at a is a.val • P.
5. With P of exact prime order p and Q the canonical encoding of x, the
   actual walk point a.val • P + b.val • Q equals the encoding of a+b*x.
   Ordinary and negated collisions yield their respective difference/sum
   recovery formulas when the corresponding denominator is nonzero.
6. For finite subgroup S, maps f and back, and a reverse-composition law
   back(f(P))=d • P on S, coprimality of d and |S| supplies an explicit inverse:
   Nat.gcdB |S| d • back(f(P)) = P. It implies injectivity of f on S and both
   directions of the integer-scalar DLP equivalence.

The statements impose no field-size performance claim. Exact point orders,
particular geometric maps/duality, untrusted-coordinate parsing, full kernel
presentations, exhaustive historical claim coverage and compiled-program
refinement remain distinct obligations.

# Statement-only mathematical review

You may read this file, your handoff, and third-party Lean/Mathlib sources and
your own work. Do not read producer code, reports, receipts, Git history,
review plans, sibling reports or other workspaces.

1. A word over translations t_i and negation v executes left to right. Its
sign is (-1) to the count of v. Its ith signed count is the sum over each
t_i occurrence of (-1) to the number of v strictly to its right. Given any
a in Z^B and e in {1,-1}, derive the exact shortest length of a word with
that sign/count tuple, and both directions of the bounded-length statement.
Include B=0, d=0 and a=(-1,2) with both signs. Derive the point-action formula
in any additive commutative group and the order of concatenation.

2. Let P have exact order n in an additive commutative group. Construct a
well-defined additive map ZMod n to that group by integer multiples of P;
derive injectivity and image. Determine precisely what the weaker assumption
n*P=0 permits. For n prime, Q=x*P and walk point a*P+b*Q, derive ordinary
and negated collision recovery and their exceptional denominators. Give
nonzero-point and composite/zero-denominator controls.

3. S is a finite subgroup, f and back are additive maps, and back(f(P))=d*P
on S, with gcd(|S|,d)=1. Derive an explicit integer recovery coefficient from
a Bezout identity and both directions of the scalar DLP equivalence. Give a
noncoprime counterexample. Do not assume a particular curve map exists.

4. In the free associative algebra over Z on v and t_i, an evaluation sends
v to negation and t_i to translation in the group ring of actual affine
permutations of ZMod n. Determine whether (v*t_i)^2-1 lies in its kernel and
whether it belongs to the two-sided ideal generated by all commutators.

5. A point/exponent state (Y,a,b) obeys Y=a*P+b*Q. A multiplier obeys
M=alpha*P+beta*Q. A step adds M and updates (a,b) to (a+alpha,b+beta),
optionally negating all three. Cycle escape doubles all three. Derive
preservation under arbitrary finite schedules. This models group algebra,
not integer arithmetic or concurrent code.

Supply your own derivations, at least one kernel-checked control per numbered
mathematical boundary where feasible, exact assumptions, and an honest read
attestation. Separate a proof from expected failure to apply a theorem.

# Independent mathematical refinement: four designated points

Let R be a commutative ring with identity, and define E(a,b,c,x,y)=a*x+b*y+c.
For every a,b,c in a nontrivial R, assess the assertion that the conjunction
E(a,b,c,0,0)=0*0, E(a,b,c,1,0)=1*0, E(a,b,c,0,1)=0*1,
and E(a,b,c,1,1)=1*1 is false.
Independently derive or refute it, identify necessary assumptions, and determine
whether it strengthens the assertion that no E equals multiplication everywhere.
Consider the zero ring as an exceptional case. Do not consult implementation,
producer proofs, native outputs, source claims, sibling reports or histories.

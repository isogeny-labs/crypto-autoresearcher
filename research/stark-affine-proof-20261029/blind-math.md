# Mathematical review input

Let R be an arbitrary commutative ring. Define E(a,b,c,x,y)=a*x+b*y+c.

Independently establish or refute these statements. Supply derivations and
explicit exceptional cases, without consulting any producer proof or report.

1. The tuple `(E(a,b,c,1,0)-E(a,b,c,0,0),
   E(a,b,c,0,1)-E(a,b,c,0,0), E(a,b,c,0,0))` equals `(a,b,c)`.
2. Equal values at `(0,0)`, `(1,0)`, `(0,1)` for two coefficient triples
   force those triples to be equal and their values to agree everywhere.
3. If R is nontrivial, no coefficient triple represents x*y for all x,y.
4. Removing the third designated probe can permit distinct affine functions.
5. Determine whether nontriviality is necessary in statement 3.

Include characteristic two, composite-modulus rings with zero divisors and
the zero ring. Distinguish expressions, polynomial coefficients and functions
on a finite ring. No serialization, hash, security, execution or cost claim is
part of these statements.

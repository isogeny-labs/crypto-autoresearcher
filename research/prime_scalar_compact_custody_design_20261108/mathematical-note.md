# Independent mathematical continuation: arbitrary-subcube certificates

Draft derivation for independent critique; no software execution or scientific promotion.

Prior scalar-prefix work established that a nonzero constant restricted generator gives a scalar-inverse certificate and that complete projection semantics require keeping auxiliary assignments. The next useful generalization separates the restriction set from the fixed variable order.

Let K be a field, f_j a multilinear polynomial, a a Boolean assignment, and S a subset of variables. Suppose f_j restricted by x_i=a_i for i in S is c≠0. Define the Boolean indicator chi_(S,a)=product over i in S of x_i if a_i=1, and (1-x_i) if a_i=0. In the quotient by x_i²-x_i, chi*f_j=c*chi: on every Boolean point the identity holds, since outside the subcube chi=0 and inside f_j=c. Boolean multilinear evaluation is injective over every field (prove by induction on variables), so equality of evaluations proves equality in that quotient. Hence chi belongs to the ideal generated by f_j in the Boolean quotient, via c^-1*chi*f_j.

The corresponding SAT clause excludes exactly this subcube and has |S| literals. It excludes2^(n-|S|) full assignments; this is set cardinality, not measured saved work. No true full root is removed, so existential projection remains sound. If S includes auxiliary variables, the learned clause must retain them; it is not automatically a projected-only exclusion. The quotient identity is a local conditional certificate, not a bound on global degree of regularity. Expanded identity degree and Boolean-reduction work must be accounted separately.

A precise structural limit: even within scalar-constant witnesses, optimizing S need not be easy. Given a family of distinct nonempty subsets E of variables, set f=1+sum_(e in E) product_(i in e) x_i and a=0 over any prime field. Restriction to a subset S of zeros deletes exactly monomials whose supports intersect S; surviving distinct monomials never merge. Thus restricted f is the nonzero constant1 iff S hits every member of E. Minimum-cardinality scalar-constant S for this family is exactly minimum hitting-set cardinality. This is an explicit reduction, not a claim that the current fixed-prefix sweep is hard; the sweep searches one chain only.

Next proof obligation: identify structural classes of elliptic-curve encoding supports/cancellations for which a small projected-variable-only S can be certified cheaply, or derive a useful bound on the cost of a restricted search. A linear or sparse encoding alone does not establish that property. The quartic, membership-augmented and quadratic-lift arms may differ in auxiliaries and supports; any later comparison must retain all projected models, complete charts, witness reconstruction and certificate checking costs. Do not infer exponent or regularity improvement from shorter local clauses alone.

## Explicit telescoping multipliers for arbitrary S

Choose any ordering i_1,...,i_k of S. Write f^(0)=f and f^(r)=f with x_(i_1),...,x_(i_r) substituted by their assigned values. Because f is multilinear, f^(r-1)=u_r+x_(i_r)v_r with u_r,v_r independent of x_(i_r). Define h_r=v_r, computed from f^(r-1)'s terms containing x_(i_r) by removing that variable. Then f^(r-1)-f^(r)=(x_(i_r)-a_(i_r))h_r. Summing gives the ordinary polynomial-ring identity

    f-c = sum_(r=1)^k (x_(i_r)-a_(i_r)) h_r.

If D=deg(f), every nonzero h_r has degree<=D-1 and every displayed product has degree<=D: substitution and removing one variable cannot increase degree. Cancellation can only lower it. For D=0, the relevant h_r are zero and the constant identity is immediate. Dividing by c gives

    1 = c^-1 f - sum_(r=1)^k c^-1 h_r (x_(i_r)-a_(i_r)).

This is a degree-at-most-D refutation of f=0 together with the fixed-value equations. It is not a refutation of the unfixed system. For multiple generators, use f_j in its coordinate and zero multipliers elsewhere. The full vector and every fixed-value equation remain explicit; omitted auxiliary equations cannot be silently assumed.

The indicator identity is different. Its displayed unreduced product degree is at most D+k, with indicator multiplier degree k. More explicitly, chi*(f-c) has zero multilinear reduction, so ordinary monomial reduction expresses it as sum_i q_i(x_i²-x_i), with each nonzero product q_i(x_i²-x_i) of degree<=D+k. Such a bound follows by replacing an x_i^e monomial by x_i one step at a time via x_i^e-x_i=(x_i²-x_i)(1+x_i+...+x_i^(e-2)); multiplying by the other variables never exceeds the starting degree. For multilinear chi and f, individual exponents are at most2, although the general reduction argument suffices. In this description degree(q_i)<=D+k-2 whenever a nonzero q_i occurs. Expanded term counts, field operations and storage remain separate costs. Neither local bound is a global degree-of-regularity bound.

## One deletion pass gives an inclusion-minimal restriction

Start from any S_0 with f|S_0=c≠0, fixing values from the same assignment a. Consider each variable of S_0 once in a fixed order. For the current S, recompute f restricted by S minus that variable directly from the original f. Delete the variable iff the exact canonical residual is a nonzero constant. Every accepted residual equals c: further restricting it to S_0 gives the original c. Keep a variable after a failed test.

After later deletions, a previously failed removal cannot become successful. If the final coarser S_final minus i produced constant c', restricting further to the earlier tested S_earlier minus i would also give c'; further restriction to S_0 forces c'=c, contradicting the failed test. Thus at most|S_0| exact restriction tests produce an inclusion-minimal S. It need not have minimum cardinality; deletion order can change the result. Starting with the entire assignment for any violated f supplies an initial scalar witness. Restricting deletions to a permitted projected-only starting set is sound only if that starting set already qualifies.

For T original terms and I total term-variable incidences, a straightforward sparse-term scan with an assignment membership array uses O(T+I) term/incidence visits per restriction, plus field accumulation and residual-key costs. Thus at most k tests use O(k(T+I)) such visits, with initialization and representation construction charged separately. This is not a bit-time bound: mask operations, hashing/equality, coefficient arithmetic and canonicalization must be counted. No incremental cancellation state is reused. Certificate reconstruction and full projection verification remain required.

Prior-work boundary: Deharbe, Fontaine, Le Berre and Mazure, Computing prime implicants (FMCAD2013), https://www.verit-solver.org/papers/fmcad2013.pdf, distinguishes prime from minimum implicants and studies shrinking sufficient assignments. This deletion principle is not claimed as new SAT methodology. The assigned contribution here is the explicit finite-field scalar-residual predicate, arbitrary-set certificate transport, degree accounting and auxiliary-projection boundary; no novelty or measured speedup is asserted.

## Evaluation lemma and reduction domain made explicit

For injectivity, write a multilinear g in n variables as u+x_n v. If g vanishes on the Boolean cube, evaluations at x_n=0 show u vanishes on the (n-1)-cube, hence u=0 by induction. Evaluations at x_n=1 then show v=0 by induction. The n=0 case is a constant. Thus the quotient's unique multilinear representative is determined by Boolean evaluations over any field.

## Safety boundary and independent evidence

After known-base substitution, one decomposition at alphaP+betaQ_t has a target
row supported on Q_t alone. Once Q_t has an original-row-certified value, a
later valid row of that form adds no information about another Q coordinate.
All cross-target construction equalities must already be present for this
reasoning to apply. This is a scoped standard linear-algebra observation to
check in the implementation, not a trusted signed proof or new symmetry.
[pcc:unverified]

Do not transfer that argument to an unknown norm base: a row involving a known
Q_t may still constrain unknown base logs and thereby help other targets.
Skipping those queries could lose useful information. This first test is
all-known48 only. It must preserve exact final named-target masks of its own
full-query reference, including unresolved targets on adverse fixtures, rather
than assume all fresh targets recover as the A16 panel did.

Charge the candidate's online 8-column Fr row insertions, repeated membership,
substitution, certificate construction/lifting/composition and public group
checks. A query count decrease may trade against more algebra or certification.
Run a fully charged native joint control on the exact candidate pool, checking
positive native48-unit/raw-row certificates and negative residuals. Separately
check the full-query reference and complete pair/triple/query-miss truth for
all queries that actually execute. Full-reference rows are validation data only;
they must not guide candidate scheduling or provide uncharged candidate work.

Tiny independent controls must cover construction-only recovery, recovery from
natural queries, cross-target cascades, no-recovery targets, coordinate aliases,
zero rows, corrupt RHS/beta/native weights/public points, wrong skip decisions,
future/reference leakage and failed-prefix retention. Require producer outputs
and decision certificates before checker invocation, full cost-payload hashes,
independent field/array schedules and exact checkpoint identity/count receipts.
A lost target relative to full reference, false skip, missing cross-target row,
certificate or cost-binding failure stops the prospective attempt with its
observed prefixes and unknown remaining work. No repeated favorable trial is
implied.

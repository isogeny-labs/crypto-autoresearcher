# Source and mechanism qualifications

The ell199 known-degree candidate-count/minimum-factor-degree separator is an exact retrospective observation. There is no recovered source stating caps32 and2048 or requiring a test at199. Any candidate cap20 through59 and degree cap1980 through2199 gives the same classification on these21 observations;32/2048 are not uniquely inferred constants.

The first cap is candidate-list length, not the number of comparisons before the correct answer. At199, omitted239/2863 and omitted256/876 have60 candidates but correct-candidate ranks3 or8. A32-comparison timeout therefore does not reject either under these source-known-degree lists. The current source allocates a list of size(ell-1)/2+1, which is100 at199, not32; no original1997 fixed-array implementation has been recovered.

The second cap is the degree of a smallest irreducible factor predicted from the full signed torsion action. It is not the degree of every polynomial constructed by a period algorithm. Morain1995 section3.5 constructs a conjugate factor of degree r*(ell-1)/2, which can be reducible. Published prime239v1,prime239v3 andP521 have r199=40, so that construction has degree3960, above2048, although their smallest irreducible factors have degrees440,440 and120. Thus a literal2048 cap on all intermediate polynomials is a different policy and fails those controls. The original caller's representation and factoring path remain unknown.

The general cap and degree-mode checks are frozen separately in policy-check-01; all counterexamples are retained. The maximum auxiliary prime observations refer to the already measured later LiDIA reconstruction, not original1997 execution. All11 omission replays stopped before199. Only the P384 andP521 publication replays reached199 or later. This rules out an at199 callback as the cause of omissions in that particular replay, not in every hypothetical older program.

Morain's1995 bibliography cites Mueller October1994 and Dewaghe December1994 works in preparation. This establishes contemporaneous work; it does not prove that those manuscripts contained the exact refinements in the later LiDIA source or that the original NIST/Reiter caller used them. No1998 full-paper algorithm is inferred from its inaccessible text. Earlier pre-Dewaghe profile names describe explicit reconstruction switches and retain their original provenance limits.

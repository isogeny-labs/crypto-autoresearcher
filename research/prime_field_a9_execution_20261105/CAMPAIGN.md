# Standing research objective

Find a reproducible improvement in prime-field factor-base index calculus over
our strongest implemented baseline. Pursue base designs, symmetries, relation
collection, cancellation and target recovery. Require fresh curve/target holdouts
and complete construction, collection, elimination, extraction, lifting and
verification accounting before claiming an improvement. Independently check
claimed outcomes; do not equate more code, administrative completion or a local
component benchmark with goal completion.

After each bounded experiment publish all outcomes, update the database,
commit/push code and raw records before reports, checkpoint to authorized S3,
and select the next discriminating experiment within existing resource limits.
Routine work is authorized; no new spending or approval is inferred. An
obstruction on one lead is documented and a justified successor is pursued.

This A9 batch strengthens A8's baseline by controlling sparse functional
application in BOTH representations. It reuses already observed systems and
therefore cannot establish fresh-holdout transfer or a complete attack-cost
improvement. Those remain explicit campaign requirements.

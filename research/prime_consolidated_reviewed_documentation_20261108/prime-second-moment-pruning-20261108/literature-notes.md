# Primary-source context and limits

Petit, Kosters and Messeng, Algebraic approaches for the Elliptic Curve Discrete Logarithm Problem over prime fields: https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/petit/files/16PKC_primeECDLP.pdf . Primary PDF retrieved; introduction discusses interval factor bases and expressing large-degree rational-map membership through small-degree equations, including subgroup-coset and isogeny constructions. This is prior-art context for representations, not evidence that low-degree rows imply favorable Groebner complexity. No novelty claim for symmetric coordinates or quadratic representations is made here.

Wagner, A Generalized Birthday Problem, section2: https://www.enseignement.polytechnique.fr/informatique/profs/Francois.Morain/Master1/Crypto/projects/Wagner02.pdf . Primary PDF retrieved; ordinary sorted and hashed list joins are established techniques. Our complete deterministic pair/triple baselines retain all matching tuples and outputs. No filtered XOR/k-tree speedup is transplanted to elliptic-curve groups; its structural assumptions and goal of finding some solutions differ from complete projected enumeration.

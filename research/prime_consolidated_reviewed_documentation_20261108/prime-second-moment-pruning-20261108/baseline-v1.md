# Complete deterministic baselines, including output-sensitive meet-in-the-middle

Let F be the canonical set of all signed affine curve points with x in the B-element interval;|F|=n<=2B, zero ordinate included once. Charge factor-base construction: B RHS evaluations, complete finite-field square-root extraction, canonical coordinates and storage. Repeated choices from F are allowed.

h3: enumerate ordered pairs(P1,P2), compute P3=R-P1-P2, reject only if final P3 is identity, and otherwise test its x interval. Since both signs are in F, membership follows from the valid group point's x interval. Intermediate identity sums remain allowed. This enumerates all ordered affine triples. Cost O(n²+N) group operations/tests and output operations for N emitted ordered relations, plus preparation and witness/reconstruction/dedup costs. Coordinate access and group-operation bit costs are charged.

h4: construct all ordered pair records(sum(P_i,P_j),i,j), including identity sums and every colliding tuple. Sort by a deterministic canonical group-point key, with identity its own key. For each ordered first pair, binary-search the complete range of records whose sum is R minus its sum and emit EVERY matched second pair. Every ordered quadruple is emitted exactly once for its ordered split. Cost O(n² log(n+1)+N) group/key-comparison/output operations; O(n²) stored records. Do not discard collisions, compress them to one witness, or treat distinct tuple orders as duplicates before the task's output convention is applied.

h5: build the same complete sorted ordered-pair table. Stream all ordered triples and look up all pair records summing to R minus that triple sum. Every ordered quintuple has one ordered3+2 split. Cost O(n³ log(n+1)+N) operations, O(n²) table records and streaming outputs; the pair-table setup is included for n>=1, with n=0 handled directly. Preserve identity intermediate sums and repeated points. No final affine point is manufactured in these two algorithms: emitted factors are already entries of F.

These are deterministic lookup/sorting baselines, not hash-table expected-time assumptions or new cryptanalytic attacks. Group-key serialization/comparison bit complexity, group arithmetic, point validation, factor-base preparation, tuple-index storage, all outputsN, conversion to requested projected models, duplicate handling, chart obligations and witness checking remain charged. There is no universal claim that field and group operations have equal cost.

Comparison to the new moment design is about pre-output enumeration pressure. In p>>B², its necessary moment-pair envelope isTheta(B³): h3 attempts that many quartic-root problems; h4 usesTheta(pB³) nonzero-q candidates plus its retained zero fiber; h5 usesTheta(p²B³) unstratified candidates. The complete baseline envelopes are O(B²+N), O(B² log(B+1)+N), and O(B³ log(B+1)+N), respectively, plus preparation and the stated costs. OutputN may dominate and is never omitted in a total-cost claim. Actual moment roots/support constraints can reduce emitted candidates, but do not erase the cost of traversing every proposed moment grid. Hence no full-grid scaling is justified by the moment interval bounds alone. An online pruning implementation or different algebraic mechanism needs its own matched evidence and finite admission, neither of which is supplied here.

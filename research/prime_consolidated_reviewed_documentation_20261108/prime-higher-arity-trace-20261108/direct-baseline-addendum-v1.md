# Complete direct affine-point baseline and ranking consequence

Let F contain both signs of every curve point whose x coordinate lies in the interval t+[0,B-1] modulo p. Each x has at most two ordinates, so |F|<=2B; zero ordinates are included once. Preparing F costs B curve-RHS tests and the necessary charged finite-field square-root extraction, canonicalization and storage. It is not free precomputation.

For each ordered(h-1)-tuple from F, compute P_h=R-sum_{i=1}^{h-1}P_i. If P_h is the identity reject it for this fixed affine arity. Otherwise the group operation already yields a curve point; its membership in F is equivalent to ((x(P_h)-t) modp)<B, since both signs are included. No hash table is necessary for that last test. Accepting tuples give the target relation. Conversely every ordered h-tuple of affine factor-base points summing to R is reached by enumerating its first h-1 entries. Repeated points are allowed and therefore are not missed. Output duplicates/permutation conventions and all requested signed/chart witnesses must still be handled and charged. Lower affine arities are separate chart cases, not silently included by accepting identity.

For fixed h, this complete direct search takes O(|F|^(h-1)) group additions and coordinate-membership tests with a straightforward bounded-h loop, plus factor-base preparation and output/witness costs. This is an operation-count bound, not measured runtime or a comparison between group and field operations.

The full unstratified bounded-trace coefficient grid has p^(h-2)R_h entries. In the nonsaturated regime and for fixed h, R_h=h(B-1)+1=Theta(B), so its size scales as Theta(p^(h-2)B). The direct ordered-point candidate bound is O(B^(h-1)). Their count-scale ratio is Theta((p/B)^(h-2)), comparing the coefficient grid to the worst-case B-based point-tuple bound, not claiming an equality for actual |F| or actual wall time. As B/p tends to zero, naive exhaustive traversal of that full coefficient grid is a poor next scaling strategy relative to this complete direct baseline. Individual degenerate strata may have smaller grids; this does not turn the full-grid count into a speedup.

The next useful research action must exploit additional constraints to prune the remaining full-field coefficients, or show a separately quantified algebraic advantage. Merely exposing the trace factor R_h/p does not justify a larger exhaustive coefficient campaign. All curve/interval preprocessing, candidate checking, certificate construction, witness reconstruction and output costs remain part of any eventual matched comparison. No experiment or runtime claim is authorized by this addendum.

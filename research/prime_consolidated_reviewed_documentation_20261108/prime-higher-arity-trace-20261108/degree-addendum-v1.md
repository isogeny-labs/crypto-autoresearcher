# Exact invariance boundary for the affine change alone

Let F be the FULL original field-polynomial generator list, before target-pivot elimination, row removal or bounded-domain augmentation. Apply the invertible affine change of(v,u) to(z0,w), leaving other variables unchanged. Its linear part L is invertible because2!=0 in F_p. For every nonzero generator f of degree d, the highest homogeneous component of f composed with the inverse affine map is f_d composed with L_inverse: translation contributes only lower-degree terms, and invertibility prevents the degree-d component from vanishing.

Thus the homogeneous ideal generated by all highest parts is carried to the new highest-part ideal by a graded polynomial-ring automorphism. Their graded quotient vector-space dimensions, hence Hilbert functions, agree degree by degree. This establishes that the affine change ALONE is not a reduction of that highest-part invariant. It does not assert equality of algorithms' operational costs or arbitrary definitions of degree of regularity.

The statement is deliberately not extended to eliminating A0, dropping/replacing rows, adding a bounded-z polynomial or CNF, or changing Boolean bit encoding: those are different operations and require separate arguments. The current design's benefit is exposing a constrained search parameter, not proving a regularity decrease.

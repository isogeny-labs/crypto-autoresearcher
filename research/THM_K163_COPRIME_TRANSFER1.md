## Exact statement

Let `E` and `E'` be additive commutative groups, let

```text
phi      : E  ->+ E'
recovery : E' ->+ E
```

be additive homomorphisms, and let `d,r` be natural numbers. Assume

```text
recovery (phi X) = [d]X  for every X in E,
gcd(d,r) = 1.
```

Then `phi` is injective on the `r`-torsion of `E`. More explicitly, if
`[r]P=[r]Q=0` and `phi(P)=phi(Q)`, then `P=Q`. Consequently, for every integer
`k`,

```text
phi(Q) = [k]phi(P)  <->  Q = [k]P.
```

For the standard K-163 subgroup constant already defined by
`CryptoResearch.Endosweep.Koblitz.K163.n`, set

```text
r = 5846006549323611672814741753598448348329118574063,
d = 45641.
```

Lean checks `gcd(d,r)=1`. The named K-163 theorem additionally assumes that
`P` has exact additive order `r`, that `Q` is killed by `r`, and that the two
supplied homomorphisms satisfy the recovery composition. Under precisely those
hypotheses it proves the displayed scalar-relation equivalence. If `Q` is
separately known to lie in the cyclic subgroup generated by `P`, this is the
corresponding discrete-log preservation statement. Without that membership
bridge both sides of the equivalence may simply be false.

The companion theorem `k163_map_preserves_generator_order` proves directly
that the image of an exact order-`r` generator still has exact order `r`.
Neither result needs Lean to prove that `r` is prime; Lean checks the published
numeric constant and its coprimality with 45,641.

## Stronger same-curve horizontal control

The retained K-163 horizontal control uses the CM element

```text
alpha     =  51 - 160 tau,
alpha_bar = -109 + 160 tau,
```

in the order `Z[tau]` with `tau^2 = tau - 2`. Lean checks the closed
coefficient factorization

```text
conj(alpha) = alpha_bar,
alpha * alpha_bar = 45641.
```

It also proves a point-action version. For any additive endomorphism `tau`
that satisfies `tau(tau(X)) = tau(X) - [2]X` for every `X`, the explicit
homomorphisms

```text
horizontalAlpha(X)    = [51]X - [160]tau(X),
horizontalRecovery(X) = [160]tau(X) - [109]X
```

satisfy both compositions

```text
horizontalRecovery(horizontalAlpha(X)) = [45641]X,
horizontalAlpha(horizontalRecovery(X)) = [45641]X.
```

Thus the recovery premise of the generic transfer theorem is derived rather
than assumed for this abstract horizontal model. Lean then proves exact image
order and scalar-relation preservation for the K-163 constant.

Using the published K-163 eigenvalue constant already present in Lean, closed
arithmetic also matches the retained control receipt:

```text
alpha(lambda)     = 4305110247233098359902032639579138367335695064214,
alpha_bar(lambda) = 1540896302090513312912709114019309980993423509791,
alpha(lambda) * alpha_bar(lambda) mod r = 45641.
```

Both residues are coprime to `r`. These are integer-consistency certificates,
not a proof inside Lean that actual Frobenius acts as `[lambda]` on the
sect163k1 generator.

This is stronger than the bare coprimality gate but it is a **same-curve
horizontal control**, not the sought vertical conductor descent. Lean does
not identify the abstract `tau` with sect163k1 Frobenius, model the concrete
curve, or show a speedup. The horizontal action is already part of the native
Koblitz arithmetic and therefore is not evidence that a conductor-floor
representative is weak.

## Proof

Suppose `phi(P)=phi(Q)` and put `Z=P-Q`. Additivity gives `phi(Z)=0`, so the
recovery identity gives `[d]Z=0`. The `r`-torsion hypotheses give `[r]Z=0`.
Since `gcd(d,r)=1`, Bezout supplies integers `a,b` with `ad+br=1`. Therefore

```text
Z = [ad+br]Z = [a]([d]Z) + [b]([r]Z) = 0.
```

Thus `P=Q`. Additivity of `phi` proves the forward scalar relation; applying
the injectivity result to `Q` and `[k]P` proves the reverse relation.

For the concrete arithmetic, one independently checkable Bezout certificate is

```text
45641 * 4197402217772063594534280302040296057815236644071
- 32770 * 5846006549323611672814741753598448348329118574063
= 1.
```

The Lean proof uses Mathlib's extended Euclidean algorithm rather than trusting
this transcribed certificate.

## Proves-too-much control

Coprimality is load-bearing. On the additive group `ZMod 6`, multiplication by
2 followed by the identity recovery map satisfies the composition `[2]`, but
it maps both `0` and `3` to `0`. The Lean module checks this noninjective
control. Therefore the theorem cannot silently drop `gcd(d,r)=1`.

| `gcd(45641,r)=1` | degree does not kill that subgroup | Lean-checked closed arithmetic |

The abstract scalar `d` is not connected to geometric degree inside Lean.
Calling it the isogeny degree is justified only after the external geometric
bridge supplies an actual degree-`d` isogeny and its algebraic dual.

## What follows, and what does not

The theorem discharges the algebraic implication

```text
certified recovery composition + coprime subgroup order
    => injective subgroup transfer + preserved scalar relation.
```

It does **not** establish a K-163 forward vertical map, its dual, either
point-level composition, field of definition, separability, endpoint identity,
map cost, transported Frobenius action, Pollard-rho improvement, or any
weak-curve label. A reverse endpoint-to-source transfer needs the opposite
composition `phi o recovery = [45641]` on the endpoint subgroup; the source
composition alone proves only the source-to-endpoint direction.

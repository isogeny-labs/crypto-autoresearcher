## Bounded question and controls

Compare three actual programs on matched fresh curves and targets:

- Native norm32, preserving its original public prerequisites and unknown order.
- The same norm32 plus 16 independently paid public-known helper atoms, with
  helper coefficients substituted into RHS and all original native rows retained
  for certificate lifting.
- Source-free paid known32, retaining A13's strongest implemented generic cold
  control; no forced source learning or imported helper/norm cache.

Ask whether the helper program adds certified original-norm information or
named target coverage over norm32, and whether any benefit survives comparison
with source-free known32 at the included cold costs. A gain over norm alone
is component progress, not an improvement over the generic control. The
mechanism is existing block substitution/rowspace recovery, not a new scalar
symmetry, norm map or novelty claim. No favorable outcome is required.

## Native certificate model and reduced unknowns

Keep raw norm32 coefficients in the native baseline and native 48 slots in the
hybrid. Helper labels and original fixed-prerequisite norm labels appear as
explicit native unit equations with signed public RHS. The original norm
projection's fixed-known entries and old unknown order are retained as a
separate reduced-column map. Substitute only the declared known slots before
calling the existing reduced joint/outside-first solver; the hybrid still has
the same original norm unknown coordinates plus eight symbolic target columns.
It does not introduce 16 outside unknown logs or inflate learned rank.

If a paid helper point coincides with an old norm unknown, retain the induced
known-value equation on that original unknown coordinate. Do not quietly drop
or renumber the old coordinate merely because new public knowledge is now
available. Any point/sign alias must be represented consistently in native
unit, source, target and reduced maps; supplied coincidence information is
separate from newly collected information.

Every positive target certificate must lift through both substitution and the
solver's row operations to the original native unit/source/target rows. Preserve
all weights and the original RHS, including contributions of known helper and
prerequisite labels. Check the resulting target functional and scalar against
those original rows, then public[q]P=Q. A certificate against only the reduced
matrix is insufficient to validate the native model or paid knowledge scope.
For absent/unidentified targets retain independently checked negative residuals.

Known32 keeps its own 32 native unit equations and source-free direct target-U
reduction with original-unit-row lifting and a fully charged joint control if
included. All eight symbolic target coordinates remain joint: cross-target
coincidences are explicit equations, not eight disconnected scalar problems.
Constructed betaQ/alpha P+beta Q is symbolic knowledge, not a public-known log.

## Geometry, guards and actual comparison work

Each native base has its own paid pair/index/query geometry and cache. Keep
helper/norm overlaps and public-source/target coincidences; never redraw to
force 48 distinct geometric atoms. Index-distinct atoms with equal or opposite
points cannot create fake geometrically distinct triples. Preserve the
canonical signed witness and retain/deduplicate real affine equations without
silently changing the original distinct-triple semantics.

The pair-entry gate must be 8192 rather than the old 4096: a distinct 48-atom
signed pair table has 4512 entries. Guard native 48 coefficient records,
retained rows/labels and reduced width separately; reduced norm unknowns plus
8 targets must fit max 64 joint columns. Refuse with observed prefixes and
unknown remaining work, never truncate, remove inconvenient atoms or expand
budgets to rescue recovery. Exact final row/cache limits belong in the protocol.

Actually execute each declared collection/algebra program and charge its work.
Physical sharing and conditional cold references must be explicit; do not
invent an executed32-atom baseline from only a48-atom engine's estimated
counts. An independent complete signed-triple/pair/query-miss oracle checks
all actual atom sets, canonical aliases, exceptional denominators and output
rows. Its atlas is a checker cost, not free producer precomputation.

Use the same implemented prepaid-sign, per-endpoint batched inversion and paid
exceptional fallback baseline across programs. Retain scalar controls if
declared, charging them as physical checks rather than extra outcomes. Share
no denominators across future endpoints or other programs for free. Source/
helper/base and target P/Q constructors, scans and paid operational prefixes
remain separately auditable; this boundary is not a secrecy assertion.

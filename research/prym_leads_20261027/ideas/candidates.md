## Concrete construction boundaries

### Same-E cover family

For `f(U)=U^3+A*U+B`, `c=f(t) != 0`, use

    C_t: y^2=x^6+3t*x^4+(3t^2+A)*x^2+c,
    pi_t(x,y)=(x^2+t,y).

The complementary quotient is

    E'_t: V^2=W^3+(3t^2+A)*W^2+3t*c*W+c^2,
    W=c/x^2,  V=c*y/x^3.

These formulas were supplied as a coordinator symbolic derivation and checked
here by direct substitution on paper; no new curve was constructed or counted.
For separable `f` in odd characteristic, a repeated root of `f(x^2+t)` would
force either a repeated root of `f` or `x=0,c=0`. Thus the stated family is
smooth when `c != 0`. The double cover is branched over the points with
`U=t`; different t have different branch supports over the fixed E. Ratios
`(U-t)/(U-s)` have odd valuations at those supports. This does not dismiss
equivalences after allowed automorphisms of E or C.

At `t=0` this is exactly the historical family. Over a common E point,
liftability depends on `U-t`; two covers can shrink the usable intersection
substantially. Select that intersection before inspecting relation labels,
remove public/sign/duplicate columns, and collect the baseline on the SAME
intersection. A rank from an old larger base is not a matched comparison.
Stacking b13 and b16, or two different elliptic targets, would combine different
logarithm variables and cannot be credited as a same-E rank gain. Small-torsion
Frey–Kani/Diem gluing is an alternate construction ingredient, not evidence of
new rank or a route to a second r-torsion direction.

### Pole completion and higher function spaces

For the monic sextic, `H=infinity_++infinity_-` has degree two. The bases

    V3=L(3H)=<1,x,x^2,x^3,y>,
    V4=L(4H)=<1,x,x^2,x^3,x^4,y,xy>

have dimensions five and seven. In the historical normalized `y-v3` slice,
source plus three distinct lifted atom points impose four homogeneous linear
conditions before normalization. The generic V3 witness is unique up to
scalar. The same constraints in V4 leave a three-dimensional vector kernel,
hence approximately `p^2` projective choices, not a cost-free extra freedom.
Dimension jumps and zero y-coefficient charts need explicit treatment.

The current `construct` rejects `len(v*v-f6) != 7` as
`exceptional_pole_order`. Leading cubic coefficient `+1` or `-1` cancels at
one infinity. Exact valuations can give unequal pole orders, further degree
drop or an infinity zero. These are not generally captured by a fixed
degree-six/six-affine-root description. The retained reason counts P=417
(b13) and P=451 (b16) are historical status counts read from the sealed raw
file; no rejected function was reconstructed here.

For `pi2=(B/x^2,B*y/x^3)`, the two infinity images are `(0,+B)` and `(0,-B)`.
They are opposite points, not individually the identity. Equal pole orders
cancel their contribution in E2. Unequal orders require the signed difference
times `(0,B)`, with infinity zeros and multiplicities handled through the exact
divisor. In contrast, both infinities map to E1 identity. A row can satisfy E1
and still fail a naively reused zero-companion equation. This observation
delimits the new completion, not the correctness of old regular witnesses.

For `F=A4+(b0+b1*x)*y`, elimination gives
`A4^2-(b0+b1*x)^2*f6`, degree at most eight. Four distinct forced roots leave
degree at most four, subject to exact pole order, common factors and
multiplicities. Generic lower degree is not automatically a useful row or
better success probability. Compare regular V3 to completed V3 first, then
completed V3 to V4. Keep x=0 excluded in these comparisons so chart completion
does not confound the function-space effect. These bounded-degree constructors
remain inside the P1546/KN-OPEN-020 measurement scope.

## Router candidate retained as uninstantiated / duplicate boundary

`v3(x_R)=y_R` and, on the regular degree-six stratum, define the monic residual

    h5(x) = (v3(x)^2-f6(x))/(leading_coefficient*(x-x_R)).

For an implicit growing-degree base
`A_d(x)=D_(2^d)(x^2+t)-c`, require `h5 | A_d`. Repeated squaring with
`D_(2n)=D_n^2-2` evaluates this remainder modulo h5 with a short circuit.
Squarefreeness, coprimality with the source root and `h5 | x^p-x` restrict the
stated stratum to distinct rational residual roots; recover y from v3 and
retain signs/subgroup checks. Nonliftable sources and excluded strata remain
failures, not missing entries. A valid solution has an explicit native source
coefficient and principal witness, so its verifier is clear.

That construction is only a predicate/encoding. It does not extract v3, a
factor h5 or a successful branch cheaply. The merged P-256 F1 work already
uses source/target-coupled cubic divisibility, short remainder circuits and
distinct/repeated-coordinate strata, with implicit extraction and its cost
still missing. The native degree-five residual wrapper does not supply a named
new extraction operation. `IDEA-20260723-001` likewise already retains the
compact exact-support constructor obligation and warns that represented

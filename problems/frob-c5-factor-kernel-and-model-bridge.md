## Problem

The mathematical C5 factor-kernel statement is proved in Lean for the three
canonical degree-five finite fields. A separate Lean certificate now proves
that q-power on each frozen polynomial quotient basis has the exact 5×5
matrix reconstructed from the archived outputs. The exact recorded matrices
now have Lean-checked C5 factor-kernel sums and trace-kernel dimension four.
The full q-power endomorphism has the certified matrix in each quotient power
basis. Lean now proves the C5 factor-kernel sums, trace-kernel dimension,
fifth-power identity, and fixed-line separation for those actual quotient
endomorphisms. A scoped independent semantic review corroborated the quotient
mathematics and identified a break in original Sage execution-source custody and
the direct-trace control. Those historical links remain open. The frozen C5
control cannot yet be promoted to a proof-backed historical run verdict. C8
and the experiment success gate remain separate.

This cross-check is not an independent Reviewer attestation or a historical Sage execution proof.

- **Independent semantic review, scoped:** The [2026-10-25 review](../docs/reports/2026-10-25-frob-c5-semantic-review/composition.md) bound 45 source files, assigned three independent joints, and passed the repository independence checker. Blind arithmetic agrees with all 75 archived matrix entries and the q=11/13/19 C5 dimensions; a pinned Lean source-body audit found only standard axioms in the reviewed public theorems. The historical custody joint **breaks**: final post-bugfix source hashes do not authenticate the driver and modules loaded by the original Sage subprocess, and `T_equals_hM` is a same-matrix consistency check rather than the specified independent direct repeated-q-power trace comparison. The original independent checker covers trace nullity but not the complete factor-kernel decomposition. This review does not refute the quotient mathematics or establish the historical C5 verdict.
- **Independently reviewed replacement direct trace:** The [successor replay](../docs/reports/2026-10-25-frob-c5-direct-trace/REPORT.md) computes the five-term trace by repeated q-power polynomial arithmetic without using a matrix on that path, then compares all entries with `h(M)` from the earlier source-bound matrix replay. The [2026-10-27 technical review](../research/frob_c5_direct_trace_review_20261027/report.md) passed two independently sealed joints and the stock independence checker: all three fixed cells agree entrywise, have trace rank/nullity 1/4 and base-field image; the q=13 four-term control has rank five. A source reviewer demonstrated that `h(M²)=h(M)`, so trace equality alone does not identify the matrix. Exact source binding and the separate prior matrix replay remain essential. This scoped validation cannot repair the original process custody gap.
- **Missing capability:** The moduli are not proved irreducible in Lean or identified with the canonical `GaloisField`; the source-bound replay and blind review check irreducibility executably. Original execution-source custody, a fail-closed successor C5 gate, and a full historical Sage refinement remain open. The driver's `T_equals_hM` flag still compares two constructions of the same matrix polynomial, so that original flag alone is only a consistency check; kernel equality, rank and directness are the load-bearing historical C5 checks.
- **What this evidence does not establish:** It does not certify all operations in the historical driver, the run's C8 control, the experiment success gate, a performance advantage or deployed-size ECDLP behavior.

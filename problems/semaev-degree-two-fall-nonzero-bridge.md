## Problem

The uniform claim that the descended Semaev `S₃` system has a nontrivial first fall at degree two is not proved by its Lean theorem. That theorem cancels degree-two coefficients for nonzero target abscissa `x_R`; it does not show that the residual gives a new nonzero class. A checked finite example contradicts the uniform conclusion in the stated toy Boolean model without refuting the narrower trace identity.

The historical first-fall implementation has a separate defect: `fall_profile` represents a monomial multiple as `{mm | m for mm in f}`. Boolean polynomial multiplication over F₂ requires duplicate monomials to cancel. The frozen summary is exactly reproducible as written, but its ranks and grades are not generally the ranks and grades of the Boolean F₂ polynomial system. A further finite audit found that even the parity-corrected selected degree-three row span is not closed under the multiplication rule used to define a `V_{F,3}` filtration; its exact matrix rank must not be silently identified with the full closed filtration.

For the single source-bound `n=4,n'=4,s=1,x_R=1` draw, three finite fixed-point methods agree that a precisely defined degree-bounded **Boolean quotient** closure has ranks 1, 11, and 79 in degrees 1, 2, and 3, with top-degree projection ranks 1, 10, and 56. Its new low-degree class counts are 0 at degree two and 12 at degree three. The selected direct-row span has degree-three rank 53 and direct-row new count 1. These quotient results have a [reproducer and exact evidence](../docs/reports/2026-10-26-semaev-closure-review/closure-evidence.json); they do not establish a polynomial-ring first-fall theorem, a Lean closure certificate, or an 80-draw closed distribution.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- **Archived observable:** The eight frozen cells report 70 of 80 Semaev draws at degree two and ten at degree three. The original script reproduced its JSON byte for byte on October 22. Its 200 support-matched null grades reproduced as well. The [archive receipt](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/archive-replay.json) binds the command, work directory and output hash.
- **Source defect witness:** In `n=4,n'=4,s=1`, the first equation multiplied by `u₀` has masks `1`, `17`, `129` in the archived set product; all three cancel in F₂. The archived product disagrees with direct product evaluation on 64 of 256 Boolean assignments; the corrected product agrees on all 256. The [checker](../research/verification/replay_ffd_semaev_20261022.py) asserts this and records it in [draws.json](../docs/reports/2026-10-22-semaev-first-fall-replay/evidence/draws.json).
- **Corrected exact data:** With XOR cancellation and independent binary ranks on the same 80 systems, 68 draws have their first positive selected-row new-class count at degree two and 12 at degree three. Two originally degree-two draws become degree three: `(n,n',s,x_R)=(5,3,4,8)` and `(5,3,8,8)`. Twelve of 200 null grades change. Seven corrected Semaev degree-three draws have `x_R≠0`; five have `x_R=0`. All 280 systems and both rank profiles are retained per draw.
- **Finite explanation:** At `n=4,n'=4,s=1,x_R=1`, the trace residual `u₀+u₃+w₀+w₃` is already the fourth linear generator. The selected Boolean row spans have ranks `1` and `11` in degrees one and two; the degree-two low-part dimension is `1`, so their degree-two new-fall statistic is zero. The selected degree-three rows have rank `53` and low-part dimension `12`, giving the executable statistic `12−11=1`. These are exact ranks of the specified finite row sets. Their identification with the full `V_{F,D}` filtration is a separate obligation, especially in degree three. The trace theorem's nonzero-target premise holds, yet it does not imply a new degree-two class in this finite model.
- **All-draw quotient closure audit:** The [independent checker](../docs/reports/2026-10-26-semaev-closure-review/distribution.py) re-derives all 80 Semaev systems from field `S₃`, validates every equation hash and archived direct-row profile, and compares three finite methods for the least degree-bounded Boolean quotient closure. The first positive degree remains 68 at degree two and 12 at degree three, draw by draw. This agreement does **not** identify the spaces: direct and closed ranks differ for 2, 68, and 74 draws at degrees one, two, and three; new-class counts differ for 23 draws at degree two and 77 at degree three. Seven selected-row spaces in degrees one and two contain vectors outside the specified quotient closure. The [exact 80-draw evidence](../docs/reports/2026-10-26-semaev-closure-review/distribution-evidence.json) does not prove a polynomial-ring first-fall or cost claim.
- **Matched-null quotient audit:** The [null checker](../docs/reports/2026-10-26-semaev-closure-review/null_distribution.py) verifies the equation hashes, source-linked support profiles and archived direct-row profiles of all 200 frozen matched controls. The selected first-positive degree distribution is 13/111/76 at degrees two/three/four; the defined Boolean quotient closure gives 1/117/82. Twelve controls move from degree two to three and six from three to four. [Exact per-control profiles](../docs/reports/2026-10-26-semaev-closure-review/null-distribution-evidence.json) preserve the archive without new sampling. These finite counts do not establish a significance, polynomial-ring or cryptanalytic performance claim.

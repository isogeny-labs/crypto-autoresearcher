## Problem

The requested complete end-to-end comparison for prime, Koblitz, and ordinary binary curves with subgroup order near 2^50–2^63 and decomposition arities 2–8 cannot yet be established: this checkout has several independently reviewed complete Koblitz N53 panels, with the ARM-composition receipt the current local reference, but the prime-field complete-solve arity ladder stops at 32 bits and m=7, and an ordinary-binary complete-solve baseline at the requested size is not identified. The [26 October N83 reconciliation](../docs/reports/2026-10-26-icperf-n83-reconciliation/README.md) identifies an additional *in-range* Koblitz reference in cryptanalysis with a 53-bit subgroup. Its current timing excludes factor-base construction, so it does not settle the fully charged baseline criterion. The original acceptance criterion is a verified measured improvement over each applicable index-calculus baseline with a whole-problem `S` including base construction, relation yield, useful rank, linear algebra, target work and verification; every row also retains matched rho distance.

**Size clarification:** The N53 Koblitz panel is a 53-bit *field* with a 45-bit prime target subgroup. Exact screening of the two standard binary Koblitz curves at field degrees 50–63 finds no prime subgroup of order at least `2^50`; therefore it is not a measured Koblitz baseline in that prime-subgroup-order range. Keep ambient field degree and target order as separate grid coordinates.

**2026-10-26 scope correction:** The current cryptanalysis N83 Koblitz row has field degree 83 but prime subgroup order `8,569,786,107,849,059` (`log2(r)=52.93`), so it is within the requested subgroup-order range. Its source ledger marks factor-base construction as uncharged. The N53 and N83 rows are different curves and cannot form a paired speedup series. Later goal queues supersede the historical lane-status text below; inspect live lane claims before dispatch.

- [ ] A committed, runnable frozen protocol identifies at least one exact public curve, prime subgroup and known-answer target schedule per family in the 50–63-bit range, with independently checked scalar recovery and matched rho on the identical unseen points.
- [ ] Its source and receipts support arities 2–8 or preserve a distinct, explicit `unavailable`, `censored`, or `invalid` result for each unexecuted cell; direct `S_8` and eight-summand `S_9` or chained systems have unambiguous labels. - [ ] At least one candidate/base comparison records target-blind base selection, exact realized size and digest, input and observed solving degree, sparsity, every attempt, verified useful rank, complete phase costs, peak memory, and independent replay. A quantitative improvement claim passes `tools.progress_fidelity validate` with a matched baseline and the remaining rho gap.

The first two conditions establish a trustworthy baseline matrix and honest censoring. The third would establish a scoped candidate comparison if it passes review. None alone proves scaling beyond the tested curve, size or target schedule.

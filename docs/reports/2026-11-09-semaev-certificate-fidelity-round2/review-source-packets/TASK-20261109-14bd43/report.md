## Missing interpretation bridges and completion controls

1. **Wire format to typed field and curve.** The source speaks of a producer certificate (`THM_SEMAEV_FALL1.md:107-117`); the target speaks of the run wrapper (`target:12-19`); the theorem begins with typed `F`, `K`, `W` (`Certificate.lean:16-17,71-78,147-155`). There is no byte/string parser, canonical field encoding, curve-coefficient decoder, or proof that the decoded `F`, `K`, `W` are the parameters of the claimed run. **Control:** malformed encoding, wrong modulus/extension, and altered curve coefficient must fail. **Completion:** a specified, bounded decoder with a proved or independently replayed decode-to-typed-value relation and parameter identity check.
2. **Declared subspace and basis binding.** `basis : Fin b → K` is used to define its own span (`Certificate.lean:21-32`); no serialized `V`, dimension, independence, or equality-to-declared-`V` witness is present. **Control:** a record swapping one basis vector, a duplicate vector, or a vector outside the advertised `V` must fail the declared-parameter check even when the new span happens to contain the candidate x-coordinate. **Completion:** bind a canonical basis or a validated span-equality certificate to the frozen instance and prove parsed coefficients and `F`-scalar action match it.
3. **Tuple and target reification.** `P : Fin m → W.Point` and `R : W.Point` are already typed (`Certificate.lean:71-78`); raw `xy : Fin m → K × K` still has a typed `R` (`147-155`). There is no parser binding certificate length, point order, x/y encodings, target point or infinity representation to the producer record. **Control:** truncated/extra point, reordered tuple where order is protocol significant, off-curve pair, and changed target must be rejected. **Completion:** deterministic parser with exact length and target checks, followed by a theorem connecting the parsed record to `validRawDecomposition` and the claimed instance.
4. **Checker execution and returned result.** `Decidable` and the iff theorem (`Certificate.lean:75-80,118-126,153-177`) establish a logical decision procedure for supplied typed arguments. They do not show that the production wrapper invokes Lean's `decide`, authenticates the theorem and Mathlib revision, records the actual Boolean, or refuses success when decoding/Lean execution fails. The target's claimed removal of a shared implementation failure mode (`target:12-19`, producer `:115-117`) needs this operational bridge. **Control:** bypass the Lean call or substitute a synthetic success bit and require rejection. **Completion:** a pinned verifier entrypoint taking the complete serialized record, checking all bindings, executing the decision, emitting an authenticated result with source/toolchain hashes, and an independent negative-control replay.

These are gaps in the *operational certificate* claim, not counterexamples to the typed iff. No prior report or sibling output was read. Mathlib's pinned point API was not available locally, and no heavy build was run.

\noindent\textbf{Status.} This is a scoped computational audit of 80 frozen
Semaev draws and 200 matched-null controls, with one detailed closure witness.
It does not change a Lean theorem, formal target, experiment
output, official hypothesis status, or ECDLP cost claim. The executable
checkers and exact machine-readable outputs are beside this PDF as
\texttt{audit.py}, \texttt{closure.py}, and their JSON evidence files.

\section*{Question and sources}
Does the selected degree-three row span of the fixed
$n=4,n'=4,s=1,x_R=1$ Semaev $S_3$ system satisfy the multiplication closure
needed to identify it with a $V_{F,3}$ filtration? The archived equation hash
is recorded in \texttt{evidence.json}. The repository sources are
\texttt{research/FFD\_SEMAEV\_MEASUREMENT1.md}, the corrected replay of
22 October, and the finite Lean row-space certificates. The relevant
mathematical boundary is the degree-budget closure definition in
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.05579}{Caminata--Gorla, Definition 1.5}.
\href{https://arxiv.org/pdf/1503.08001}{Kosters--Yeo, Corollary 4.11 and
Remark 4.12} give the trace identity and describe degree-two first falls as
usual, rather than universal under every factor-base choice.

\section*{Closure witness and limit}
The published degree-two witness $W$ belongs to the selected rank-53
degree-three span and lies outside the selected degree-two span. Multiplying
by $u_0$ gives a degree-three polynomial outside that rank-53 span; adjoining
it raises the rank to 54. Since $\deg W=2$ and $\deg u_0=1$, the selected
degree-three span is not closed under this Boolean-quotient degree-budget
product. This does \emph{not} invalidate the Lean theorem about the literal
80 rows. It shows that a separate bridge is needed before calling that span
the full closed $V_{F,3}$ filtration.

\section*{Fixed Boolean quotient closure}
We computed the least subspace of $\mathbb F_2[u_0,\ldots,u_7]/(u_i^2-u_i)$
containing the four source-bound equations and closed under $fg$ when
$\deg g\leq D-\deg f$, using squarefree monomial degree. Three methods agree
on each space: degree-filtered elimination with repeated variable products,
degree-filtered elimination with all allowed monomials, and a separate
projection-kernel iteration. Their joint rank equals each method's rank.
At degrees $1,2,3$, the closed ranks and top-degree projection ranks are
$(1,1),(11,10),(79,56)$. Thus the low-degree intersection at degree three
has dimension $79-56=23$, and its new quotient count is $23-11=12$. The
selected direct-row statistic instead gives $53-41-11=1$. The degree-two
quotient count remains zero. This is one fixed draw, not the 80-draw
distribution or the polynomial-ring $V_{F,D}$ with Boolean field equations.
The quotient-to-polynomial-ring bridge and a Lean closure certificate remain
open.

\section*{All 80 archived draws in the defined Boolean quotient}
The independent checker \texttt{distribution.py} re-derives all archived
component equations from field $S_3$, validates the field moduli, basis
independence, curve target, and per-draw equation hashes, and reproduces the
selected-row profiles. Three finite fixed-point methods agree on each closed
quotient space at degrees one, two, and three. The first degree with a positive
new-class count agrees draw by draw: 68 at degree two, 12 at degree three.
This agreement is not equality of spaces or multiplicities:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Number of draws & $D=1$ & $D=2$ & $D=3$\\
\midrule
Selected and quotient ranks differ & 2 & 68 & 74\\
New-class counts differ & 0 & 23 & 77\\
Selected rows outside the quotient closure & 2 & 5 & 0\\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\noindent The selected row rule admits products by their actual degree after
Boolean cancellation; the defined closure constrains multiplier degree by
$D-\deg f$. Seven selected spaces therefore contain rows absent from that
closure. The exact 80-draw profiles are in
\texttt{distribution-evidence.json}. Normal and optimized Python runs agree
byte for byte. This is finite computational evidence, not a Lean certificate
or an identification with polynomial-ring $V_{F,D}$.

\section*{The 200 archived support-matched null controls}
The checker \texttt{null\_distribution.py} validates each archived null's
equation hash and per-equation monomial-degree multiset against its recorded
Semaev source draw. It reproduces all selected-row profiles through degree
six. Three methods agree on each defined Boolean quotient closure space, and
the spaces are nested by degree. These are the existing controls; no new
random systems were generated.

\begin{center}

\midrule

\noindent Twelve nulls move from degree two to three and six from degree three
to four; 182 retain their label. Selected rows are outside the specified
quotient closure in 69 controls at degree two and 23 at degree three. The exact
per-control profiles are in \texttt{null-distribution-evidence.json}. The
80 Semaev draws retain their 68/12 first-positive labels under this quotient,
but the Semaev and null sets have different sizes. No significance, general
curve, security, or performance inference is made from this finite contrast.

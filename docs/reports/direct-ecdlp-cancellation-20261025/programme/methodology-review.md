# Preliminary adversarial review of the classical experiment programme

Scope: methodology review of the named leads in `PLAN.md`, 25 October 2026. No scientific trial, independent reproduction, performance validation, or official research-state review was performed. This same-session review does not satisfy the later independent-session review requirement. All proposed computations are classical and use public synthetic inputs.

## Main findings

1. Exhaustive coverage can mean that every named lead has a testable question, construction obligation, controls and disposition. It cannot certify that every classical algorithm or every repository branch has been explored. Preserve the existing review's explicit coverage limits.
2. Separate three outcomes: a sound measurement was collected; a particular mathematical or algorithmic prediction survived; a complete solver has a measured advantage. An ordinary or null observation can be a successful study. A missing constructor is not a measured failure.
3. The main tagged-parity object is known algebraic model counting. Its correctness is elementary. The missing result is a uniformly constructible EC-specific rewrite, closed under composition, with charged cost. No experiment should quietly replace that missing construction with a coefficient, inverse, low-rank factorization or successful scalar oracle.
4. Implementation self-tests can begin now. Formal scientific dispatch requires a committed runnable packet and the repository's own admission checks. A document that specifies an absent launcher is a design, not a ready experiment.

## Exactness hazards in the direct approach

Write the claim in this order: there is one public algorithm A such that for every admitted (E,P,Q), A constructs and evaluates the response, and its total cost satisfies the claimed bound. The construction may specialize on Q, but may not use k, learned test labels, an uncharged inverse table, or advice chosen after solving Q. The weaker statement that every target has a small post-solution representation does not provide an algorithm.

Use canonical full point encodings, with an explicit infinity code, range validation and subgroup policy. Enforce b<r, rather than accepting all bit strings or reducing them modulo r: repeated representatives can cancel in characteristic two. Every internal witness must have exactly one satisfying extension for each scalar input. Test a deliberately doubled auxiliary witness as a negative control: the unique scalar's response becomes zero under even multiplicity. A correct final scalar on a few targets does not establish unique-extension semantics.

Addition and multiplication in R preserve exact coefficients; ordinary integer counts, floating-point contractions and numerical ranks are not interchangeable with this calculation. Record constant and all bit coefficients, even when the constant is zero. The ring is not a field, so a rank claim must name and justify its ring-theoretic meaning. Do not infer a cheap construction from rank one at the final target slice.

For compiled counting circuits, use a sound compilation theorem with its hypotheses. In the usual route, AND needs decomposability, OR needs determinism, and omitted variables need smoothing or their explicit weight sums. Here a positive and negative scalar-literal weight sum is epsilon_j, not 1, so ignoring an absent variable is particularly unsafe. Plain Boolean DAG substitution is not model counting.

Test a proposed block identity on every boundary assignment in its declared finite domain, comparing the entire coefficient vector, not merely the final decoded scalar. If the identity applies only to valid coordinate boundaries, prove the validity invariant and its closure under the intended composition. Include exceptional additions, inverses, infinity, doubling at y=0, field/carry ranges, unused scalar bits and canonical output constraints. One counterexample invalidates that exact identity; preserve it as a minimal reproducible certificate. A finite exhaustive check proves only the stated finite case, unless paired with a parameter-uniform argument.

The tagged-ring reference, an injective affine Boolean map, ordinary XOR elimination, fixed-window multiplication and a tiny explicit permutation are correctness/control cases. The permutation table's construction and storage must be charged; it is not a fair asymptotic baseline. An invertible basis change preserves the instance and is not a structure-destroying null. A replaced arithmetic relation needs its own determinism and unique-extension checks.

## Per-lead discriminating obligations

| Lead | Test or construction that can discriminate | Outcome that must not be overclaimed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Prime-to-p jets / canonical lift | First identify whether the output is an additive map to a target on which multiplication by r is invertible. Such a map is zero. A genuinely non-homomorphic, public, computable section needs fresh resolution-matched prediction tests, including signs and fibre-matched nulls; charge construction of every lift. | Reproducing scalar linearity on a hard lift constructed using k is not recovery. Failed non-homomorphic feature panels do not prove all sections useless. |
| EDS / elliptic nets | Specify a nonlinear target-dependent locator and its public evaluator. Compare a target-shifted sequence with relabelled/translated controls; separate known recurrence identities from scalar-identifying information. Existing short-annihilator classes serve as scoped regression tests. | A short syntax, low displacement rank, period or identity true on every k-fibre does not orient k. A period supplied by enumerating the orbit costs the enumeration. |
| Endomorphism scalar action | Record the induced scalar action, available eigenvalues, construction cost and any purported new coordinate-valued output. Generic endomorphism-image relations are controls; an escape must name information beyond those relations. | A known-scalar speedup, short k-independent lattice vector, or extra congruence that is algebraically redundant does not establish a changed ECDLP exponent. |
| IDEA-050 matchgate/Pfaffian transform | Supply explicit public basis transforms and exact local identities over the declared coefficient system, then construct the global object and evaluate with all costs. Verify tags and exceptions, and compare against the generic compiler. | Testing an already-solved target slice, fitting a transform separately using each answer, or finding numerical low rank does not construct a solver. |
| IDEA-111 radical ladder quotient | Distinguish a full lossless branch representation from a target-local functional. Define the exact ideal/saturation and output functional; verify branch multiplicities and exceptional cases before measuring dimensions or cost. | A small radical, compact normal form, or shared prefix is not a cheap constructor or decoder. Changing to a one-target question does not automatically evade the construction problem. |
| IDEA-070 reverse automaton | Require a uniform state/transition constructor and a public target-to-state procedure. Measure ambiguity and construct collision witnesses between distinct scalars with the same observable; demonstrate orientation and decoding. | Counting few visited states after enumerating all scalars or learning states from labelled test targets does not constitute a cheap reverse automaton. |
| IDEA-156 coefficient locator | Implement the coefficient evaluator, not just a coefficient formula. Compare against exact enumeration and established coefficient/counting tools; charge degree, monomial growth and every adaptive query. | Unavailable coefficient access, degree bounds, or a compact coefficient name cannot be counted as unit-time operations. |
| IDEA-379 Ax–Katz locator | Specify the field, polynomial system, degree hypotheses, integer solution count and desired decision. Independently count tiny cases and verify that the divisibility theorem actually applies to that encoding, including auxiliaries. | Divisibility cannot distinguish zero from a positive divisible count. If an encoding has exactly one solution, a theorem forcing p-divisibility cannot simultaneously meet its hypotheses. Tags do not automatically inherit an unweighted counting theorem. |
| Lossy propagation | For each fixed projection, measure fibre sizes, exact transition ambiguity, conditional uncertainty and held-out prediction error under a frozen train/test split. Use a curve-level random-labelling null and matched coordinate/sign controls. Any list decoder must charge candidate-list growth and verification. | Deterministic whole-orbit propagation is rigid; probabilistic tests are a different scope. Failure of a finite projection panel cannot close every probabilistic projection or every belief-propagation method. Above-null prediction is not by itself complete scalar recovery. |

The atlas's lossy-propagation wording that a finite null panel would close the entire probabilistic regime is too broad. Restrict any negative statement to the frozen projections, learners, resolutions, sample populations and sizes actually tested.

An exact rigidity check needs no statistical inference. Let G=<P> have prime order r. If f(x+P)=T(f(x)) for every x in G, the sequence f(0), f(P), ... is an orbit of T whose period divides r. Since it closes after r steps, its image has size 1 or r. Equivalently, translation-invariant fibres are cosets of a subgroup. This rules out nonconstant, genuinely lossy, deterministic exact propagation under the full translation action. It does not rule out noisy propagation, a partial domain, additional state, approximate predictions or a different action. If extra state restores determinism, measure that full state rather than only the displayed projection.

## Samples, blinding and performance accounting

Retain the proposed discovery panel of four registered curves at each of 8, 10 and 12 subgroup bits, with exhaustive canonical scalars. These observations are in-sample discovery evidence. Select fixture curves by a recorded deterministic procedure; record all candidates/rejections so that easy instances cannot be chosen after observing performance. Certify the subgroup order, field primality and non-anomalous condition. Handle special-j, special-prime and cofactor families as separate strata rather than silently excluding slow strata.

Retain the proposed held-out panel of six fresh curves at each of 12, 14, 16 and 18 bits, eight random targets per curve (192 target cases). Forced edge cases k=0,1,r-1 and exceptional arithmetic cases belong to a separate correctness panel, not the random performance sample. Freeze the algorithm, grammar, seeds, input hashes, metrics, exclusions and analysis before revealing target labels. Discovery and held-out curve overlap at 12 bits is acceptable only if the actual curves, targets and labels are fresh. A fixture generator may know labels, but the candidate process should receive only public inputs; store labels for an independent verifier, not in candidate-accessible input files.

Targets on a common curve are clustered. Report per-target values and per-curve aggregates; use curves as the independent replication unit for uncertainty and resampling. Do not count 192 observations as 192 independent curve families. Repeated timing runs measure implementation variability, not additional mathematical instances. With only six curves per size, uncertainty may be large; present it rather than claiming a universal law. Zero observed errors supplies finite empirical evidence, not correctness on all curves or targets.

For a full solver comparison, run a verified rho implementation on the identical public targets and hardware with negation and applicable efficiently computable automorphisms. Include restarts, fruitless cycles, distinguished-point infrastructure where used, output verification and its full scalar-tracking cost. Name the memory regime; include BSGS when its time/memory point is relevant. Repeat stochastic rho runs with preregistered seeds (for example 16 runs per target) and retain all completions and censoring; avoid selecting the fastest run. A/A repetitions can quantify measurement noise. Baseline parameters may be tuned on discovery cases and frozen for held-out cases.

Charge candidate parsing, arithmetic encoding, normalization, rewrite/schedule search, compilation, Q-specific setup, factorization, contraction, decoding, checking, failed branches and storage. Report cold single-target cost first. Amortized results need an explicit reuse count and a comparator allowed corresponding preprocessing. Per-stage gains stay per-stage gains. Bit-operation counters and group-operation counters cannot be divided into a speedup without a justified conversion or common wall-time calibration. Record peak live memory and retained preprocessing separately.

Do not estimate a sub-square-root asymptotic exponent from the 12–18-bit ladder. A fitted log-cost slope is descriptive for those implementations and sizes; the ceiling, overheads and size-dependent algorithm choices can dominate. A mathematical exponent claim needs a uniform cost derivation and explicit assumptions; larger measurements can challenge that derivation but cannot replace it.

## Outcomes and continuation rules

Use separate fields rather than one ambiguous pass/fail flag:

- `execution`: complete, censored, infrastructure_error, unavailable_constructor.
- `integrity`: valid, invalid_encoding, invalid_output, invalid_counter, leakage_detected.
- `observation`: actual metrics and all cases, including null/control behavior.
- `prediction`: matched, contradicted, or unresolved, with the exact frozen claim and domain.
- `claim_scope`: correctness fixture, local identity, composition result, stage cost, full solver cost, or no algorithmic claim.

For an exact rewrite, any verified counterexample rejects that frozen identity. Repairing it creates a new revision and a fresh test obligation, while retaining the old failure. A hidden-scalar dependency or uncharged inverse lookup invalidates the proposed public procedure. A performance prediction contradicted by a valid held-out measurement is a failed prediction at that scope, not a theorem against all nearby methods. Multiple exploratory rules tested on the same data need explicit multiplicity treatment before significance language; newly selected rules require fresh confirmation data.

If no rule in a frozen finite grammar outperforms the generic simplifier, report the full grammar, blocks, exact counts and costs. Continue by changing a mathematical construction or by collecting a justified new observable, not by relabelling compiler retuning as novelty. A descriptive study may still complete successfully with this ordinary behavior.

Set worker and memory limits from actual host headroom. A watchdog is a process-protection threshold, recorded with elapsed time and current progress; it is not a mathematical failure or an arbitrary research-budget cap. Retain interrupted outputs and right-censor times. A pilot may set a documented justified watchdog before freezing the comparison; do not retune it after seeing which arm it favours. Continue justified finite work under the standing authorization rather than requesting repeated budget permission.

## Minimum packet before a scientific dispatch

For each genuinely executable protocol: exact source and input hashes; a launcher with declared outputs and unique fresh run paths; certified public synthetic fixtures; complete metrics, controls, frozen samples/seeds and analysis; handoff/ownership and lawful goal lane; concurrency/memory/watchdog settings; all trial and failure archive coverage; output-correctness checks; public-input/hidden-label separation; and an additive record of known limitations. Use the repository's allocator and templates for official IDs rather than inventing status-bearing IDs.

The repository requires `tools/experiment_preapproval.py --packet <PACKET.json>` to report `approvable: true` for the exact prospective specification/trial-plan/handoff/queue before a Coordinator records approval. After the approval and archive are committed, `tools/newest_experiments.py --experiment <EXP-ID> --json` must report `execution_state: ready`; dispatch through the existing `run` route with its built-in admission checks. Do not manufacture approvals or bypass these checks when a full checkout, launcher, claim or required input is unavailable. Keep missing-constructor protocols `review_required` with a concrete unblock condition. This requirement is distinct from routine development self-tests, which supply implementation validation and must not be presented as scientific trials.

Publish observations with immutable run records, all failures, and a source-linked report/graph/PDF. Performance claims additionally require machine-readable progress-fidelity validation, matched baseline evidence and the stipulated independent verification. No amount of within-session review certifies novelty; compare the exact surviving construction with prior records and primary literature, and retain a precise remaining search scope.

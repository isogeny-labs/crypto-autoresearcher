and a two-sided zero `0`. Define `n·a` by `0·a=0` and `(n+1)·a=(n·a)+a`.

and `T≠0`. Derive or refute `r·(P+T)=T` and `r·(P+T)≠0`.

Construct a nonvacuous model and attack oddness and `T≠0` separately.

## Public constructor and admissible information

Let `G=<P>` have odd prime order r, and let Q=[k]P with canonical 0≤k<r. Put ell=ceil(log2 r). For a fixed public point feature phi, the proposed fitted kernels are

`K_b(a,c) = Pr(phi([2]X+[b]P)=c | phi(X)=a)`, b∈{0,1},

where X is uniform over the entire subgroup. The constructor can sample X, evaluate both public point operations and record feature labels without knowing any discrete logarithm. A fixed 2,048-sample training set, a fixed feature alphabet and pseudocount-one smoothing define an actual finite procedure. Empty/unseen state rows must have a predefined distribution; the feature alphabet must not be discovered using target truth labels.

The candidate receives public curve/subgroup parameters and public target coordinates. It must not consume `orbit[k]=[k]P`, a target's k, a scalar dictionary, a target-conditioned exact completion oracle, an enumerated inverse table or a labelled intermediate ladder trace. Coordinate sampling, Tonelli–Shanks, cofactor projection and public point operations are charged construction work. A full census in a preparation certificate is not a free candidate primitive.

The separate exact entropy instrument may enumerate the subgroup and use scalar labels to enumerate pairs. That is explicitly charged measurement/reference work and must remain outside the decoder call path. The light candidate parser can verify field/subgroup primality, nonsingularity, P and target membership while consuming the previously certified public order N and cofactor h. It must not call the census helpers merely as an unnoticed input-validation step and then claim sub-enumerative setup.

The proposed source-enforced target separation is useful, but it is not OS-enforced blindness. Keep the custodian's label file out of candidate argv, model-fitting inputs and feature/tie seeds. Publish the public Q manifest and its hash before candidate execution. Every candidate k can be checked independently by [k]P=Q; a secret label is unnecessary to verify a positive output. Exact source inspection and the recorded interface, rather than a claimed sandbox that does not exist, support the separation.

Because Q is already public, a deterministic phi(Q) adds no Shannon information to Q. The question is whether a low-cost representation and operation set makes some of that existing information computationally usable. A projection entropy gap is not by itself scalar leakage, a scalar decoder, or a computational advantage.

## Uniform subgroup sampling: required argument

For a nonsingular short Weierstrass curve over odd Fp, choose x uniformly. If the right-hand side is a nonzero square, accept and select either square root with probability one half. If the right-hand side is zero, accept its single root with probability one half. Reject nonsquares. Each accepted finite point then has the same unnormalised probability 1/(2p), so the conditional output is uniform on E(Fp) minus O. Omitting the half-acceptance for y=0 would overweight rational two-torsion.

If N=#E(Fp) is certified, mix this finite sampler with O at probability 1/N. The result is uniform on all E(Fp). With N=hr, r prime, gcd(h,r)=1 and P of order r, multiplication by h maps E(Fp) onto its unique order-r subgroup with equal-size fibres. Its image is therefore uniform on G, including O. Reject unsupported cofactors or certificate failures; do not assume this conclusion after discarding identity outputs.

This argument assumes the declared uniform bounded draws and root selection. The seeded PRNG provides reproducibility rather than a proof of ideal randomness. The code must preserve its actual seed domains, draw/rejection counts, field exponentiations, cofactor multiplication and membership checks. The fixture certificate establishes the finite inputs; it is not an oracle providing a target logarithm.

## What the random offsets repair

For each target/view choose R uniformly in G, independently of k. Start X0=R and process a scalar word with `X_(j+1)=[2]X_j+[b_j]P`. After ell bits,

`X_ell = [2^ell]R + [k]P = [2^ell]R + Q`.

Both the start point and transformed target are publicly computable. No log of R is needed. For any fixed prefix u of length j, `X_j=[2^j]R+[u]P` is uniform on G because [2^j] is a permutation of the odd-order subgroup. Thus each individual prefix-state marginal has the same population used to construct K_b.

That is the exact scope of the repair. Conditioning on earlier observed labels or on the actual starting state generally makes the distribution within a later feature fibre nonuniform. Marginal uniformity does **not** establish the hidden-state Markov property or justify multiplying these marginal kernels as an exact likelihood. For more than one step, the coarse state can omit relevant history. Four independently sampled offsets do not restore that lost history automatically.

## Exact deterministic ladder quotients remain obstructed

The source rigidity theorem states that if a projection of a prime-order group propagates exactly under the full group law, it is injective or constant. A shorter proof applies directly to this binary ladder interface.

Assume deterministic maps A0 and A1 exist on the full image S=phi(G), satisfying `phi(2X)=A0(phi(X))` and `phi(2X+P)=A1(phi(X))` for every X∈G. Because doubling permutes G, A0 is surjective on finite S and hence bijective. For Y=2X,

`phi(Y+P) = A1(A0^-1(phi(Y)))`.

There is therefore an exact deterministic translation map T on S. The sequence phi(O),phi(P),...,phi((r−1)P) is a closed T-orbit whose period divides prime r. Its image size is 1 or r. Consequently phi is constant or injective.

This is a derived, self-contained statement about exact whole-subgroup propagation. It does not rule out the fitted stochastic kernels, approximate propagation, a restricted domain, added history/state, a growing nonlossy state, non-group actions or other coordinate algorithms. Empirically point-mass training rows do not prove the deterministic hypothesis; finite samples can miss alternative outputs. A finite negative study likewise cannot close all probabilistic propagation.

## Pair entropy, unary calibration and multi-step inference are distinct

The earlier panel measures `H(phi(X+Y) | phi(X),phi(Y))` for independent uniform X,Y. The proposed decoder instead uses conditional laws of `[2]X` and `[2]X+P`, with P fixed. A difference in the pair statistic does not imply a difference in either unary channel, and accurate one-step channels do not imply a valid multi-step coarse Markov model.

The frozen follow-up should therefore retain three separate measurements:

1. Exact pair entropy and sign/fibre-matched references on fresh fixtures, including Kummer/full-point/constant controls.
2. Exact unary conditional entropies `D_b=H(phi(2X+bP)|phi(X))`, or equivalently their complete finite conditional tables for the scale instrument; sampled kernels additionally receive 512 fresh, unlabelled held-out X values for b=0,1, with log loss, accuracy and state counts. These samples do not tune the frozen kernel.
3. Actual endpoint-to-scalar candidate recovery with the declared beam widths and public verification. Keep successes, misses, false proposed candidates, candidate-list length, ties and all resources. Do not infer recovery from either entropy or calibration alone.

Kummer is an important check: x(2X) is determined by x(X), while adding the fixed P generally requires sign/orientation information. A single scalar statistic combining both actions can hide that asymmetry. The constant projection also has zero propagation entropy but no scalar orientation.

## The multi-view prefix score is an explicit heuristic

For prefix u with legal suffix set V_u, let n_u=|V_u|. If L_i(uv) is the per-view endpoint likelihood under the fitted Markov model, exact joint-prefix mass within that model is proportional to

`sum_(v in V_u) product_i L_i(uv)`.

Forward/backward computation per view gives `s_i(u)=sum_(v in V_u) L_i(uv)`. The proposed score is

`S(u)=n_u product_i (s_i(u)/n_u)`

or `log S=sum_i log s_i − (m−1)log n_u`. Its count correction avoids multiplying the uniform scalar prior m times. It does not replace the common suffix by a single coupled marginalisation: product-of-sums generally differs from sum-of-products. The two coincide for m=1 and at a leaf n_u=1, but not in general for an internal prefix. The frozen procedure therefore makes both a coarse-Markov approximation and a multi-view prefix-combination approximation. It must not be described as an exact posterior.

Range checks matter. Bits are MSB-first; the backward dynamic program must count only the canonical scalar interval, using a correct less/equal state for the bound r−1. At every prefix, `n_u` is the number of canonical completions rather than `2^(remaining bits)` unless the whole prefix interval is valid. A padded extra representative can otherwise introduce duplicate points, bias the prior or admit k+r.

The constant-state control has L_i=1 on every legal scalar, so S(u)=n_u. Its final candidate set must be chosen independently of k; with B distinct final candidates and uniform k, recovery chance is B/r. A tie seed derived from the hidden scalar, or a target-generation seed reconstructing it, invalidates that control. Fixed target-index/fixture domains independent of k are appropriate. The returned exact list size matters if it is less than B.

## A cheap model-ceiling diagnostic

For a row-stochastic fitted matrix K, its Dobrushin coefficient is

`delta(K)=1/2 max_(a,a') sum_c |K(a,c)−K(a',c)|`.

For probability rows p,q, total variation satisfies `TV(pK,qK)≤delta(K) TV(p,q)`: decompose p−q into equally weighted positive and negative probability parts and bound every pair of transition rows by the displayed maximum. Repeating this inequality gives a product of contraction factors.

Thus two different prefix-state distributions followed by the same t suffix bits have endpoint-label probabilities differing by at most `delta_max^t`, where delta_max=max(delta(K0),delta(K1)). For m views, the difference between products of their endpoint probabilities is at most `m delta_max^t`, by telescoping products of factors in [0,1]. This is an absolute probability bound inside the fitted model. It is not a relative-posterior bound, a bound after rare-event normalisation, an ECDLP lower bound, or a theorem about a richer model with additional state.

Emitting the tiny matrices' contraction coefficients and these suffix-length bounds before interpreting a failed beam can distinguish a model that washes out early-prefix differences from a failed search implementation. These require only arithmetic on already constructed matrices, not new samples. They do not justify closing the broader lead.

## Controls, costs and result boundaries

Freeze four candidate features: `x_mod_7`, `power_residue_5` where available, a public sign-invariant `x_hash_7` complexity control, and `constant`. The public x-hash is not an exact fibre-profile matched random projection merely because it has seven bins. Its realised fibres must be described; the exact entropy instrument's sign/fibre-preserving relabeling ensemble supplies that stronger comparison separately. Do not feed its orbit-indexed null table into the purported cheap public decoder.

Keep unrestricted and p≡1 mod5 ordinary-curve strata separate. The latter ensures replication for a fifth-power residue that had only one applicable pilot row. It samples a conditional family rather than an unbiased cross-section of all curves. Publish the generator, exclusions and rejected candidates. Eight targets share each of 24 fixtures and the same fitted kernels; curves are the replication units, and widths/features/views produce correlated outcomes. The fresh 192-target set is not the original 12–18-bit blinded solver cohort.

Self-tests should include a small scalar-labelled cyclic full-state positive control, explicitly labelled as a simulator and not an admissible ECDLP feature; exact one-view forward/backward versus exhaustive branch enumeration; a non-power-of-two scalar range; deterministic constant-state candidates; an intentionally corrupted kernel or prefix count; unseen feature rows; invalid/off-subgroup points; and public verification rejecting a wrong scalar. A known intermediate scalar trace is a privileged-data control, never an endpoint-only input.

Report the 2,048 training draws, 512 held-out draws, square roots/rejections, group operations, 4 offset constructions, transition counts, row/state support, smoothing, dynamic-program operations, beam expansions, peak retained vectors, all candidate verifications and any persistent preprocessing. Cold first-target cost includes construction. Amortised results name the reuse count and retain setup. Training already uses far more than sqrt(r) point samples on these toy groups; a recovery success alone is not a cost advantage. Any eventual rho comparison needs its own matched, strong reference. Do not divide floating-point operations by group additions to invent a speed ratio.

Success of this descriptive study is complete valid measurement and preserved controls. One wrong returned scalar falsifies the output claim; one counterexample refutes an asserted exact quotient; neither a failed beam nor a timeout rules out every approximate model. An above-reference recovery observation warrants separate confirmation and cost analysis. No finite entropy panel, fitted state model or locally admissible basis certifies a new ECDLP algorithm.

## Matchgate audit: scope and coefficient domain

The pilot exhausts one small common-basis grammar, not every basis. A useful extension isolates the local pin hypothesis. For a common invertible M=[[a,b],[c,d]] over a field, requiring both unabsorbed unary CONST0 and CONST1 signatures to have matchgate parity support gives ac=bd=0. Nonzero determinant then forces M to be diagonal or antidiagonal. Its inverse transpose is also monomial. These transforms only rescale and permute tensor coordinates. The AND signature has both even- and odd-parity support, which remains mixed after any such transform. Thus this **single-common-basis, unabsorbed-both-pins signature bundle** cannot be made a matchgate bundle in that way.

Per-wire gauges, absorbing pins into larger blocks, changing the boundary representation and using a different global algorithm remove or change hypotheses of this argument. They are not automatically cheap constructions, but the argument does not close them. An AND-only rational positive control can test that omitting the pin hypothesis actually changes the problem.

The classical tagged-response coefficient ring has characteristic two and zero divisors. A rational matrix with an even determinant or a denominator of two does not automatically define an invertible transformation there. An invertible matrix over the square-zero tag ring reduces to an invertible matrix over F2; an asserted parity identity must survive that reduction. The separate six-matrix GL2(F2) audit can check this finite necessary condition. Its outcome is not presumed in this methodology note. A uniform local-gauge obstruction is not a lower bound on arbitrary target-conditioned classical elimination.
